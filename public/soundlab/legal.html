Privacy

1. Local generation

The current SOUNDLAB local engine generates audio inside your browser. Prompt text and locally generated audio are not uploaded by that engine merely to create the track.

2. Payments

When online checkout is enabled, iKhokha processes payment details through its secure payment service. SOUNDLAB should receive payment status and transaction references needed to grant access; it should not receive or store your full card number or card security code.

3. Service data

We may process information reasonably required to operate the service, prevent abuse, maintain project history, provide customer support, fulfil paid entitlements and comply with law. We aim to collect only what is needed for those purposes.

4. Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the platform, process payments, host data or comply with a lawful obligation.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, dispute handling and security. Applicable privacy rights may be exercised through the merchant contact channel supplied with your transaction or customer account.

Refunds & Cancellations

Failed or duplicate payments

If you are charged more than once for the same order, or a confirmed payment does not deliver the purchased entitlement because of a technical failure, we will investigate and provide the appropriate remedy, including a refund where due.

Generated digital content

Because generated audio and downloadable digital files can be consumed immediately, refunds are not automatically due merely because a successfully generated track is no longer wanted. Any mandatory consumer-law rights remain unaffected.

Subscriptions

A subscription may be cancelled before the next renewal once recurring billing is introduced. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically reverse a period that has already been supplied, except where required by law or where the service was not delivered.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds should normally be returned through the original payment method or another lawful method agreed with the customer. Processing time can depend on the payment provider and banking network.
