# IZAKHONO AI — Private Development Workspace

**Status:** Approved visual identity reference  
**Canonical product name:** IZAKHONO AI — Private Development Workspace  
**Portfolio classification:** IZAKHONO AI / Core Platform

## Brand promise

IZAKHONO AI is the private development workspace for building, creating, managing, deploying and growing software, products, projects and businesses across the IZAKHONO ecosystem.

**Primary capability sequence:** BUILD • CREATE • MANAGE • DEPLOY • GROW  
**Brand line:** INNOVATE • DEVELOP • ACHIEVE

## Visual identity

The approved reference establishes a premium, cinematic technology identity:

- **Obsidian black:** primary canvas and background; conveys focus, privacy and depth.
- **Metallic gold:** primary brand accent, emblem, key actions and premium highlights.
- **Brushed silver / platinum:** supporting typography, structural surfaces and secondary marks.
- **Electric blue:** controlled secondary accent for intelligence, connectivity, active states and motion.
- **Warm white:** high-legibility text and contrast where required.

The visual balance should remain predominantly dark, with gold as the signature accent and electric blue used selectively. Avoid turning every component into a glowing effect.

## Brand mark

The approved reference features a sculptural gold IZAKHONO monogram, a connected global globe, and orbital motion. Treat the supplied artwork as the master visual reference. Do not redraw, distort, simplify or substitute the approved mark without owner approval.

For small screens, favicons and compact navigation, use an approved simplified mark only after it has been derived and reviewed against the master artwork. Do not use the full poster composition as a tiny icon.

## Interface application

The brand must be applied to the existing IZAKHONO AI / SUPER APP experience and shared Visual Engine, not used as a reason to create a duplicate platform.

- Preserve a familiar, chat-first interaction model with clear navigation and low cognitive load.
- Keep the primary workspace readable, fast and usable before decorative effects load.
- Apply gold to primary actions and selected navigation; use blue for live/connected/AI activity cues.
- Use silver or warm white for readable body text; maintain accessible contrast.
- Use dark glass surfaces, subtle depth and restrained orbital/light motifs for hero areas and selected panels.
- Provide semantic static fallbacks for WebGL, shader, motion and liquid-glass effects.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences, keyboard navigation, mobile performance and accessibility.
- The experience must remain consistent on Android/mobile and desktop.

## UX principles

**Simple on the surface. Extremely powerful underneath.**

The user should not need to choose models, agents, servers or orchestration internals. Present clear work modes and transparent progress, approvals, costs/capacity constraints and evidence. Keep independent products and their names, ownership boundaries and source-of-truth systems intact when they are surfaced through the workspace.

## Implementation boundaries

1. This is a visual identity and presentation standard; it does not assert that the brand has already been deployed to every application.
2. The existing IZAKHONO Visual Engine remains the reusable presentation layer. Extend its token/profile system rather than introducing a competing design engine.
3. Preserve product-specific brand identities. IZAKHONO AI is the workspace identity, not a mandate to rebrand every IZAKHONO product.
4. Do not claim a release is live based only on source changes or a READY preview deployment. Require production deployment and device-level acceptance evidence.
5. No paid service, subscription, domain purchase or irreversible change is authorised by this branding approval.
