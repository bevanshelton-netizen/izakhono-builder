Questions, answered

Clear information before you commit.

Is FAISReady the FSCA?

No. FAISReady is an independent exam-preparation platform and does not administer the official examinations.

Does FAISReady guarantee a pass?

No. Results depend on your preparation and the official examination. FAISReady is built to help you prepare more effectively.

Can I study on my phone?

Yes. The experience is designed mobile-first.

What about RE2?

RE2 is not offered as a current active FAISReady course. The current platform focuses on RE1, RE3, RE4 and RE5.
