## 7. Standard submission controls

Before any supplier registration or bid:

1. Confirm buyer and procurement portal are authentic.
2. Confirm exact legal entity and registration number.
3. Use only current evidence.
4. Match categories to demonstrated capability.
5. Do not claim capacity that has not been verified.
6. Do not claim customer relationships, contracts, certifications or approvals without evidence.
7. Record registration date, portal, account owner and status.
8. Save confirmation/reference number.
9. Record any outstanding compliance documents.
10. Set follow-up date.

## 11. Procurement operating rule

**DISCOVER → QUALIFY → REGISTER → BID → WIN → SUPPLY → REPEAT**

Every registration must create a reusable account record. Every RFQ must create a reusable pricing/product reference. Every completed order must create evidence for future bids.

## 12. Evidence language

Use:
- "current evidence confirms"
- "available for controlled pilot"
- "subject to buyer qualification"
- "technology capability / prototype / pilot"
- "registration submitted" only after actual submission
- "supplier approved" only after documented approval
- "contract awarded" only after an executed award/PO

Do not use:
- "approved supplier" without approval evidence
- "preferred supplier" without evidence
- "production-ready" without production validation
- "certified" without a valid certificate
- "guaranteed capacity" without verified capacity
- "government supplier" based solely on an old CSD record
