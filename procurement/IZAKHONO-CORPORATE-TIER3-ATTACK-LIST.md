## Verified current routes

- MSD confirms supplier diversity registration and lists site services, travel/transportation, logistics, fleet, IT and professional services among procurement areas.
- ArcelorMittal South Africa states that new vendor onboarding is driven by validated business need and qualifying supplier requirements; its ESD route is available for small and upcoming enterprises.
- TFG lists registered-entity, banking, tax, B-BBEE and social/ethical compliance prerequisites, including active SEDEX membership for relevant merchandise manufacturing suppliers.
- Rio Tinto Buy Local requires business registration, relevant experience, HSE/community management systems and financial capacity; registration does not guarantee an award.
- City of Tshwane has moved supplier registration to SAP Ariba and requires CSD registration for municipal procurement.
- DBSA's eProcurement portal requires a valid CSD MAAA number before suppliers can participate.

## Execution rule

1. Register only when current evidence supports the application.
2. Never claim supplier approval before receiving confirmation.
3. Capture portal/reference number and category selections.
4. Record buyer/procurement contact and response.
5. Convert every qualified opening into RFQ/RFP/EOI pursuit.
6. Government and municipal opportunities remain conditional on CSD recovery.
7. Technology claims must retain their maturity labels: architecture, prototype, pilot or production evidence.
