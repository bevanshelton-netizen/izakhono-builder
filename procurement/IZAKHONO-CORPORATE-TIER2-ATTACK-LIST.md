1. Build the evidence bundle required by the strictest target: current CIPC, SARS/TCS, B-BBEE, bank confirmation, shareholder/UBO, insurance/COID, manufacturing evidence, references, sector certifications.
2. Register/verify P1 private corporate accounts where portals are available.
3. For portals that are temporarily closed or need an invitation, send the official procurement/ESD enquiry rather than inventing a registration.
4. Position Izakhono separately by buying need:
   - PPE/uniforms/apparel
   - contract manufacturing
   - furniture/upholstery
   - branding/embroidery
   - technology/automation/security
5. Record every registration reference, category, buyer contact, response and next action.
6. Convert qualified registrations into RFQ/RFP conversations.

## Evidence discipline
- Registration is not an award.
- Supplier database listing is not a contract.
- Do not claim buyer interest without written evidence.
- Do not claim a certification, audit, accreditation or compliance status unless the document is current and verified.
- Government opportunities remain CSD-gated where the tender requires CSD.
