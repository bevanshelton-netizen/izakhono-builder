## Account-specific execution controls

### Sun International
Official supplier registration is administered by B1SA. Sun states that an accurate supplier profile improves sourcing prospects and that supplier categories include uniforms/clothing, PPE, furniture, IT, logistics and security-related goods/services. Registration does not guarantee business.

**Execution:** complete profile only from verified company evidence; if the portal flags an existing entity, use B1SA support rather than creating a duplicate.

### Absa
Absa's Procurement Marketplace allows SMMEs to register profiles and value propositions. Its due-diligence process includes organisational structure, shareholder equity/structure, BBBEE equity structure and UBO checks.

**Execution:** prepare a bank-specific value proposition and complete compliance evidence before submission.

### Anglo American / De Beers
Anglo American states that prospective suppliers can self-register and that supplier interactions are moving through Ariba SLP. Registration does not guarantee work; specific sourcing events determine opportunities.

**Execution:** register once evidence is complete and monitor sourcing events.

### Sasol
Sasol states that new prospective-supplier self-registration through SAP Ariba is temporarily closed. Interim requests can be sent to the Sasol Contact Centre or shared-services email; future SMME entry is expected through the Linkage platform.

**Execution:** submit a concise prospective-supplier request focused on high-fit categories and retain proof of contact.

### Rio Tinto
Rio Tinto's Buy Local pathway expects business registration, relevant experience, HSE/community management systems and financial capacity.

**Execution:** build an evidence-led supplier profile; do not imply vendor status until formally awarded.

## Opportunity conversion workflow

1. Register/profile.
2. Confirm category alignment.
3. Monitor sourcing/RFQ/RFP invitations.
4. Qualify each opportunity against mandatory requirements.
5. Price using controlled costing.
6. Submit bid with evidence checklist.
7. Track clarification/addenda deadlines.
8. Record outcome.
9. Convert successful award into delivery controls.
10. Pursue repeat/framework/offtake opportunities.

## Bid/no-bid gate

**BID** only when:
- Scope matches actual capability.
- Mandatory documents are available.
- Closing date and submission method are verified.
- Pricing can be delivered profitably.
- Required certifications/standards can be evidenced.
- Capacity and lead time are credible.

**NO-BID / HOLD** when:
- Mandatory CSD or other compliance is missing for a government opportunity.
- A required accreditation cannot be evidenced.
- The scope exceeds actual capacity.
- Submission deadline cannot be met responsibly.
- Customer-specific development is prohibited under the relevant funding/procurement rules.
