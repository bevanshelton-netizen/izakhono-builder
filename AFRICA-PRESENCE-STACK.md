# IZAKHONO AFRICA PRESENCE STACK

## Mission

Build an African owner-controlled digital industrial group that can operate across finance, insurance, media, music, engineering, education, commerce and AI while keeping each regulated or commercial entity legally and operationally separate.

The common rule is:

**Separate businesses. Shared IZAKHONO infrastructure. No accidental commingling.**

## Group platform layer

Every operating business should consume shared infrastructure through defined internal contracts:

- IZAKHONO ID — identity, entity membership, MFA/device trust
- IZAKHONO ACCESS — subscriptions and entitlements
- IZAKHONO PAY — payment orchestration and reconciliation
- IZAKHONO LEDGER — entity-scoped double-entry financial ledger
- IZAKHONO DATA — databases, object storage and backups
- IZAKHONO EDGE — domains, TLS, routing and WAF
- IZAKHONO VAULT — secrets and key management
- IZAKHONO OBSERVE — logs, metrics, uptime and audit evidence
- IZAKHONO AI GATEWAY — replaceable AI model routing
- IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE — user-facing AI workspace
- SHELTON FORTRESS — security controls across the stack
- IZAKHONO CODE / BUILDER / NODE / CONTROL — software development and runtime control

## Africa expansion model

Do not attempt 54-country regulatory launches simultaneously.

Use a hub-and-spoke model:

1. South Africa — engineering, operating proof, revenue, governance.
2. SADC — countries with practical partner routes and customer demand.
3. East Africa — fintech/media/education partnerships.
4. West Africa — music, media, payments and education.
5. North Africa — later localisation and regulated partnerships.
6. Pan-African digital layer — unified brand and technology, locally compliant operating entities/partners.

## Capital discipline

Build shared software once, then let multiple entities use it through internal service contracts.

Priority order:

1. revenue-producing education and subscriptions
2. payment + access + ledger
3. media/music monetisation
4. insurance distribution/administration with licensed partners
5. financial products through licensed partners
6. cloud/data/edge scale
7. CAD/engineering commercialisation
8. regulated bank build/acquisition only when governance, capital and licensing are credible

## No false readiness

The group may build software for banking, insurance and payments before it has licences. It must not market itself as a bank, insurer, fund custodian or licensed financial institution until the relevant legal authorisation exists.

Software readiness, partner readiness, regulatory readiness and commercial readiness are separate gates.
