# AFRICA RISE — 90-Day Delivery Register

This register converts the founding charter into accountable work. It is a working control document, not evidence that listed work is complete.

## Status definitions
- **Not started** — no verified work has begun.
- **In progress** — work has started and has an accountable owner.
- **Blocked** — progress depends on a named decision, partner, resource or approval.
- **Ready for approval** — evidence is assembled and a decision is required.
- **Pilot approved** — scope, budget, risks, compliance and success measures are signed off.
- **Pilot active** — controlled delivery is underway.
- **Complete** — deliverable and acceptance evidence are recorded.

## Stage gates

### Gate 1 — Foundation
No pilot is promoted until it has:
- an accountable owner;
- a defined customer or beneficiary and evidenced need;
- a written scope and delivery plan;
- a cost estimate and identified funding source;
- a risk, legal and compliance review;
- measurable success and stop criteria.

### Gate 2 — Pilot approval
No external promise, customer activation or public launch until the relevant approvals, partner agreements, operational capacity, security controls and customer terms are in place.

### Gate 3 — Scale decision
Scale only when pilot evidence demonstrates demand, reliable delivery, acceptable unit economics, compliance and a credible support model.

## Weekly operating review

For each active work item record:
1. What was delivered this week?
2. What evidence proves completion?
3. What is the next milestone and due date?
4. What is blocked, by whom or by what?
5. What decision or resource is required?
6. What changed in cost, risk, customer impact or timeline?
