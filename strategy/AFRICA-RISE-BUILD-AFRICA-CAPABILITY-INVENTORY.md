# AFRICA RISE — BUILD AFRICA Manufacturing Capability Inventory

**Status:** Blank evidence register for internal verification.  
**Purpose:** Establish what the proposed anchor manufacturer can actually produce, in what quantity, to which specification, within what lead time and at what verified cost.  
**Control:** No blank field is evidence of capability. Do not publish this register or use it as a sales representation until the evidence is checked and approved.

## 8. Verification and sign-off

A capability item may be marked **Verified** only when:
1. evidence exists and is accessible to the authorised reviewer;
2. issuer, date, scope and validity have been checked;
3. the evidence matches the proposed product and buyer requirement;
4. gaps and limitations are recorded;
5. the reviewer and verification date are recorded.

## 9. Pilot release decision

**Do not release a buyer-facing capability statement or quotation until** the legal entity, relevant product capability, capacity, quality, material supply, costing, delivery plan and related-party disclosure have passed review.

A verified capability register is not itself a buyer order, certification, tender qualification or guarantee of delivery.
