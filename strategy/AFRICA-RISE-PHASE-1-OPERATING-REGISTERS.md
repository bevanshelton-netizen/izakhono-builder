**Qualification rule:** general interest, an informal conversation or a request for a catalogue is not a qualified order opportunity. A qualified brief needs a real requirement, written product specification, quantity, delivery timing, approval process, sample/quality requirements, procurement route and payment terms.

Do not enter sensitive personal information. Do not use buyer logos, names, order values or procurement intentions in public material without permission.

## 9. Phase 1 immediate action sequence

1. Confirm the responsible legal entity, contracting scope and authorised decision-maker.
2. Review the founding charter and governance adoption pack; record formal decisions.
3. Appoint accountable leads and approve delegated authority and financial thresholds.
4. Complete declarations and activate independent related-party review.
5. Verify the manufacturing capability inventory with source evidence and physical checks.
6. Conduct non-binding buyer discovery and collect written uniform specifications.
7. Verify the buyer/manufacturer fit, sample, costing, capacity, cash flow and delivery.
8. Score the pilot opportunity; submit a complete dossier for explicit approval.
9. Do not accept an order, buy speculative stock, promise delivery or announce a live pilot before approval.

**Current control position:** legal entity, appointments, financial thresholds, buyer demand, verified capacity and pilot approval remain open until evidence and authorised decisions are entered above.
