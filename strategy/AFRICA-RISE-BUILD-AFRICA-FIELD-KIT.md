### Interview questions
1. What product or service do you need, and what problem must it solve?
2. Can you provide the complete written specification and acceptance standard?
3. What quantities, sizes, materials, colours and packaging are required?
4. What is the delivery location and required date?
5. What sample, inspection, testing or certification evidence is mandatory?
6. How do you onboard suppliers and approve purchase orders?
7. What payment terms and supporting documents apply?
8. What are the most common quality or delivery problems with current suppliers?
9. What would make a new supplier credible enough to trial?
10. May we record this requirement and follow up with a costed proposal?

Do not record sensitive personal information or confidential procurement data beyond what is necessary and authorised.

## G. After-action review

After each completed or cancelled opportunity, record:
- Was the buyer requirement complete and realistic?
- Was the quotation accurate against actual cost?
- Did material and labour assumptions hold?
- Was delivery on time and accepted?
- What defects, rework or returns occurred?
- Was payment received according to agreed terms?
- What was actual gross margin and cash conversion time?
- What should change before the next quote?
- Is there evidence of repeat demand?

A buyer introduction, verbal interest or quote submission is not a completed sale. Record the stage accurately.
