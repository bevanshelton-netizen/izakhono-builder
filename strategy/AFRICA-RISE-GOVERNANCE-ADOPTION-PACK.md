## 3. Delegated authority — proposed control matrix

The legal entity must set actual monetary limits and signatory rules. Until it does, the following conservative rule applies: **no person may commit AFRICA RISE or an IZAKHONO entity to expenditure, a contract, a grant condition, a guarantee, a public-sector representation or a regulated service merely by virtue of a proposed programme role.**

| Activity | Required control |
|---|---|
| Routine planning and internal research | May proceed within existing employment authority and approved operating resources; no external commitment |
| Supplier or buyer discovery | Non-binding only; clearly identify exploratory status; no promise of award, volume, price or exclusivity |
| Purchase or expenditure | Approved itemised budget, identified funding source, authorised purchase approval and supporting invoice/receipt |
| Contract, MOU or letter of commitment | Legal review where appropriate and signature by an authorised representative of the contracting entity |
| Grant, sponsorship or restricted funding | Finance and legal review; written acceptance by authorised signatory; separate tracking and compliance with restrictions |
| Related-party procurement or referral | Written disclosure, independent review, documented rationale and approval by a non-conflicted authorised decision-maker |
| Public announcement or partnership claim | Evidence of the underlying arrangement and written permission to use partner name/logo; authorised communications approval |
| Customer data collection or service activation | Approved privacy notice, lawful basis, security controls, service terms, support process and applicable regulatory clearance |
| Borrowing, guarantees, equity, exclusivity or material IP transfer | Reserved decision of the responsible legal entity |

## 4. Financial control minimums

Before any pilot expenditure or receipt of funds:

1. Identify the contracting and accounting entity.
2. Approve an itemised budget with cost owner, purpose, timing and funding source.
3. Separate commercial revenue, grants, sponsorships, donations and restricted funds in the accounting records.
4. Use approved procurement steps and retain quotations, selection rationale, purchase approvals and invoices.
5. Require evidence of delivery and approval before payment.
6. Reconcile bank, payment-provider and accounting records at least monthly.
7. Maintain a commitments register, including signed contracts, purchase orders and contingent liabilities.
8. Record budget-to-actual variances, cash forecast, aged receivables/payables and restricted-fund balances.
9. Prohibit personal use of programme funds and undisclosed related-party transactions.
10. Escalate suspected fraud, bribery, diversion, conflicts or material control failures promptly.

**No budget amount or spending authority is approved by this draft.** Actual limits, bank mandates and approvers must be recorded in an authorised resolution.

## 5. Conflict-of-interest and related-party protocol

Because AFRICA RISE may work with businesses in the IZAKHONO ecosystem, related-party safeguards are essential.

- Every decision-maker and evaluator submits an initial and periodic interests declaration, and updates it when circumstances change.
- Disclose ownership, employment, family, financial, supplier, customer and other material interests relevant to a decision.
- Record each conflict in a register with the affected decision, mitigation, recusal and approving authority.
- A conflicted person must not score, recommend, negotiate, approve or oversee the affected transaction unless an authorised independent process explicitly permits a limited role.
- Use consistent, documented qualification criteria for IZAKHONO and external suppliers.
- Record market testing, price reasonableness, selection rationale, deliverables and independent approval.
- Keep programme administration and commercial delivery records distinguishable.
- Do not present AFRICA RISE as an independent endorsement of a related business or as a guaranteed source of work.

## 6. Risk, compliance and decision registers

Maintain one controlled register with, at minimum, these fields:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Risk / decision ID | Unique reference |
| Programme and workstream | Affected programme |
| Description | Specific event, uncertainty or decision |
| Cause and consequence | Why it may occur and what it affects |
| Inherent rating | Likelihood and impact before controls |
| Controls and evidence | Existing prevention/detection measures and proof |
| Owner | Named accountable person after appointment |
| Action and due date | Specific mitigation or decision required |
| Residual rating | Risk after controls |
| Status | Open / mitigating / escalated / accepted / closed |
| Approval | Named authorised decision-maker and date |

Initial risk categories to assess include legal-entity uncertainty, regulatory licensing, privacy, cybersecurity, safeguarding, product quality, supplier failure, buyer payment, working capital, delivery capacity, fraud/bribery, conflicts, restricted funding, reputational claims and cross-border trade compliance.

## 7. Pilot approval dossier — mandatory contents

A pilot cannot be marked **Pilot approved** until the dossier contains:

- named accountable owner and authorised sponsor;
- defined customer or beneficiary and documented evidence of need;
- precise scope, exclusions, geography, duration and delivery method;
- verified delivery capacity, suppliers and partner status;
- itemised budget, cash-flow forecast and confirmed funding source;
- commercial model and unit economics where relevant;
- legal, regulatory, privacy, security, safety and insurance review as applicable;
- procurement and conflict-of-interest controls;
- customer/beneficiary terms, complaints and support route;
- baseline, measurable outcomes, reporting cadence and evidence source;
- risk register, incident response and stop conditions;
- written approval by the authorised legal entity.

For the BUILD AFRICA uniform buyer-match candidate, additionally require verified manufacturer capacity, written buyer specification, sample approval, full cost build-up, acceptable margin and working-capital plan, delivery schedule, quality acceptance criteria and a signed order/contract before production begins. No buyer, order, price, capacity or revenue is presumed by this pack.
