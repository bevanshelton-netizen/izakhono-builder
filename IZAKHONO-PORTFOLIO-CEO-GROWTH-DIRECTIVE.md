# IZAKHONO PORTFOLIO CEO GROWTH DIRECTIVE

Effective: 23 September 2026

## Mandate

Every current and future IZAKHONO application is operated with a CEO-level growth system covering:

**Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue → Partnerships → Unit economics.**

Growth is a product responsibility, not an advertising afterthought.

## Portfolio operating chain

`Product → IZAKHONO CREATE → approved campaign package → IZAKHONO ADS → audience → product activation → retention/referral → revenue → CEO dashboard`

The existing Marketing Control Directive remains authoritative: all portfolio creative originates in IZAKHONO CREATE and all approved distribution is controlled through IZAKHONO ADS.

## CEO rules

1. **One primary customer and one primary action.** Every app must state who it serves and the most valuable next action.
2. **Activation over registrations.** A signup without a useful outcome is not growth.
3. **Organic proof before paid scale.** Use referrals, partnerships, creators, communities, search, direct sales and portfolio cross-promotion before increasing ad spend.
4. **Product-led distribution.** Public apps require sharing, referral, invite or partner-distribution mechanics appropriate to the product.
5. **Retention is designed.** Every app needs a recurring reason to return: workflow, learning progress, community, content, protection, payments, operations or repeat purchase.
6. **Revenue is explicit.** Every commercial app defines its revenue event and margin logic before paid acquisition.
7. **No surveillance growth.** No cross-site tracking, advertising IDs, hidden psychological profiles, dwell-time addiction scores or dark-pattern notification pressure.
8. **No spam growth.** Invitations and campaigns must be user-initiated or sent through authorised business channels with appropriate consent.
9. **Claims require evidence.** Financial, insurance, education, employment, health, security, broadcasting and industrial claims remain subject to their legal/commercial gates.
10. **Owned-first infrastructure.** Growth must not create a dependency that prevents the product from moving back to IZAKHONO-owned infrastructure.

## Growth archetypes

### Community / social
Win dense networks and communities. Growth loops: invites, organisers, public sharing, events and creator/community partnerships.

### Education
Win cohorts. Growth loops: campuses/schools/employers, learner referrals, progress milestones and institutional seats.

### Creator / media
Win creators and their audiences. Growth loops: artist onboarding, shareable content, live events, cross-promotion and creator economics.

### Commerce
Win repeat buyers and institutional accounts. Growth loops: product-led creative, WhatsApp sharing, team/school/corporate referrals and seasonal drops.

### B2B services
Win through trust and measurable outcomes. Growth loops: assessments, demos, founder-led sales, partner channels, case studies and recurring workflow value.

### Financial / security / trust
Win through evidence and integrations. Growth loops: bank/insurer/employer partnerships, API pilots, referrals and recurring risk/protection utility.

### Developer / infrastructure
Win portfolio adoption first. Growth loops: reference deployments, developer docs, technical integrations and enterprise pilots.

### Industrial programmes
Win qualified capital and strategic partners. Growth loops: prototypes, technical evidence, DFI/government engagement, supplier ecosystems and B2B contracts.

## CEO dashboard

Portfolio reviews must use aggregate operational metrics:
- activated users/accounts;
- qualified leads;
- activation rate;
- retained active accounts;
- referrals/shares/invites;
- partner-sourced accounts;
- conversion to revenue;
- gross revenue and contribution margin;
- support/safety/compliance backlog;
- infrastructure cost per active account.

Do not use engagement-at-any-cost as the portfolio north star.

## Portfolio cadence

**Monday — Numbers:** activation, retention, pipeline, revenue, margin, safety/compliance and infrastructure cost.

**Wednesday — Distribution:** partnerships, community/creator/employer/campus onboarding, campaign throughput and landing-page bottlenecks.

**Friday — Product growth:** remove onboarding friction, strengthen share/referral loops, review experiments, approve the next week's highest-leverage work.

## Release gate

A revenue-facing public app is not growth-ready until it has:
- 5-second landing clarity;
- one primary CTA;
- a working share/referral/partner loop appropriate to its archetype;
- activation event defined;
- retention loop defined;
- approved revenue route where applicable;
- CREATE campaign brief/creative package path;
- ADS distribution path;
- privacy/legal/compliance gate;
- aggregate measurement without behavioural surveillance.
