# IZAKHONO Portfolio Landing & Growth Standard

Every public-facing platform in the portfolio must explain itself immediately, look alive, and make sharing easy.

## 5-second rule
A first-time visitor should understand within 3–5 seconds:
- the platform name;
- what the platform is;
- who it is for;
- the main benefit;
- the single best action to take next.

## Platform-name rule
1. **Never tuck the platform name into a small corner logo.** The platform name is a primary hero element.
2. The name must be large, unmistakable and visually alive on desktop and mobile.
3. Use a distinctive display typeface appropriate to the platform category. Typography should feel intentional, striking and premium rather than generic.
4. Motion may be used sparingly — reveal, glow, pulse, waveform, kinetic type or other category-appropriate effects — but must not reduce readability or performance.
5. The logo may support the name, but the wordmark/name must remain clearly readable.
6. Each platform should have its own typography personality while still meeting portfolio accessibility and performance standards.

## Above-the-fold requirements
1. **Platform name** — dominant and prominent.
2. **Benefit-led headline** — say what the platform helps the visitor do.
3. **Plain-language subheadline** — one short explanation of the service.
4. **Service-specific hero graphic** — the visual must demonstrate the actual offer, not act as generic decoration.
5. **Primary CTA** — one obvious action such as Start, Buy, Enrol, Listen, Upload, Check, Book or Sign Up.
6. **Share action** — a visible Share button or share cluster available from the hero or persistent navigation.
7. **Mobile-first composition** — name, headline, useful visual and CTA must appear immediately on common phone screens.
8. **No empty/black opening screens** and no large spacer before the value proposition.
9. **No vague brand-only heroes** — branding supports the offer; it does not replace the explanation.
10. **Paid journeys** — where applicable, the primary purchase/enrolment flow should surface the approved iKhokha payment route clearly.

## Graphics rule
Graphics must speak loudly and specifically about the service. A visitor should be able to infer the category from the visuals even before reading all the copy.

Examples:
- music platform → artists, performance, audio player, live music;
- vehicle diagnostics → car, scan/diagnostic interface, repair quote/second opinion;
- learner-driver prep → road signs, Codes 08/10/14, motorcycle, simulator;
- ECD training → classroom, practitioner, virtual facilitator, course journey;
- business growth → owner, dashboard, roadmap, advisory interaction;
- market education → charts, learning modules, market intelligence, paper trading.

Avoid generic handshakes, random city skylines, abstract AI imagery or unrelated stock photos unless they directly support the service message.

## Share-it standard
Every public platform must include a working share mechanism.

Required behaviour:
- Use the native Web Share API on supported mobile devices.
- Provide fallback share actions for WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and copy-link.
- Share the canonical public URL, not a preview or admin URL.
- Use platform-specific Open Graph title, description and preview image so shared links look professional.
- Include UTM parameters or equivalent campaign tags on campaign/share links where practical so traffic can be measured.
- Ensure share buttons work before release.

## Social-advertising standard
Each revenue-facing platform should have a reusable social creative pack:
- square feed creative;
- vertical Story/Reel/TikTok creative;
- landscape/link-preview creative;
- short headline, primary copy and CTA;
- destination URL with campaign tracking.

Creative must visually show the product/service and use the platform's own striking typography. The advert should lead directly to the relevant landing/action page, not a generic portfolio homepage.

Distribution should cover the social channels actually connected and authorised for publishing, with platform-appropriate copy rather than identical spam across every network. Organic posts should be prioritised where paid advertising is not required.

## Visual hierarchy
**Platform name → what it does → proof/demo visual → primary action → share → secondary detail.**

## Acceptance test
Show only the first screen to someone with no prior context for five seconds. They should be able to answer:
1. What is this platform called?
2. What is this?
3. What can I do here?
4. What should I click next?

Then test sharing:
5. Can I share the platform in one or two taps?
6. Does the shared preview show the correct name, message and image?

If any answer is no, the landing experience is not finished.
