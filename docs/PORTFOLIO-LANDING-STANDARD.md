# IZAKHONO Portfolio Landing Standard

Every public-facing platform in the portfolio must explain itself immediately.

## 5-second rule
A first-time visitor should understand within 3–5 seconds:
- what the platform is;
- who it is for;
- the main benefit;
- the single best action to take next.

## Above-the-fold requirements
1. **Platform name** — clear and prominent.
2. **Benefit-led headline** — say what the platform helps the visitor do.
3. **Plain-language subheadline** — one short explanation of the service.
4. **Service-specific hero graphic** — the visual must demonstrate the actual offer, not act as generic decoration.
5. **Primary CTA** — one obvious action such as Start, Buy, Enrol, Listen, Upload, Check, Book or Sign Up.
6. **Mobile-first composition** — headline, useful visual and CTA must appear immediately on common phone screens.
7. **No empty/black opening screens** and no large spacer before the value proposition.
8. **No vague brand-only heroes** — branding supports the offer; it does not replace the explanation.
9. **Paid journeys** — where applicable, the primary purchase/enrolment flow should surface the approved iKhokha payment route clearly.

## Graphics rule
Graphics must speak loudly and specifically about the service. A visitor should be able to infer the category from the visuals even before reading all the copy.

Examples:
- music platform → artists, performance, audio player, live music;
- vehicle diagnostics → car, scan/diagnostic interface, repair quote/second opinion;
- learner-driver prep → road signs, Codes 08/10/14, motorcycle, simulator;
- ECD training → classroom, practitioner, virtual facilitator, course journey;
- business growth → owner, dashboard, roadmap, advisory interaction;
- market education → charts, learning modules, market intelligence, paper trading.

Avoid generic handshakes, random city skylines, abstract AI imagery or unrelated stock photos unless they directly support the service message.

## Visual hierarchy
**Platform → what it does → proof/demo visual → primary action → secondary detail.**

## Acceptance test
Show only the first screen to someone with no prior context for five seconds. They should be able to answer:
1. What is this?
2. What can I do here?
3. What should I click next?

If they cannot, the hero is not finished.
