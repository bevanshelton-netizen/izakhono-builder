# IZAKHONO Digital Growth & Website Production Pipeline

**Status:** Permanent portfolio operating model  
**Effective:** 26 September 2026

## Decision

IZAKHONO will no longer build public websites as isolated one-off projects.

Every qualifying website, landing page, campaign page and product front door moves through one reusable production line:

**Brief → Website Factory → Graphics & Media Studio → International Review → CRM Connection → Verified Deployment → Advertising Engine**

The objective is speed without lowering standards.

## The five permanent departments

### 1. Website Factory
Systems: **IZAKHONO WebStart, IZAKHONO Builder, IZAKHONO Fabric and each platform's own engine.**

Responsibilities:
- turn a short brief into the site structure;
- generate multiple design directions where useful;
- produce responsive coded pages;
- create SEO/social metadata;
- connect forms and legal-page requirements;
- run the international professionalism gate.

Reusable layout work must be automated. Human/custom development starts only where the product genuinely differs.

### 2. Graphics & Media Studio
Systems: **IZAKHONO CREATE, IZAKHONO STUDIO, IZAKHONO Creative Suite and owned/provider-neutral IZAKHONO SUPER AI media adapters.**

Responsibilities:
- original hero graphics;
- product and sector imagery;
- campaign banners;
- social creatives;
- investor/partner graphics;
- video/storyboard assets;
- image optimization and alt-text planning.

Images must be original or rights-clear, accurate to the product and strong enough for an international commercial site.

### 3. Advertising Engine
Systems: **IZAKHONO ADS, IZAKHONO SIGNAL, IZAKHONO Social and approved portfolio cross-promotion inventory.**

Responsibilities:
- organic campaign packages;
- scheduled publishing;
- partner/institutional outreach;
- portfolio cross-promotion;
- paid-media packages only after explicit budget authority.

Advertising does not bypass product readiness. A campaign cannot point to a broken, unfinished or unverified destination.

### 4. Lead & CRM Engine
Systems: **IZAKHONO CRM plus platform-specific lead and payment adapters.**

Responsibilities:
- capture leads, enquiries, quotes, enrolments and partner opportunities;
- create platform-scoped CRM records;
- assign owner, stage and next action;
- provide aggregate operational reporting.

CRM never invents payment success and never absorbs protected product records merely for convenience.

### 5. Deployment & Verification Engine
Systems: **IZAKHONO NODE → RUNTIME → EDGE/TLS → DNS**, with reversible external hosting only as resilience/fallback.

Responsibilities:
- deploy the approved build;
- verify health;
- verify public HTTPS;
- verify the actual intended content;
- preserve rollback;
- record evidence before the site is labelled LIVE.

## International release rule

A site is not ready because it compiles.

It must have:
- clear product/business name;
- clear offer and primary CTA;
- mobile, tablet and desktop quality;
- professional typography, spacing and hierarchy;
- strong product-appropriate graphics;
- working contact/lead/checkout journey;
- trust, privacy and legal presentation appropriate to the product;
- no placeholders, fake testimonials, fabricated counters or unsupported credentials;
- visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support;
- optimized media;
- 95+ quality score where the automated gate applies;
- real browser visual review;
- verified HTTPS 200 and intended-content check.

## Privacy and measurement

Portfolio growth uses **aggregate first-party operational metrics only**.

The pipeline prohibits:
- behavioural profiling;
- advertising IDs;
- cross-site surveillance;
- UTM/referral identifiers;
- silent analytics;
- dark-pattern engagement scoring.

Useful business metrics remain allowed: qualified leads, activated accounts, conversion to revenue, sales pipeline, paid orders, retained active customers and infrastructure cost.

## Speed standard

For ordinary brochure/corporate sites, the factory should reuse:
- layout;
- mobile system;
- navigation;
- lead form;
- CRM adapter;
- SEO/social metadata;
- legal structure;
- QA;
- deployment manifest.

Custom work is reserved for:
- brand identity;
- unique product interactions;
- commerce/payment;
- regulated workflows;
- complex dashboards;
- specialist media.

This is the operating model for all current and future IZAKHONO public products unless a signed product-specific exception supersedes it.
