# IZAKHONO NAV — Tracker & Response Network v1

## CEO product decision
IZAKHONO NAV is a dual-layer safety platform:

1. Phone-first software for mass adoption.
2. Optional dedicated vehicle/asset hardware for independent tracking and higher-assurance protection.

The tracker is not itself the emergency responder. It is a sensor/telemetry endpoint feeding the IZAKHONO NAV + FORTRESS response workflow.

## Product layers

### NAV App
- navigation
- AI Driver Companion
- SOS
- journey sharing
- road-risk intelligence
- emergency workflows
- family safety

### NAV Tracker
- GNSS/GPS location
- cellular connectivity
- backup battery
- vehicle power state
- tamper/disconnect detection
- geofencing
- movement/theft alerts
- optional ignition/vehicle telemetry
- optional impact/crash telemetry
- store-and-forward when connectivity is temporarily unavailable

### FORTRESS Response Cloud
- ingest tracker and phone events
- classify incidents
- correlate vehicle, journey and user signals
- notify authorised contacts
- route incidents to an authorised monitoring/response provider
- maintain incident audit trail
- continue location intelligence during an active incident

## Response hierarchy

1. Driver / vehicle owner
2. Nominated emergency contacts
3. IZAKHONO NAV/FORTRESS incident workflow
4. Contracted private-security/monitoring partner
5. Appropriate public emergency service where an authorised integration or customer workflow exists
6. Fleet/control-room operations for enterprise customers

IZAKHONO must not claim that it dispatches police, ambulance or fire services unless a verified operational integration/contract exists.

## Partner strategy

Preferred first-response partner target: Fidelity Services Group / Fidelity SecureDrive.
Secondary strategic targets: Tracker South Africa and Netstar.

The partnership model is intentionally non-exclusive: IZAKHONO owns the NAV/FORTRESS customer experience and intelligence layer; partners provide contracted monitoring, physical response, recovery and/or hardware capabilities where agreed.

## Commercial ladder

- NAV Free — phone only
- NAV Guardian — software safety subscription
- NAV Fortress — advanced protection subscription
- NAV Fortress Vehicle — tracker + subscription + response capability
- NAV Fleet — hardware + fleet intelligence + monitoring/response
- Enterprise/CIT/Government — negotiated contracts

## Hardware roadmap

Phase 1: integrate a proven GNSS/cellular tracker through an adapter/API layer.
Phase 2: branded/custom enclosure and installation program.
Phase 3: IZAKHONO-controlled firmware and hardware specification.
Phase 4: own hardware family across vehicle, motorcycle, trailer, asset and high-security use cases.

## Security and privacy requirements

- explicit user consent and lawful tracking basis
- owner/authorised-user access controls
- encrypted telemetry in transit and at rest
- least-privilege partner access
- incident audit logs
- tamper-resistant device identity
- device revocation and credential rotation
- privacy/retention controls appropriate to South African and target-market law
