# IZAKHONO AFRICA x MTN Strategic Digital Services Partnership Proposal

**Date:** 26 September 2026  
**Status:** Discussion draft

## Executive proposition

IZAKHONO AFRICA proposes a controlled 90-day strategic pilot with MTN around three customer-facing services:

1. **WorkNow** - employment and employability.
2. **FAISReady** - professional learning and exam preparation.
3. **KORA** - African creator economy, events, ticketing and commerce.

The common transaction and trust layer is **IZAKHONO PAY + MTN MoMo where approved**, with **FORTRESS** as an additional fraud/trust layer and consent-based customer/CRM integration.

The partnership thesis is simple: **MTN provides connectivity, MoMo and distribution; IZAKHONO provides practical digital services customers have reasons to use repeatedly.**

IZAKHONO remains independently deployable, retains its IP and multi-network capability, and treats MTN integrations as approved adapters rather than a dependency.

## Value to MTN

- New digital-service usage across employment, learning, creator commerce and later SME services.
- Additional eligible MoMo transaction demand from the IZAKHONO portfolio.
- New SME and employer engagement through WorkNow and future business tooling.
- Creator-economy engagement through KORA.
- A pipeline of practical mini-app/PWA candidates.
- Optional FORTRESS trust/risk capability as a complementary layer.
- Multilingual and AI-assisted experiences without adding behavioural tracking as a product requirement.

## Value to IZAKHONO

- Access to MTN connectivity and distribution channels subject to commercial approval.
- MoMo Collections and other approved payment capabilities.
- Developer APIs, sandbox environments and onboarding paths.
- Potential mini-app / super-app distribution subject to programme and country availability.
- MTN Business channels for later SME bundles.
- Commercial, security, regulatory and operational partnership for responsible scale.

## 90-day pilot

### WorkNow
**Need:** Find work and improve job readiness.  
**Experience:** Mobile jobs, candidate profiles, readiness tools and employer hand-off.  
**Measure:** Activated users, applications, employer enquiries and repeat use.

### FAISReady
**Need:** Prepare for professional exams.  
**Experience:** Mobile learning, practice and paid learning packages.  
**Measure:** Learner activation, completion, conversion and payment success.

### KORA
**Need:** Discover creators, events and services.  
**Experience:** Creator discovery, ticketing/commerce and artist tools.  
**Measure:** Active users, transactions, artist participation and repeat use.

### IZAKHONO PAY + MoMo
**Need:** Simple trusted payment.  
**Experience:** MTN MoMo Collections where approved, asynchronous status confirmation and reconciliation.  
**Measure:** Checkout initiation, success, abandonment and reconciliation accuracy.

## Pilot sequence

- **Weeks 1-2:** NDA, technical discovery, mini-app/API eligibility, legal/commercial workstream and KPI definition.
- **Weeks 3-5:** Sandbox integration, MoMo test flows, security review and privacy/data mapping.
- **Weeks 6-8:** Controlled user pilot.
- **Weeks 9-11:** Conversion, usage, payment and support optimisation.
- **Week 12:** Joint performance review and expansion decision.

## Commercial principles

- Revenue share only on business attributable to an MTN/MoMo distribution channel, under agreed attribution rules.
- Standard provider/payment fees apply as contractually agreed.
- No blanket exclusivity.
- Each party retains pre-existing IP; jointly developed IP requires a separate schedule.
- Customer data remains purpose-limited and governed by applicable law, consent and agreed processing terms.
- Later phases may bundle selected IZAKHONO SME tools with MTN Business connectivity and device/data packages.

## Technical principles

- MTN MoMo is added as an optional IZAKHONO PAY rail, not as a replacement for the payment abstraction layer.
- Payment is never marked paid solely from an unauthenticated callback; final status must be verified and reconciled.
- Selected services can be packaged as mini apps or PWAs where MTN approves the programme and market.
- MTN APIs are consumed only where the relevant product is approved and available in the target OpCo.
- FORTRESS complements but does not replace MTN security, banking, PCI or statutory controls.
- IZAKHONO-owned engines remain independently deployable.

## Expansion opportunities

- SME Digital Operating Stack: CRM, websites, marketing, bookkeeping/admin and payments.
- EDU-BUILD learning and approved skills pathways.
- FORTRESS merchant trust and anti-impersonation services.
- African-language AI assistance.
- Wider KORA creator services.
- Practical consumer utilities such as AUTO AI and learner-driver services.

## Immediate asks to MTN

1. Confirm the correct MoMo Collections production-onboarding route for a South African pilot.
2. Confirm eligibility and process for the MoMo Mini App programme or equivalent distribution route.
3. Nominate technical and commercial contacts.
4. Review WorkNow, FAISReady and KORA as pilot services.
5. Agree pilot KPIs, data boundaries, security requirements and commercial principles.
6. Schedule a 60-90 minute discovery workshop.

## Status boundary

No MTN-connected capability may be described as live until MTN onboarding, production credentials, endpoint/callback approval, HTTPS, end-to-end payment proof, reconciliation, privacy/security review and the applicable legal/commercial gates are complete.
