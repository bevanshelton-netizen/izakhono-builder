# IZAKHONO WORLD-CLASS DESIGN SYSTEM v1

## Purpose
Every IZAKHONO-owned site must communicate what the business actually offers, look premium and vibrant, and turn attention into a clear next action.

## Non-negotiable principles
- Product/service proposition is visible above the fold.
- One dominant CTA per page; secondary actions remain obvious.
- Use real brand/product imagery and purpose-built graphics before generic stock imagery.
- Typography is an identity system, not decoration: expressive display face + highly readable body face, with responsive sizing.
- Vibrant colour is intentional and brand-specific; preserve contrast and accessibility.
- Use editorial composition, strong grids, generous spacing, layered imagery, gradients and restrained motion where they improve comprehension.
- Mobile-first, fast, accessible and semantic. Visual quality never excuses slow or unusable pages.
- Every visual asset must have a job: explain, prove, differentiate, build trust or convert.
- No fake testimonials, fake metrics, fake product screens, fake payment states or invented claims.

## Standard page anatomy
1. Announcement / trust strip when useful.
2. Navigation with clear product/service categories.
3. Hero: concise value proposition + primary CTA + strong visual.
4. What we offer: 3–6 concrete offerings with short explanations.
5. Proof / capabilities / outcomes.
6. Product or service showcase with strong imagery/graphics.
7. How it works / customer journey.
8. Pricing or commercial CTA where appropriate.
9. Trust, credentials, partners or evidence where verified.
10. Final CTA.
11. Footer with complete navigation and contact routes.

## Visual direction
- Premium African/global identity: confident, contemporary, culturally grounded without becoming stereotypical.
- Strong headline scale; use variable fonts where practical.
- Distinctive colour accents, gradients and image treatment rather than generic template palettes.
- Use cards selectively; favour composed sections and visual storytelling over repetitive boxes.
- Motion should guide attention, reveal hierarchy or demonstrate a product. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Images should be high quality, correctly cropped, compressed and lazy-loaded where appropriate.

## Graphics-worker workflow
For each site, graphics/design workers should produce:
- hero art direction;
- image selection or generation brief;
- icon/illustration system;
- typography pairing and scale;
- colour tokens;
- section backgrounds and decorative graphics;
- product/service showcase graphics;
- social/share assets;
- favicon/OG assets;
- responsive visual QA checklist.

## Copy rule
Do not merely say that a company is innovative, world-class or professional. Say what it sells, who it serves, what problem it solves, what it costs where appropriate, and what the visitor should do next.

## Revenue rule
Commercial sites must make the buying path obvious. Where payment is available, expose the verified checkout. Where payment is not yet connected, use a truthful enquiry/order path and label the status accurately.

## Portfolio inheritance
This system is the default design contract for new IZAKHONO sites and for visual rebuilds. Existing original business ideas, IP and content are preserved; old implementation and deployment assumptions are not automatically preserved when rebuilding from scratch.

## Acceptance gate
A site is not design-complete until:
- the offer is immediately understandable;
- hero and CTA work on desktop and mobile;
- typography renders correctly;
- imagery is sharp and optimized;
- no broken links/assets exist;
- accessibility basics pass;
- page metadata and sharing images are present;
- performance is acceptable;
- all commercial claims and statuses are truthful;
- external deployment is independently verified before being called live.
