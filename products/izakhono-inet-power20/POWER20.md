# IZAKHONO I-NET — POWER 20

## Master build directive

POWER 20 is the product benchmark for the IZAKHONO Parallel Internet. Every major Google and Microsoft product category is treated as a capability baseline, then redesigned to deliver materially greater user value through privacy, automation, interoperability, resilience, African-first intelligence, ownership and control.

POWER 20 is not twenty disconnected clones. The products run on shared I-NET Core primitives: IZAKHONO ID, FORTRESS, permissions, privacy policy, storage, AI orchestration, payments, communications, service discovery and the controlled Internet bridge.

## The twenty baseline domains

1. Search — private universal search across web, I-NET, news, images, video, maps, commerce and specialist indexes.
2. AI — personal and business agents with explicit data boundaries and user-controlled memory.
3. Mail — secure intelligent mail with automation, translation, scheduling and threat protection.
4. Cloud Storage — owner-controlled files, sync, sharing, recovery and portability.
5. Documents — collaborative document creation, editing, research and AI assistance.
6. Spreadsheets — calculation, modelling, forecasting, automation and business intelligence.
7. Presentations — AI-assisted storytelling, design, data visualisation and live updating.
8. Calendar — predictive scheduling, availability, travel and workflow coordination.
9. Contacts — relationship intelligence without uncontrolled cross-service profiling.
10. Maps & Navigation — IZAKHONO NAV with safety, offline resilience and African road intelligence.
11. Browser/Web — privacy-first access to both the conventional Internet and I-NET.
12. Video — KORA NETWORK media, publishing, discovery and creator infrastructure.
13. Music — Allegro discovery, streaming, creator and rights infrastructure.
14. Meetings — secure video/audio meetings with AI notes and controlled retention.
15. Chat & Collaboration — private messaging, channels, files, tasks and organisation workspaces.
16. Office & Business Suite — integrated IZAKHONO WORK operating tools.
17. Enterprise Collaboration — identity, teams, projects, approvals, workflows and administration.
18. Identity — IZAKHONO ID with scoped permissions, portability and revocation.
19. Security — FORTRESS zero-trust protection, auditability and automated defence.
20. Developer & Cloud Platform — APIs, SDKs, app sandboxing, service registry, deployment and third-party ecosystem.

## POWER 20 privacy floor

- Data minimisation by default.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Explicit, scoped permissions.
- Deny-by-default third-party access.
- No silent cross-service tracking.
- Data-domain separation for identity, payments, location, education, communications and other sensitive records.
- User-visible access and security audit history.
- Export and deletion controls where technically and legally possible.
- AI memory and training boundaries controlled by policy.
- Enterprise/consumer data isolation.
- Child and family protections.
- Zero-trust authorisation at every service boundary.
- Independent security testing and vulnerability disclosure.

## Build rule

Existing IZAKHONO capabilities must be reused before new parallel implementations are created. External providers remain adapters, not sources of product truth, and can be replaced without changing the public I-NET contract.

No capability is labelled live, launched or production-ready until it has passed its relevant verification gate.
