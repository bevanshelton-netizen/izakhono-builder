## Selection rule

Do not draft every topic automatically. Score candidates for:

- Clear and recurring reader need
- Ability to deliver a practical outcome in a focused guide
- Reliable, current source availability
- Distinctiveness from existing products and books
- Suitability for a US$7 purchase
- Localisation and specialist-review requirements
- Responsible handling of risk

Select the strongest topics first, then produce them through the full research, writing, review, rights and PDF QA process.
