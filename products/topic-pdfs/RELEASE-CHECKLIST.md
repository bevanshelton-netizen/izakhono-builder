# Topic PDF Release Checklist

A product may move to **published** only after every applicable release gate is completed and recorded.

## Topic and product fit
- [ ] One specific reader problem and target reader are defined.
- [ ] The product promise is realistic and supported by the content.
- [ ] Scope, exclusions, jurisdiction and language are explicit.
- [ ] Existing books and PDF products were checked for overlap.
- [ ] The guide provides enough practical value to justify the US$7 target price.

## Research and writing
- [ ] Every material claim is mapped to one or more stable source IDs in the internal evidence ledger.
- [ ] Every source has been opened and checked by a human; search snippets and AI-generated citations are not verification.
- [ ] The evidence ledger records exact title, publisher, canonical URL, publication/update date, access date, relevant passage, jurisdiction, reliability limits and rights/reuse notes.
- [ ] Verified sources include a named human reviewer, review date and passage locator; unresolved, inaccessible, stale or contradicted sources are resolved or their claims removed/qualified.
- [ ] Claims have credible supporting sources.
- [ ] Sources are current enough for the subject and date-sensitive claims are checked.
- [ ] Primary/official sources were preferred where appropriate.
- [ ] Explanations are original; no source is copied or lightly paraphrased as the product.
- [ ] Examples and stories are original, clearly fictional/composite where applicable, and non-identifying.
- [ ] The document has developed explanations and practical steps, not only slogans or fragments.
- [ ] Limitations, uncertainty and alternative perspectives are handled honestly.

## Specialist and safety review
- [ ] Health, legal, financial, safety or other high-stakes content received appropriate qualified review.
- [ ] Local laws, services, terminology and referral routes are verified for the stated jurisdiction.
- [ ] The guide does not diagnose, guarantee results or misrepresent professional advice.
- [ ] Appropriate emergency or professional escalation guidance is included.

## Rights, privacy and independence
- [ ] Text is original and all quotations are limited, attributed and justified.
- [ ] Images, illustrations, fonts, icons and templates have documented usage rights.
- [ ] Personal stories and identifiable information have valid permission or are excluded.
- [ ] No affiliation, endorsement or accreditation is implied without written evidence.
- [ ] Reader-facing source list matches the internal evidence ledger and source-access dates are complete.
- [ ] Rights clearance is recorded separately from factual verification; a public URL is not a reuse licence.

## PDF design and final QA
- [ ] Cover/title page identifies title, author and edition/version.
- [ ] Typography, contrast, headings, page numbers and navigation are readable.
- [ ] PDF metadata is set and correct.
- [ ] Links work and are safe.
- [ ] Accessibility checks are completed to the intended standard.
- [ ] Rendered PDF was inspected page by page for clipping, widows/orphans, broken tables and layout issues.
- [ ] Proofreading and final source-list check are complete.
- [ ] File opens correctly and file integrity is verified.
- [ ] Publication date, version and next review date are recorded.

## Commercial release
- [ ] Product description matches the actual guide.
- [ ] Price is set to the US$7 target or an approved local equivalent.
- [ ] The final sale PDF is rendered separately from editorial proofs and contains no “EDITORIAL DRAFT — NOT FOR SALE” watermark.
- [ ] Publication status is explicitly set to published only after all review and PDF QA gates pass.
- [ ] Checkout uses the approved currency, exact price, product/publication identity and customer identity.
- [ ] Payment completion is verified server-to-server with signature validation and idempotent event processing; browser redirects alone never grant access.
- [ ] Active customer entitlements are granted only after confirmed payment and revoked/refunded through verified refund/dispute events.
- [ ] Customer library and private download are tested for ownership, entitlement, withdrawn/unpublished status, expired/revoked payment, storage outage and audit logging.
- [ ] Downloaded files are private, attachment-delivered, integrity-checked and not exposed through public object URLs.
- [ ] Storefront, refund, tax and delivery settings are reviewed before launch.
- [ ] No paid service or advertising is activated without explicit approval.
- [ ] Customer feedback and correction process are ready.
