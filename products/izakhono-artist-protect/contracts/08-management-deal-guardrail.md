## Stop and review if any answer is YES

[ ] Manager owns or receives copyright/master ownership.  
[ ] Manager receives commission on income they did not procure or materially service.  
[ ] Commission continues indefinitely after termination.  
[ ] Manager has unilateral renewal/options.  
[ ] Manager can sign contracts or borrow money in the artist's name under a broad power of attorney.  
[ ] Manager controls bank accounts or passwords without dual controls/audit trail.  
[ ] Expenses are uncapped or need no approval.  
[ ] Commission base is undefined (gross vs net; before/after taxes, agent fees, production costs).  
[ ] Manager receives a 360 share of touring, merchandise, sponsorship, acting or businesses without specific services/value.  
[ ] Artist cannot inspect books or statements.  
[ ] There is no performance obligation on the manager.  
[ ] There is no termination route for breach/non-performance.  
[ ] There is a conflict because the manager is also label/publisher/booking agent without disclosure.

## Minimum terms an attorney should verify

- exact services and minimum activity;
- term and options;
- commission percentage and calculation base;
- excluded/pre-existing income;
- post-term sunset commission;
- expense approval cap;
- accounting frequency;
- audit rights;
- conflicts/related-party deals;
- authority limits;
- termination/cure;
- dispute procedure;
- ownership of artist data/accounts;
- return of property/passwords after termination.
