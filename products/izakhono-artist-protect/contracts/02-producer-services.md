## 3. Composition

Composition ownership is **not** created merely by producing a recording. Any songwriting/composition share must be separately agreed and included in a signed split sheet.

## 8. Expenses and recoupment

No expense is recoupable unless it is listed here or approved in writing in advance: ____________________.

No cross-collateralisation applies unless separately negotiated and expressly written.

## 9. Termination

If material deliverables are not provided after written notice and a reasonable cure period, the non-defaulting party may terminate. Rights in unpaid/unused material must be addressed expressly.

## 10. Warranties

Each party warrants they have authority to contribute what they provide and will disclose known third-party claims.

## 11. Disputes

Parties first attempt good-faith written resolution. Material ownership disputes must be escalated before further exploitation of the disputed right.
