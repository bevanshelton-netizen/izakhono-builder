Each party may register/notify their agreed share with the relevant collecting society, publisher or rights administrator. Nobody may knowingly register a share inconsistent with this signed split without a later signed amendment.

No party may represent that they own more than their stated share. Material synchronisation, adaptation or other licences requiring co-owner approval will be handled according to applicable law and any later administration agreement.

Each party confirms that they have disclosed any publisher, administrator or prior assignment affecting their share.

## 7. Amendments

Changes to percentages must be in writing and signed by every affected owner.

A dispute over one person's share should, where operationally possible, hold only the disputed share while undisputed shares remain accounted for.
