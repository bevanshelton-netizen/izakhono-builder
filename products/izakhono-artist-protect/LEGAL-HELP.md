# ARTIST PROTECT — LEGAL HELP ESCALATION

Use this guide when the Contract Shield says `review_required` or `lawyer_required`.

## Lawyer-required matters

Escalate before signature where the proposed deal includes:
- copyright/master assignment;
- long-term or perpetual exclusivity;
- management representation;
- publishing assignment/administration with unusual economics;
- 360 participation;
- broad power of attorney;
- cross-collateralisation;
- uncapped indemnity/liability;
- major advances/guarantees;
- settlement of an ownership dispute;
- litigation/takedown counterclaims;
- minor artists with material commercial commitments.

## Verify the lawyer

Use the South African Legal Practice Council practitioner search to confirm that a proposed attorney/advocate is on the practising roll.

Official directory:
https://lpc.org.za/members-of-the-public/search-practitioners/

ARTIST PROTECT should record:
- practitioner name;
- LPC confirmation date/reference;
- practice/firm;
- declared entertainment/IP expertise;
- conflict check;
- fee quote;
- scope of review.

## If the artist cannot afford advice

Legal Aid South Africa offers legal advice/representation subject to its eligibility and matter rules. Government lists the toll-free advice line as 0800 110 110.

ARTIST PROTECT must not promise that Legal Aid will accept a commercial entertainment matter. It is a referral option, not guaranteed representation.

## Rights organisations

Depending on the right involved:
- SAMRO — performing rights for composers/authors/publishers;
- CAPASSO — mechanical rights administration/licensing for composers/authors/publishers;
- SAMPRA — needletime/neighbouring rights for recording artists/record companies.

These organisations do not replace a private attorney for a disputed contract.

## Platform rule

No IZAKHONO employee, AI assistant or automated workflow may tell an artist that a lawyer-required contract is "safe to sign". It may:
- explain clauses in plain language;
- compare terms against the guardrail;
- identify missing terms/red flags;
- prepare questions/amendment requests;
- preserve evidence;
- route to a verified practitioner.
