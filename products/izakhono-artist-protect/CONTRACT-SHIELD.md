# IZAKHONO CONTRACT SHIELD

Contract Shield is the artist-facing legal-assistance layer of ARTIST PROTECT.

It is designed to make the **safe default** obvious and the dangerous deal difficult to sign accidentally.

## Golden rules

1. Never sign away copyright, masters or exclusivity because somebody says "this is standard".
2. Never rely only on a WhatsApp, DM or verbal split when money will flow from the work.
3. Separate composition ownership from master ownership and performer rights.
4. Put percentages, royalty bases, deductions, recoupment and accounting dates in writing.
5. Require a defined term, territory, rights granted and termination path.
6. Do not grant a broad power of attorney for convenience.
7. Do not allow uncapped expenses or recoupment without an approved budget.
8. Do not allow cross-collateralisation by default.
9. Preserve audit/accounting rights.
10. Preserve attribution/credit and moral-right protections.
11. Treat minors, samples, beats, covers and AI-source material as special clearance cases.
12. When a deal transfers major future value, the system must say **DO NOT SIGN YET — LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED**.

## Contract status

- DRAFT — facts incomplete.
- REVIEW — standard business/legal review required.
- LAWYER_REQUIRED — high-risk rights/economic transfer.
- READY_TO_SIGN — facts complete and red flags resolved.
- SIGNED — signature evidence and document hash stored.
- SUPERSEDED — replaced by a newer agreement; old evidence retained.
- DISPUTED — legal hold on affected rights.

## Minimum contract facts

Every agreement should identify:

- full legal names and identifying details of parties;
- stage/business names if used;
- exact work/release/event;
- rights granted and rights expressly retained;
- ownership before and after the agreement;
- payment/royalty amount and calculation base;
- approved deductions;
- recoupment terms;
- accounting/payment timetable;
- term and territory;
- exclusivity or non-exclusivity;
- credit/attribution;
- warranties/permissions;
- termination;
- dispute process;
- governing law/jurisdiction;
- signature and date.

## Evidence

For every signed document, ARTIST PROTECT stores:

- original file/private object reference;
- SHA-256 document hash;
- signer identity/contact;
- signature method;
- timestamp;
- version/template code;
- supersession/dispute history.

The evidence ledger is append-only.

## Important boundary

The templates are artist-protection starting points and deal checklists. They are not a substitute for individual legal advice.

Attorney review is mandatory inside the workflow where a contract involves a substantial assignment of copyright/master rights, long-term exclusivity, management, publishing administration with unusual terms, 360 participation, broad powers of attorney, material guarantees, cross-collateralisation, uncapped indemnities, litigation settlement or a significant dispute.
