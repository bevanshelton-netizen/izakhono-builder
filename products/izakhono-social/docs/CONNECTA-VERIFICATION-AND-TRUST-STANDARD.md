# CONNECTA Verification & Trust Standard

## Non-negotiable rule

**Everything presented as factual, official, partnered, verified, charitable, promotional, financial, or beneficial must be verified before CONNECTA presents it as such.**

If verification is incomplete, CONNECTA must say so clearly and must not imply endorsement.

## Applies to

- NPOs and NGOs
- Adopt-a-Spot organisations
- fundraising campaigns
- beneficiaries and campaign organisers
- corporations and commercial partners
- exclusive deals and promotions
- coupons and vouchers
- flights, hotels and travel offers
- vehicles and inventory
- competitions and giveaways
- free products/services
- jobs, scholarships and opportunities
- event tickets
- prices, stock, availability and claims
- impact figures and beneficiary counts
- payment and donation status
- testimonials and evidence

## Trust states

Every externally meaningful record must have an explicit state:

1. `PENDING_VERIFICATION` — submitted, not yet verified; not promoted as verified.
2. `VERIFIED` — required checks passed and evidence recorded.
3. `ACTIVE` — verified and currently eligible for public discovery.
4. `SUSPENDED` — temporarily removed from discovery pending investigation/reverification.
5. `EXPIRED` — validity period ended; historical record retained where appropriate.
6. `REJECTED` — failed verification or eligibility requirements.
7. `COMPLETED` — campaign/offer/project completed and outcome evidence recorded where applicable.

## Fundraising verification

A fundraising campaign cannot become publicly active until CONNECTA has verified, as applicable:

- organiser identity
- organisation identity and legal/registration information where applicable
- beneficiary identity and relationship to organiser
- purpose and requested amount
- destination/payment account ownership or authorised beneficiary relationship
- supporting evidence appropriate to the campaign
- required consent and safeguarding information
- applicable geographic/legal requirements
- fraud, sanctions and abuse screening appropriate to the service

Verification does not mean CONNECTA guarantees the underlying outcome. The platform must distinguish **verified identity/evidence** from **guaranteed results**.

## Donations and disbursements

CONNECTA must never display a donation as received, cleared, disbursed or delivered until the corresponding payment/settlement state is confirmed by the authorised payment system.

Required states should distinguish:

`PLEDGED → PAYMENT_PENDING → RECEIVED → SETTLED → DISBURSEMENT_PENDING → DISBURSED → CONFIRMED`

Failed, reversed, refunded or disputed funds must never be counted as completed giving.

## Adopt-a-Spot

An organisation may be nominated by anyone, but nomination is not endorsement.

A public Adopt-a-Spot profile must clearly distinguish:

- community nominated
- verification pending
- verified organisation
- adopted
- sponsorship active
- impact evidence submitted
- impact evidence reviewed

No corporation or individual may be described as an official partner unless a valid agreement exists and its status has been recorded.

## Commercial offers

CONNECTA must verify the partner's authority to make the offer and the material offer terms before publication. Price, discount, stock, expiry, eligibility, territory, redemption method and material conditions must be evidence-backed.

An offer must not be labelled `EXCLUSIVE TO CONNECTA` unless the exclusivity is supported by a valid agreement covering the stated scope.

## Competitions and giveaways

Before promotion, CONNECTA must verify the organiser, rules, eligibility, dates, prize description, winner-selection mechanism and applicable legal requirements. A winner must not be announced as factual until the result is confirmed.

## Impact reporting

Impact claims must be evidence-backed. CONNECTA must distinguish:

- committed
- received
- spent/disbursed
- delivered
- beneficiaries reached
- outcome evidenced

Unverified claims are not converted into headline impact numbers.

## User submissions

Users may nominate organisations, campaigns, offers and opportunities. User submission creates a **lead for verification**, not an endorsement.

The interface must make this distinction obvious.

## Safety and fraud controls

Verification must be complemented by reporting, investigation, suspension, audit trails, duplicate detection, payment reconciliation, abuse monitoring and re-verification when material information changes.

Sensitive identity documents and financial information must not be exposed publicly. Store only what is necessary, protect it appropriately, and expose verification badges/status rather than private evidence.

## Product rule

**No badge without evidence. No partner claim without agreement. No donation claim without settlement. No impact claim without evidence. No promotion without verified terms. No fabricated beneficiary, price, prize, stock, result or outcome.**

CONNECTA's promise is simple:

> **Good Samaritans should be able to give with confidence, while communities and organisations receive support with dignity, transparency and accountability.**
