# CONNECTA Exclusive Partner Promotions Standard

## Commercial rule

A company that becomes an official CONNECTA commercial partner must receive a defined package of **exclusive deals, promotions, offers, launches, or customer benefits distributed through CONNECTA** for the agreed partnership term.

CONNECTA is not merely an advertising channel. Partnership must create measurable commercial value for the partner and a real benefit for CONNECTA users.

## What "exclusive" means

The agreement must identify the exact exclusivity promised. It may cover one or more of:

- CONNECTA-only price or discount
- CONNECTA-only vehicle/product bundle
- CONNECTA-only voucher or promo code
- early-access launch
- CONNECTA member bonus
- limited-time CONNECTA campaign
- exclusive financing/upgrade/service incentive where legally permitted
- CONNECTA-only event or activation
- category-exclusive featured placement for an agreed period
- exclusive lead-generation campaign

Exclusivity must never be implied. It must be written into the signed commercial agreement with scope, territory, duration, product/category, eligibility and redemption rules.

## Partner lifecycle

`Prospect → Commercial due diligence → Partnership agreement → Offer creation → Approval → Campaign → Lead attribution → Redemption/sale → Reporting → Renewal`

## Required partner record

Each approved partner should have:

- legal entity and trading name
- verified business identity
- category and subcategory
- authorised contact
- agreement status
- agreement start/end dates
- exclusivity scope
- offer catalogue
- campaign status
- tracking/attribution configuration
- lead destination
- redemption mechanism
- reporting permissions
- compliance/claims review status

## CONNECTA offer engine

The product architecture should support structured offers rather than relying on ordinary social posts alone.

Each offer should have:

- `partner_id`
- `category_id`
- `offer_type`
- `title`
- `description`
- `benefit`
- `eligibility`
- `valid_from`
- `valid_until`
- `exclusive_to_connecta`
- `promo_code` or redemption mechanism
- `landing_destination`
- `inventory/reference data` where applicable
- `territory`
- `terms_url`
- `approval_status`
- `campaign_id`
- attribution parameters

## Vehicle example

A vehicle partner such as a Japan Cars-type importer/dealer could agree to provide CONNECTA members with a defined exclusive benefit, for example:

> CONNECTA EXCLUSIVE: qualifying customers receive the agreed partner benefit when the purchase/enquiry is attributed to CONNECTA during the campaign period.

The actual discount, inventory, eligibility and legal terms must come from the signed partner agreement. CONNECTA must never invent an offer or represent a company as partnered before signature.

## Partner visibility

Official partners should have a dedicated commercial presence inside the relevant category:

`Vehicles → Official Partners → Partner Storefront → Exclusive Deals → Inventory → Enquire → Track Lead`

The same model can apply to Property, Jobs, Shopping, Travel, Education, Agriculture, Technology, Sports and other CONNECTA categories.

## Revenue model

CONNECTA may earn through an agreed combination of:

- qualified lead fees
- sale/transaction commissions
- campaign fees
- sponsored commercial placement
- partner storefront subscriptions
- campaign production services
- API/inventory integration fees
- analytics/reporting services
- event and activation packages

Commercial fees must be transparent in the partner agreement.

## Trust and compliance

CONNECTA must distinguish clearly between:

- official partner
- verified business account
- sponsored campaign
- exclusive CONNECTA offer
- ordinary user-generated content

No company may be described as a partner until the commercial agreement is executed. No discount, price, stock level, financing claim, warranty or availability claim may be fabricated by CONNECTA.

## Strategic principle

**If a company partners with CONNECTA, its customers must have a reason to choose CONNECTA.**

The partnership promise is therefore:

**Exclusive value for CONNECTA users. Measurable sales opportunities for partners. Transparent attribution for everyone.**
