# CONNECTA GIVING — Good Samaritan Fundraising

CONNECTA Giving is the platform's GoFundMe-style social-impact layer. Its purpose is to help Good Samaritans discover verified needs, contribute safely, and see what their support changed.

## Product promise

**Good Samaritans can help change lives.**

CONNECTA Giving supports:
- personal emergency and life-change fundraisers;
- NPO/NGO and community-organisation campaigns;
- Adopt-a-Spot sponsorships;
- education, food, housing, health, disability, youth and community projects;
- disaster and urgent community response;
- in-kind offers such as food, clothing, equipment, transport and professional services;
- recurring or once-off support where payment rails and applicable law permit.

## Trust model

A submitted campaign is not automatically verified. CONNECTA distinguishes:

- Community nominated
- Identity pending
- Organisation verified
- Campaign verified
- Fundraising active
- Funds/disbursement pending
- Impact reported
- Completed
- Suspended/review required

Verification, fraud controls, beneficiary protection, payment-provider controls, applicable fundraising law, sanctions/AML requirements where relevant, and platform safety rules must be applied before a campaign is represented as verified.

## Campaign record

A future engine-backed campaign should include:

`campaign_id`, `owner_id`, `organisation_id`, `category_id`, `title`, `story`, `beneficiary_summary`, `goal_amount`, `currency`, `raised_amount`, `supporter_count`, `location`, `cause`, `verification_state`, `risk_state`, `payment_state`, `start_at`, `end_at`, `cover_media`, `updates`, `terms_url`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.

## Good Samaritan flow

Discover → Understand → Verify status → Choose amount/action → Confirm through approved payment rail → Receipt → Campaign update → Impact evidence.

The platform must clearly distinguish a **pledge/intent**, a **successful payment**, **pending funds**, and **available/disbursed funds**. CONNECTA must never imply that money was received or delivered when it was not confirmed by the payment/disbursement system.

## Revenue and sustainability

Basic discovery and ordinary fundraising participation should not be paywalled. CONNECTA may earn through clearly disclosed payment-provider economics, optional campaign production/support services, verified organisation tools, enterprise impact programmes, sponsored impact campaigns, and other lawful value-added services. Fees must never be hidden from the fundraiser or supporter.

## No fabrication rule

Do not seed the public product with invented beneficiaries, donation totals, partners, prizes, emergency claims, bank details, campaign outcomes or testimonials. Demo campaigns must be explicitly marked as DEMO and must not accept real money.

## Relationship to Adopt-a-Spot

Adopt-a-Spot is the organisation/community sponsorship layer. CONNECTA Giving is the broader campaign layer. An Adopt-a-Spot organisation may publish multiple Giving campaigns, while an individual may create an eligible campaign without being an organisation.

## Freedom and safety

Fundraising is subject to CONNECTA Community Standards, fraud prevention, child/beneficiary safeguarding, privacy, intellectual-property and applicable-law requirements. Legitimate causes should remain accessible regardless of popularity or political viewpoint, while credible fraud, exploitation, threats and other serious harms are acted on proportionately with notice and appeal mechanisms where appropriate.
