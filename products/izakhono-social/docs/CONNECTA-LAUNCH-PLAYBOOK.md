# CONNECTA Launch Playbook

## Founding cohort

Launch with a deliberately mixed founding cohort:
- community organisers
- sports clubs
- schools/training institutions
- creators
- SMEs
- family/community groups

Each founding partner receives:
- official profile/community setup
- admin onboarding
- unique invite code
- launch checklist
- content prompts
- reporting/safety guide

## Activation script

The product should guide every new user through:
1. choose name/handle,
2. connect with at least 3 real people,
3. join at least 1 community,
4. choose feed preference,
5. publish or reply once,
6. create/share an invite.

## Weekly CEO cadence

Monday: review activation, invite conversion, community health and safety backlog.

Wednesday: onboard new anchor organisations and unblock product issues.

Friday: review public feedback, select communities to highlight and approve next week's growth experiments.

No growth experiment may introduce behavioural tracking or weaken moderation.
