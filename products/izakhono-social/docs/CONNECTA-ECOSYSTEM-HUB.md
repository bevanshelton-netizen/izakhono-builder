# CONNECTA Ecosystem Hub

## Purpose

CONNECTA is the social and discovery layer of the IZAKHONO ecosystem. The Ecosystem Hub gives users one coherent entry point into specialised experiences while preserving the identity, purpose and brand of each product.

**Positioning:** One ecosystem. Many worlds. One identity.

## Core worlds

| World | Purpose | Trust / commercial boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CONNECTA Social | People, posts, communities, messaging | Ordinary lawful participation remains free; no paywall on ordinary speech |
| DOTSEE | Digital discovery / ecosystem footprint | Discovery layer; does not imply endorsement of listed entities |
| Allegro Vibez | Music, radio, artists, entertainment | Rights and partner claims require evidence |
| KORA | African content, culture and creators | Creator identity and rights must be verified where required |
| YHVH | Gospel, worship, ministries and Christian creators | Ministry/partner claims require verification |
| Giving | Good Samaritan fundraising | Donation and settlement claims require evidence |
| Adopt-a-Spot | NPO/NGO/community adoption | Organisation verification before official publication |
| Benefits | Coupons, deals, flights, competitions, giveaways and opportunities | Offer terms, dates and redemption must be verified |
| Commerce | Businesses, products and services | Business identity and material claims require verification |
| Vehicles | Vehicle discovery and partner commerce | Seller identity, inventory and commercial terms must be verified |
| IZAKHONO NAV | Mobility, driver safety and navigation | Safety-critical claims require appropriate evidence |
| IZAKHONO ONE | AI, search, intelligence and business tooling | Provider independence and data dignity remain architectural requirements |

## Discovery categories

The hub exposes major real-life categories including Vehicles, Property, Jobs & Careers, Business & Services, Shopping & Products, Technology, Sports, Music & Entertainment, Education & Training, News & Community, Travel & Hospitality, Food & Lifestyle, Agriculture, Finance & Opportunities, Government & Public Services, Faith & Culture, Arts & Creativity, Health & Wellness and Events.

## Integration principles

1. One IZAKHONO identity should work across the ecosystem where technically and legally appropriate.
2. Product brands remain distinct; integration must not erase their identity.
3. CONNECTA is the social/discovery layer, not the owner of third-party intellectual property or rights by default.
4. External services are adapters or integrations, not automatic production dependencies.
5. IZAKHONO-owned infrastructure remains the preferred production authority.
6. No partner relationship is represented as official before a signed and verified agreement.
7. No charity, beneficiary, prize, offer, price, inventory, impact or settlement claim is represented as verified without evidence.
8. Users can nominate entities and opportunities, but submission is not verification.
9. The ecosystem should monetise useful value—commerce, business tooling, creator services, enterprise, campaigns, analytics, referrals and partnerships—not ordinary speech.
10. Privacy, security, accessibility, low-bandwidth resilience and multilingual African usability are first-class requirements.

## User journeys

### Commerce

Discover → verify → enquire → partner handoff → outcome/attribution → reporting.

### Giving

Discover → verify → give → settlement → delivery/evidence → impact.

### Benefits

Discover → verify terms → claim/redeem → partner outcome → attribution.

### Creator / media

Discover → follow → community → content → event/commerce/support where applicable.

## Route

The initial hub is available at `/ecosystem` within the CONNECTA web application.

## Verification

This hub is a product surface and architectural integration point. It does **not** by itself prove that any named third-party company, ministry, charity, offer, inventory feed, API, domain, route or commercial agreement is active. Activation requires the relevant evidence gate.
