# CONNECTA Partner Commerce Agreement Framework

**Purpose:** standard operating framework for commercial partners such as vehicle dealers, retailers, service companies, creators and other approved organisations.

> This is a product/commercial framework, not legal advice and not a signed agreement. Local counsel should convert it into a jurisdiction-specific contract before execution.

## 1. Parties

- IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD / CONNECTA operator
- Partner legal entity
- Approved trading/brand name
- Responsible commercial and technical contacts

## 2. Partnership scope

The agreement identifies the category, products/services, territories, campaign period, approved assets, inventory source and commercial model.

For a vehicle partner, the scope can include vehicle listings, dealer profile, stock feeds, lead generation, campaign distribution and sales attribution.

## 3. Partner obligations

Partner must provide accurate, current and lawful inventory information; maintain required licences/registrations; honour represented pricing and availability subject to stated terms; respond to qualified enquiries; and promptly correct or withdraw unavailable listings.

## 4. CONNECTA obligations

CONNECTA provides the agreed category surfaces, listing/campaign tooling, attribution mechanisms, reporting and technical distribution services, subject to platform availability, safety controls and applicable law.

## 5. Commercial terms

The agreement must explicitly state one or more of:

- fixed subscription;
- campaign fee;
- listing fee;
- qualified-lead fee;
- referral fee;
- percentage commission on completed sales;
- revenue share;
- custom enterprise pricing.

No commercial term is considered active until signed by authorised representatives.

## 6. Attribution

Every commercial listing/campaign should receive an immutable campaign/listing reference and tracked enquiry ID. Attribution records should identify source category, campaign, partner, listing, timestamp and conversion status without exposing unnecessary personal information.

## 7. Consumer data

Lead collection must be purpose-specific and consent-aware. Only data required for the agreed enquiry flow should be collected and transferred. Partner use of lead data must comply with applicable privacy law and the agreed data-processing terms.

## 8. Advertising and disclosure

Paid or partner-controlled distribution must be clearly identifiable as sponsored, partner or commercial content where required. CONNECTA must not disguise paid placement as independent editorial endorsement.

## 9. Verification and trust

Partner activation requires KYB/identity/business verification appropriate to the risk and jurisdiction. Verification status must be auditable and revocable.

## 10. Prohibited conduct

No partner may use CONNECTA to facilitate fraud, impersonation, deceptive pricing, unlawful products/services, discriminatory unlawful conduct, spam, manipulation, fake reviews, fake scarcity or misleading claims.

## 11. Suspension and termination

CONNECTA may suspend commercial distribution where there is a credible safety, fraud, legal or material contractual issue. The partner should receive a reason and remediation path where appropriate. Termination terms, notice and surviving obligations must be stated in the signed contract.

## 12. Intellectual property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. The agreement grants only the licences necessary to display, format, distribute and market approved partner assets during the agreed term.

## 13. Performance reporting

Reports may include impressions, category views, listing views, enquiries, qualified leads, click-throughs, campaign cost, attributable conversions and partner-reported sales. CONNECTA must distinguish measured events from partner-reported outcomes.

## 14. No fabricated partnership

CONNECTA software may contain partner templates and integrations, but a company must not be displayed as an active CONNECTA commercial partner until the agreement and required verification are complete.
