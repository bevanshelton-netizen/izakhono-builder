# CONNECTA — Freedom & Responsibility Charter

## Product principle

CONNECTA is a free-to-use social network. Basic participation is not paywalled.

Users should be able to post, reply, share, upload video and use live broadcasting without a subscription-based reduction in ordinary expression.

There is no paid tier whose purpose is to unlock ordinary speech, longer ordinary video, or ordinary live participation.

Paid products may provide business, creator, commerce, analytics, storage, discovery, support or other value-added tools, but they must not become a paywall around basic expression.

## Freedom of expression

CONNECTA should protect lawful expression and viewpoint diversity.

The platform should not remove content merely because it is unpopular, controversial, critical of CONNECTA, critical of public institutions, politically inconvenient, religiously controversial, or contrary to the opinion of a moderator.

Moderation decisions must be based on published rules and the actual conduct/content at issue, not on the identity, popularity or political viewpoint of the speaker.

## Responsible use

Freedom of expression is paired with responsibility.

CONNECTA may restrict or remove content and accounts for serious violations of its published Community Standards, including:

- credible threats and targeted violence
- child sexual exploitation or sexual abuse material
- non-consensual intimate imagery
- direct facilitation of serious criminal harm
- targeted harassment and stalking
- fraud, impersonation and coordinated deception intended to cause harm
- malicious distribution of private or highly sensitive personal information
- spam, bot abuse and platform manipulation
- copyright or other rights violations where legally required and properly established
- content that must be restricted by applicable law

The standard is not "anything a moderator dislikes." The standard is whether a defined rule has been violated and whether enforcement is proportionate.

## Live video

Live broadcasting is a normal CONNECTA capability, not a premium privilege.

CONNECTA should support responsible live use with:

- reporting during a live
- moderator intervention
- blocking and muting
- account safety controls
- replay/report workflows
- emergency escalation for credible imminent threats
- clear enforcement notices
- appeals after enforcement

Where a live broadcast creates an immediate and serious safety risk, CONNECTA may intervene before the broadcast concludes.

## Video

Ordinary users should be able to publish video without a subscription being required to unlock normal video length.

Technical capacity, abuse prevention and storage limits may be managed as infrastructure requirements, but monetization must not be designed to deliberately cripple ordinary expression in order to force upgrades.

## Appeals and due process

Enforcement should be explainable and reviewable.

For significant actions, CONNECTA should provide:

1. the rule or safety reason involved,
2. the action taken,
3. the relevant duration where applicable,
4. a route to appeal,
5. human review for appropriate high-impact cases,
6. restoration when an appeal establishes that enforcement was incorrect.

Emergency interventions may be immediate, but the review process must remain available.

## Monetization rule

CONNECTA makes money from value created around the network, not from restricting ordinary speech.

Preferred revenue layers include:

- business subscriptions
- creator and organiser tools
- commerce and transaction services
- event services
- optional storage or operational services where genuinely additive
- contextual sponsorships
- enterprise/API services

Basic free participation remains the foundation of network growth.

## CEO rule

**Free expression. Responsible use. Proportionate enforcement. Transparent rules. Real appeals. No paywall on ordinary speech.**

CONNECTA should be a place where people can disagree strongly without being silenced simply for disagreeing, while still protecting people from serious harm and abuse.
