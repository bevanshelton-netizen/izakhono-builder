# IZAKHONO SUPER SEO V4 — Growth Engine

V4 turns search opportunities into an executable acquisition funnel:

`Search opportunity → Content brief → Human approval → Publish → CTA → Lead → Revenue attribution`

## Modules

- `growth-engine.mjs` — deterministic campaign and content-brief generation.
- Search metrics are used only when a configured provider actually supplies them.
- Revenue is never inferred from rankings, traffic, or generated content.
- Attribution is designed around first-party, consent-aware events.

## Operating model

1. Run SUPER SEO search intelligence.
2. Rank opportunities by observable signals.
3. Generate a campaign for the highest-value opportunities.
4. Produce a content brief.
5. Human approves factual/product claims.
6. Publish to an owned destination.
7. Capture a clear CTA conversion.
8. Join lead/customer/revenue events using the campaign identifier.
9. Measure actual outcomes and feed them back into prioritisation.

## Guardrails

V4 deliberately does **not** automate backlink exchanges, fabricate search volume/rankings/leads/revenue, or create mass low-value AI pages. The objective is durable customer acquisition from useful content and measurable first-party outcomes.
