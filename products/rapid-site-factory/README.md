# IZAKHONO RAPID SITE FACTORY

## Operating rule

Client websites are revenue deliverables. Hosting, DNS, email, Commander and internal infrastructure must never block a reviewable website preview.

## Production pipeline

1. Capture a short client brief.
2. Select an industry/site profile.
3. Generate the site from reusable sections and content blocks.
4. Apply brand identity and business content.
5. Run automated content, responsive and link checks.
6. Publish an immediate preview URL.
7. Collect approval/revisions.
8. Attach the custom domain.
9. Configure email separately.
10. Complete billing and handover.

## Gates

PREVIEW_READY is independent of DOMAIN_READY, EMAIL_READY and HOST_READY.

A DNS, mailbox, registrar or internal infrastructure issue must not prevent delivery of PREVIEW_READY.

## Target

A normal brochure/business site should be reviewable within minutes of receiving a sufficiently complete brief. Complex applications remain a separate engineering track.

## Product principle

Build the production engine once; reuse it across customers. Custom work changes content, branding and approved components—not the underlying deployment machinery.
