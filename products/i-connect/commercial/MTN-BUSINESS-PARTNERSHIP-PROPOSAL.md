# IZAKHONO GLOBAL × MTN Business — Partnership Discussion Proposal

**Status:** Draft for exploratory discussion; not an appointment, offer acceptance or existing partnership  
**Proposed counterpart:** MTN Business / relevant enterprise, SME, wholesale or channel team  
**Proponent:** IZAKHONO Infrastructure and I-CONNECT

## Executive proposition

IZAKHONO proposes exploring a non-exclusive commercial and technology relationship with MTN Business to help South African SMEs and enterprises adopt managed digital services alongside connectivity.

IZAKHONO is not seeking to duplicate MTN's mobile network or enterprise connectivity portfolio. We propose to complement it with an independent customer and business-technology layer: customer onboarding, service orchestration, business software, hosting through IZAKHONO HOST where production-ready, security through FORTRESS, managed infrastructure, payment integrations through I-PAY when approved, and consolidated service operations through I-CONNECT.

## What IZAKHONO contributes

- I-CONNECT customer, reseller and enterprise operations platform.
- IZAKHONO Infrastructure and owned-first HOST architecture, subject to production acceptance.
- Business software and workflow integration capabilities.
- FORTRESS identity, access, audit and security controls.
- PROVISIONER typed job orchestration and reconciliation.
- SME onboarding, implementation and support workflows.
- Provider-neutral architecture that can support multiple networks.

## Potential cooperation

1. SME distribution and referral programmes.
2. Approved reseller or wholesale connectivity arrangements.
3. Joint SME digital adoption packages.
4. API integration and service-status/order workflows.
5. Enterprise implementation and subcontracting.
6. Managed infrastructure and business application services.
7. A controlled pilot for a mutually agreed customer segment.

## Proposed pilot

A limited South African SME cohort, selected jointly, with clearly defined products, customer consent, pricebook, fulfilment process, support allocation, escalation and success measures. The pilot would start with software and business services that are ready to deliver. Any MTN connectivity product would be offered only under an executed agreement and approved serviceability/ordering process.

## Requested discussion

We request an exploratory meeting to understand:
- suitable wholesale, channel, referral or integration model;
- product and geographic availability;
- commercial terms, wholesale pricebook and settlement;
- API, sandbox and certification requirements;
- customer ownership, branding and data responsibilities;
- support, SLA and fault escalation;
- compliance and regulatory allocation;
- pilot approval process and decision owners.

## Partnership principles

The relationship should be non-exclusive unless both parties later agree otherwise. IZAKHONO retains ownership of its software, customer experience, platform IP and provider-neutral orchestration. MTN retains its network, service and supplier responsibilities. Customer-facing descriptions will accurately identify the underlying service provider and service terms.

No MTN appointment, approval, integration, coverage or pricing is represented as existing.

## Next step

Nominate the appropriate MTN Business contact and arrange a discovery session. Following the session, both parties can decide whether to proceed to due diligence, NDA, technical workshop and a pilot term sheet.
