# IZAKHONO GLOBAL × Telkom Business — Partnership Discussion Proposal

**Status:** Draft for exploratory discussion; not an appointment, offer acceptance or existing partnership  
**Proposed counterpart:** Telkom Business / BCX / Openserve relevant enterprise, wholesale or channel team  
**Proponent:** IZAKHONO Infrastructure and I-CONNECT

## Executive proposition

IZAKHONO proposes exploring a non-exclusive commercial and technology relationship with Telkom Business and relevant group businesses to help South African SMEs, multi-site organisations and enterprises improve their digital operations using connectivity-enabled software and managed infrastructure.

IZAKHONO is not seeking to duplicate Telkom's fibre, mobile or enterprise network assets. We propose to complement those assets with an independent customer and business-technology layer: service orchestration, business applications, integration, hosting through IZAKHONO HOST where production-ready, security through FORTRESS, managed infrastructure and customer operations through I-CONNECT.

## What IZAKHONO contributes

- I-CONNECT customer, reseller and enterprise operations platform.
- IZAKHONO Infrastructure and owned-first HOST architecture, subject to production acceptance.
- SME and enterprise application engineering and integration.
- FORTRESS identity, access, audit and security controls.
- PROVISIONER typed job orchestration and reconciliation.
- Multi-site service inventory, customer onboarding and support workflows.
- Provider-neutral architecture that avoids single-network lock-in.

## Potential cooperation

1. Wholesale fibre and business connectivity arrangements through the appropriate group channel.
2. SME distribution and referral programmes.
3. Joint multi-site digital transformation packages.
4. API integration for serviceability, order status, provisioning and fault workflows.
5. Enterprise implementation, subcontracting and managed-service opportunities.
6. Hosting, cloud, security and business application integration.
7. A controlled South African SME or multi-site pilot.

## Proposed pilot

A small cohort of businesses or multi-site organisations with defined service locations, requirements, provider pricebook, serviceability checks, installation responsibility, support allocation and escalation. The pilot would begin with software and infrastructure services IZAKHONO can demonstrably deliver. Any Telkom/Openserve/BCX service is offered only under the relevant executed agreement and verified serviceability and fulfilment process.

## Requested discussion

We request an exploratory meeting to understand:
- the appropriate Telkom Business, BCX or Openserve contracting route;
- wholesale/reseller/subcontracting eligibility;
- fibre and enterprise product availability and serviceability;
- API, sandbox and integration opportunities;
- pricing, settlement and commercial margin;
- customer ownership, branding and data responsibilities;
- installation, fault escalation, SLA and support allocation;
- compliance, procurement and regulatory requirements;
- pilot approval process and decision owners.

## Partnership principles

The relationship should be non-exclusive unless otherwise agreed. IZAKHONO retains ownership of its software, customer experience, platform IP and provider-neutral orchestration. The relevant Telkom group entity retains its own network, service and supplier responsibilities. Quotes and customer terms identify the actual service provider and distinguish connectivity charges from IZAKHONO software and managed-service fees.

No Telkom, BCX or Openserve appointment, approval, integration, coverage or pricing is represented as existing.

## Next step

Nominate the appropriate commercial or technology partnership contact and arrange a discovery session. Following the session, both parties can decide whether to proceed to due diligence, NDA, technical workshop and a pilot term sheet.
