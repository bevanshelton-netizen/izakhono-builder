## Commercial engagement rules

1. Every engagement remains exploratory until a signed agreement and required approvals exist.
2. No exclusivity, minimum volume, deposit, spend, reseller appointment, wholesale commitment or customer-data transfer is implied by correspondence.
3. IZAKHONO retains its IP, software, platform architecture and customer relationship subject to negotiated contracts and applicable law.
4. Regulated acquiring, settlement, custody, e-money and payment-system activities remain with appropriately authorised providers unless IZAKHONO separately obtains the required permissions.
5. Do not disclose FORTRESS source code, scoring logic, detection methods, credentials, private architecture or sensitive security controls in initial outreach. Use an appropriate NDA and controlled technical disclosure process.
6. Record inbound replies, named owners, meeting dates, agreed actions and evidence. Never convert a sent email into a claimed partnership.
7. All partner propositions are non-exclusive by default and require commercial, legal, security, privacy and operational review before commitment.

## Pilot qualification checklist

- [ ] Named commercial sponsor and technical owner
- [ ] Written problem statement and target customer segment
- [ ] Partner role and IZAKHONO role clearly separated
- [ ] Technical integration and data-flow review
- [ ] Regulatory perimeter and responsibility assessment
- [ ] POPIA, consent, security and fraud controls
- [ ] Commercial model, cost validation and margin approval
- [ ] Support model, service levels and escalation path
- [ ] Pilot cohort, success measures, duration and exit criteria
- [ ] Signed agreement and required onboarding/approvals
- [ ] Sandbox and end-to-end test evidence before live activity
