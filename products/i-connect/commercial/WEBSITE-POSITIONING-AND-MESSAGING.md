## Core value propositions

### One place to manage your digital services
Bring customer accounts, service requests, orders, support and service status into a coordinated business experience.

### Infrastructure built around your business
Access hosting, runtime, backup, resilience and managed infrastructure services through IZAKHONO Infrastructure, subject to service availability and agreed scope.

### Connectivity without unnecessary lock-in
We design for provider choice. Where approved provider arrangements exist, we help customers compare and manage suitable connectivity services.

### Business technology that works together
Connect your digital services with business applications, automation, payments and customer operations through supported integrations.

### Security and accountability by design
FORTRESS-aligned identity, access, audit and governance controls are applied according to each service's readiness and contracted scope.

## Solution pages

### Small business
A practical digital foundation for growing businesses: business applications, customer workflows, managed website and hosting options, support and connectivity coordination.

### Multi-site business
A coordinated view of branches, services, support requests, connectivity providers and operational status. Availability and failover depend on contracted services and verified site conditions.

### Enterprise and institutions
Architecture, software integration, managed infrastructure, security improvement and service operations delivered through scoped engagements, governance and acceptance criteria.

### Connectivity partners
We work to build non-exclusive relationships with network operators, fibre wholesalers, ISPs and technology providers. Provider services are offered only where commercial, technical and regulatory approval is in place.

## How it works

1. **Discover:** We understand your organisation, sites, users and business outcomes.
2. **Design:** We identify suitable services, dependencies, risks and costs.
3. **Connect:** We coordinate approved providers and supported integrations.
4. **Manage:** You use I-CONNECT for the service workflows and visibility that are available under your plan.
5. **Improve:** We review performance, support and opportunities as your organisation grows.

## Trust and transparency

- We identify the provider responsible for third-party connectivity.
- We confirm serviceability before accepting provider-dependent orders.
- We distinguish quoted, ordered, provisioning and active service states.
- We publish only approved prices and service terms.
- We do not claim network coverage, uptime, security certification or regulatory approval without evidence.
- We explain which services are live, in pilot or not yet available.
