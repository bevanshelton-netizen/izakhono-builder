# IZAKHONO GLOBAL / I-CONNECT — Service Catalogue & Pricebook Draft

**Status:** Internal pricing hypotheses; not approved public tariffs or provider quotations  
**Currency:** ZAR; VAT treatment to be confirmed per legal entity and service  
**Rule:** Separate software fees, professional services, infrastructure consumption and connectivity pass-through charges.

## 1. Pricing principles

- Do not subsidise wholesale connectivity from software subscription revenue without an approved margin model.
- Quote connectivity only from a current provider pricebook and verified serviceability.
- Show once-off setup, recurring fees, usage charges, equipment, installation, taxes and cancellation terms separately.
- No unlimited data, speed, coverage, uptime or response-time promise unless supplier terms and operations support it.
- Store money in integer cents in software and accounting records.
- All indicative ranges below require cost validation, tax review, market research and management approval before publication.

## 4. Connectivity pass-through

Every quote records:
- supplier and wholesale SKU;
- site/address serviceability result and timestamp;
- access type and speed tier as confirmed by supplier;
- installation and equipment fees;
- recurring supplier cost;
- usage/overage terms;
- contract length and cancellation charges;
- support ownership and fault escalation;
- IZAKHONO management/reseller fee;
- tax and customer total.

The quote identifies the underlying supplier where legally/contractually required and distinguishes supplier charges from IZAKHONO fees.

## 5. Connectivity product families

- Fibre broadband: orderable only after address-level serviceability and wholesale agreement.
- Fixed wireless / LTE / 5G: orderable only after provider coverage, device, data and service terms are confirmed.
- Business internet: quote with SLA and contention terms only as contracted.
- Backup connectivity: failover requires compatible equipment and a tested route; two subscriptions do not imply automatic failover.
- Managed Wi-Fi: hardware, installation, configuration and support priced separately.
- Airtime/data bundles: require approved aggregator/operator contract, catalogue, pricebook and fulfilment certification.
- SIM/eSIM: requires operator/MVNE agreement, KYC/RICA allocation, secure fulfilment and verified activation.
- SIP/PBX/voice: requires service-provider agreement, numbering/interconnect/legal review and emergency calling disclosures.
- Satellite: provider, terminal, availability, geography, plan, installation and usage terms verified per location.

## 6. Infrastructure service families

- Managed website and domain operations through IZAKHONO HOST after production acceptance.
- Managed DNS and TLS only for domains and zones under documented authority.
- Business email only after mail delivery, reputation, security and support acceptance.
- Managed backup with defined scope, retention, encryption, restore objectives and successful restore evidence.
- Managed application hosting with capacity, isolation, monitoring, incident and recovery controls.
- Infrastructure assessment, migration and optimisation as scoped projects.

## 7. Margin model

For every SKU calculate:
- customer recurring revenue excluding applicable tax;
- provider recurring wholesale cost;
- payment processing cost;
- support and operations cost allocation;
- hosting/storage/bandwidth cost allocation;
- installation/equipment amortisation where applicable;
- commissions and reseller share;
- bad debt, refunds and expected credits;
- contribution margin in ZAR and percentage.

Do not approve a SKU solely on revenue. Require positive contribution margin or an explicitly approved strategic subsidy with expiry/review date.

## 8. Approval workflow

Product owner drafts SKU → Infrastructure validates capacity/cost → Provider owner verifies wholesale terms → Security/compliance validates controls and disclosures → Finance validates tax, settlement and margin → Commercial owner approves → Engineering publishes versioned pricebook → QA verifies quote and invoice calculations.

Every published pricebook has effective date, currency, tax treatment, version, approver and source evidence. Historical quotes retain their original pricebook version.

## 9. Public pricing disclaimer

All indicative figures are planning assumptions only. They are not an offer, supplier quote, guarantee of coverage or confirmation of service availability. Final prices and terms are issued only after eligibility, cost, tax, regulatory and operational checks.
