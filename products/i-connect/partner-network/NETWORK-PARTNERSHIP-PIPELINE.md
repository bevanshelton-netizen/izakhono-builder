# I-CONNECT Network Partnership Pipeline

## Objective
Build I-CONNECT as the customer-facing connectivity brand while using multiple wholesale/network partners for mobile, fibre, wireless and carrier connectivity. IZAKHONO retains the customer relationship, provisioning, identity, billing/product layer and platform IP.

## Priority targets

### 1. Cell C — MVNO
**Role:** Mobile voice/data/SIM/eSIM infrastructure.
**Why:** Cell C states that its MVNO model lets partners operate under their own brand, design tariffs and manage the customer relationship while Cell C provides technical fulfilment. Its FY2026 results report 5.713 million MVNO HLR subscribers and 80–85% estimated South African MVNO market share.
**Next action:** Submit I-CONNECT MVNO enquiry and request commercial/technical onboarding pack, minimum commitments, wholesale rates, API/SIM/eSIM capabilities, KYC requirements and launch timeline.
**Public route:** Cell C MVNO information page / official enquiry route.

### 2. Afrinet — White-label ISP
**Role:** Rapid launch path for fibre/LTE/5G and ISP services.
**Next action:** Obtain partner pricing, provisioning/API documentation, reseller agreement, service catalogue and launch requirements.

### 3. Openserve — Fibre
**Role:** National wholesale fibre access.
**Next action:** Request ISP/reseller onboarding, wholesale pricing, API/provisioning access, SLA and coverage data.

### 4. WIRUlink — White-label wireless/fibre
**Role:** Fixed wireless, fibre and Layer-2 connectivity.
**Why:** WIRUlink explicitly offers Open Access and Authorised Reseller programmes, including white-label services, wholesale Layer-2 pricing and customer billing under the partner brand.
**Contact:** sales@wiru.co.za
**Next action:** Apply for Open Access Partner status and request wholesale catalogue, coverage, SLAs, installation model and API/provisioning options.

### 5. Konekt — Network aggregation
**Role:** Multi-network aggregation to reduce dependency on a single FNO/network.
**Next action:** Request wholesale aggregation proposal, coverage matrix, API/provisioning capabilities, commercial model and SLA.

### 6. WECOM — Carrier/backbone
**Role:** IP Transit, Layer 2, carrier Ethernet, national connectivity, metro/long-haul, peering, datacentre interconnect and white-label virtual ISP services.
**Contact:** enquiries@wecom.co.za
**Next action:** Request carrier/virtual ISP proposal, POP map, pricing, transit/peering options, SLAs and onboarding requirements.

## Commercial architecture
I-CONNECT → IZAKHONO provisioning/billing/identity → multi-provider orchestration → mobile/fibre/wireless/carrier networks.

## Partner selection gates
1. Wholesale economics
2. White-label rights
3. API/provisioning access
4. Coverage
5. SLA and NOC support
6. Customer ownership
7. Portability/exit rights
8. KYC/regulatory responsibilities
9. Minimum volume/commitment
10. Technical integration effort

## Rule
No partner is represented as signed, approved, integrated or live until documentary and technical evidence exists.
