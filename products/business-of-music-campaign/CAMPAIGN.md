Generate qualified musician traffic and convert it into R149 eBook purchases without paid advertising.

**DON'T SIGN BLIND.** Your talent creates the music. Your knowledge protects the business.

Social content → 60-second Music Business Test → campaign landing page → R149 checkout → digital delivery.

Independent musicians, emerging artists, songwriters, producers, DJs, managers and music students.

1. Contract fear: “Would you know what you are giving away?”

3. Ownership: “Who owns the master? Who controls publishing?”
4. Career: “Talent is not a business plan.”
5. Catalogue: “Your songs can become assets.”
6. Education: one practical business question per post.

## Organic execution
- Repurpose each pillar into Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn posts.
- Use short vertical videos with one question, one consequence and one CTA.
- Ask musicians to comment with their answer before revealing the lesson.
- Encourage artists to share the test with bandmates and collaborators.
- No paid advertising until confirmed organic sales demonstrate conversion.

## Measurement
Track landing-page visits, test completions, checkout clicks, confirmed purchases and confirmed revenue. Never count clicks or payment attempts as sales.
