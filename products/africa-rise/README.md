# AFRICA RISE microsite

A responsive, dependency-free public-facing microsite for the AFRICA RISE initiative.

## Contents
- `index.html` — responsive landing page with mission, five programmes, operating principles, expansion roadmap and governance commitments.
- No build step or third-party runtime dependency is required for local preview.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a modern browser or serve this directory with any static HTTP server.

## Current status
This is a built preview artifact in the IZAKHONO Builder repository. It is **not yet deployed or publicly launched**. No domain, DNS, contact form, mailing list, analytics, CRM or partner-intake backend is configured by this artifact.

## Launch gates
Before public launch:
1. Approve the AFRICA RISE identity, wording and relationship to IZAKHONO GLOBAL.
2. Confirm the legal entity or entities responsible for the initiative and programme activities.
3. Approve a monitored partner-enquiry channel and privacy notice before collecting personal information.
4. Confirm domain ownership, hosting target, DNS, TLS, backups and rollback plan.
5. Test mobile, accessibility, browser compatibility, performance and security.
6. Confirm public claims, programme status and contact details against evidence.

## Design principles
- Premium, editorial, Africa-forward visual language.
- Responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
- No invented statistics, endorsements, partner logos, public contact details or operational claims.
- No non-functional lead form pretending to submit data.
- Clear distinction between strategic ambition, pilots under preparation and operational services.


## Preview route

The branch preview exposes the microsite at `/africa-rise/`. This is a non-production preview route; no public domain, customer enquiry collection or launch claim is enabled.
