Music is an art. Music is also a business.

A serious career requires more than a hit. It requires ownership, contracts, cash-flow discipline, audience strategy, professional teams and a catalogue that can continue creating value.

What the book teaches

Rights & Royalties

Understand compositions, masters, publishing, registrations, royalty pathways, licensing and catalogue ownership.

Money & Wealth

Move beyond getting paid. Learn cash flow, budgeting, tax awareness, investment thinking, margins and asset-building.

Contracts & Deals

Understand record deals, distribution, management, publishing, sponsorships, touring, licensing and exit provisions.

Brand & Audience

Build an artist brand people recognise, develop an audience and turn attention into sustainable commercial relationships.

Protection

Reduce exposure to fraud, weak administration, unclear ownership, cyber risks and poorly documented relationships.

Legacy

Build a catalogue and business that can survive beyond a single release — and eventually beyond the artist.
