## Commercial rule
Do NOT present a school-specific crest/logo as an official supplier relationship unless the school has authorized the use. Until then, advertisements should be labelled:
“SAMPLE CONCEPT — NOT OFFICIAL SCHOOL MERCHANDISE”
and use the school's verified colourway/uniform silhouette, or a neutral placeholder badge.

## Production pipeline
1. Verify school identity.
2. Verify current uniform rules.
3. Verify current colours from the strongest available source.
4. Inspect crest/logo from a public source.
5. Create a clean vector-style reference internally.
6. Create blazer/jersey/socks/bag/tie mockups.
7. Add “SAMPLE / NOT OFFICIAL” watermark for prospecting.
8. Build school-specific bulk quotation CTA.
9. Contact school / governing body / approved supplier for authorization.
10. Replace SAMPLE treatment with approved branding only after authorization.
