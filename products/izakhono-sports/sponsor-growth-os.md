# Sponsor Growth OS

## The proposition

**We do not sell sponsors exposure. We help sponsors grow.**

IZAKHONO SPORTS turns sponsorship from a logo-placement expense into a measurable growth system.

### Sponsor journey

`Discover → Match → Contract → Activate → Measure → Optimise → Renew → Expand`

## Sponsor command centre

### 1. Discover
- searchable sports properties
- audience profiles
- geographic/community reach
- athlete and team profiles
- competition calendars
- available inventory
- indicative commercial value

### 2. Match
A matching engine scores opportunities against sponsor objectives:

- target market
- geography
- age/life stage
- sport affinity
- community impact
- brand category
- budget
- campaign timing

### 3. Activate
Every campaign becomes a structured set of deliverables:

- venue presence
- digital content
- athlete/creator activation
- community programmes
- tickets/hospitality
- competitions
- broadcasts/media
- fan offers
- product trials

### 4. Prove
Evidence is attached to each activation. Examples:

- verified attendance
- content publication
- engagement
- clicks/leads
- offer redemptions
- registrations
- community participation
- media delivery
- contracted deliverables completed

### 5. Grow
The system recommends:

- underperforming activations to fix;
- high-performing properties to expand;
- adjacent sports/audiences to test;
- renewal opportunities;
- cross-sport campaigns;
- community impact opportunities.

## Sponsor ROI framework

Every campaign can report:

**Investment**
- contracted value
- activation cost
- total spend

**Reach**
- verified audience
- impressions/views where available
- attendance

**Engagement**
- interactions
- content engagement
- participation

**Commercial outcomes**
- leads
- conversions
- offer redemptions
- attributable revenue where evidence permits

**Brand/community outcomes**
- sentiment measures where available
- youth participation
- community reach
- development outcomes

## Marketplace economics

IZAKHONO SPORTS can charge the sports side through subscriptions and the commercial side through sponsor analytics and marketplace services. Where contractually and legally appropriate, the platform can earn a commission on sponsorship transactions.

The objective is alignment: **when sport grows, IZAKHONO grows; when sponsors grow, sport gains stronger commercial demand.**

## Trust rule

The platform must distinguish:

- reported metric;
- measured metric;
- verified metric;
- attributed outcome.

It must never turn an estimate into a claimed fact.
