# IZAKHONO SPORTS Sponsorship Marketplace v2

## Purpose
Turn sporting inventory into a measurable, searchable and transact-able global sponsorship marketplace.

## Marketplace flow
DISCOVER → MATCH → SHORTLIST → PROPOSE → NEGOTIATE → CONTRACT → ACTIVATE → MEASURE → RENEW → EXPAND

## Inventory
Every sponsorship opportunity can be attached to a sport, competition, team, athlete or media property and can describe:
- audience segments
- geography
- objectives
- asking value and currency
- availability window
- deliverables
- measurement requirements

## Matching
The existing sponsor matcher scores sport, geography, objectives, audience and budget fit. Marketplace v2 treats that score as discovery assistance, not a contractual promise.

## Commercial guardrails
1. Inventory ownership must be organisation-scoped.
2. Sponsor campaigns and activations must remain auditable.
3. Financial commitments must flow through the provider-neutral billing boundary.
4. Activation evidence must be captured before performance claims are made.
5. Marketplace ranking must never imply guaranteed sponsorship placement.

## Revenue model
- transaction commission
- campaign management fees
- premium sponsor intelligence
- enterprise/federation marketplace contracts
- measurement and reporting services

## Product surfaces
- Sponsor marketplace
- Organisation inventory manager
- Sponsor campaign workspace
- Activation tracker
- Measurement dashboard
- Sponsor ROI reporting
