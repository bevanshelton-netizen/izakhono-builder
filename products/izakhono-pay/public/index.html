How it works

One controlled payment lifecycle.

01 · IntegrateMerchant creates a payment intent through the IZAKHONO PAY API.
02 · RouteEligibility and risk policy select an approved provider and payment method.
03 · VerifySigned provider events and independent status checks confirm payment.
04 · ReconcileMerchant operations track transaction evidence, refunds and settlement records.
Honest availability: the current Alpha checkout is ZAR-only. International currencies, additional countries and payment methods are roadmap capabilities, not live offers. Live-money processing remains disabled until production infrastructure, provider approval, legal/compliance and end-to-end acceptance gates pass.
IZAKHONO PAY is payment orchestration software, not a bank or a custodian of funds. Regulated payment processing and settlement are performed by approved providers. Availability depends on merchant eligibility, provider contracts and applicable law. © IZAKHONO.
