Business and contact information may be provided when a prospective merchant communicates with us or submits onboarding information. This can include business name, contact details, website information and information required for verification and compliance.

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess merchant applications, perform onboarding and verification, provide requested services, maintain records, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal or compliance obligations.

IZAKHONO PAY is designed to minimise exposure to sensitive payment credentials. Payment-card information should not be submitted through this public website. Where a payment provider is used, payment data is handled through the applicable provider's secure payment environment and terms.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Third parties

Where payment, hosting, verification or other services require third-party providers, information may be processed by those providers subject to their applicable terms, privacy notices and legal obligations.

Updates

This notice may be updated as IZAKHONO PAY moves from development into controlled merchant activation and production operation.
