WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

A single payment layer for growing businesses.

IZAKHONO PAY is designed as a technology layer between merchants and authorised payment rails. Businesses will be able to work with one merchant-facing integration while payment providers and regulated institutions perform the applicable payment and settlement functions.

01

Online Checkout

Merchant checkout experiences designed for websites, applications and digital commerce.

02

Payment Links

Create payment requests that businesses can share through digital channels such as messaging, email and social platforms.

03

Merchant APIs

Developer-facing APIs for payment intents, transaction references, status handling and integrations.

04

Reconciliation

Transaction records, merchant references, ledger events and reporting designed to make payment activity easier to reconcile.

05

Provider Routing

A technology control layer intended to connect approved payment providers through replaceable provider adapters.

06

Business Payments

Infrastructure intended for retailers, service businesses, digital businesses, marketplaces and other approved merchants.

TECHNOLOGY

Technology owned by IZAKHONO. Payment rails remain properly controlled.

Our architecture separates the merchant-facing technology from regulated movement of funds. Provider adapters are designed to be replaceable, allowing the platform to support multiple approved rails without forcing merchants to rebuild their integration.

COMPLIANCE & RESPONSIBLE LAUNCH

We will not represent a development platform as a licensed payment institution.

IZAKHONO PAY is currently in a controlled development and testing phase. The platform is not being presented on this website as an independently authorised payment institution, bank, acquirer or settlement institution.

Merchant verification

Merchants will be subject to onboarding and applicable KYB/KYC, risk and compliance checks before live activation.

Regulated partners

During the initial operating model, applicable payment processing and settlement functions are intended to be provided through authorised banks, acquirers and payment service partners.

Fail-closed live processing

Production payment processing remains disabled until the required provider credentials, compliance controls, security verification and operational arrangements are complete.

Data protection

The platform is designed to minimise sensitive payment-data exposure and to keep payment credentials and card data outside IZAKHONO systems wherever the selected payment rail supports that model.

Important: Availability of any particular payment method, provider or settlement service is subject to eligibility, commercial agreement, technical integration and applicable law.
