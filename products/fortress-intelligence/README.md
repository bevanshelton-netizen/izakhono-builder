# IZAKHONO FORTRESS INTELLIGENCE™

**Business intelligence, screening and relationship-risk platform.**

## Product promise

Search a person or organisation once and turn fragmented public-record signals into an explainable relationship and risk view.

### Core modules

- Person/company search
- Sanctions screening
- PEP screening
- Adverse-media signal intake
- Ownership and control relationships
- Linked-company/entity graph
- Risk scoring with reason codes
- Supplier/customer due diligence
- Procurement screening
- Case management and audit trail
- Watchlists and re-screening jobs
- API-ready architecture

## Important boundary

This product is an original IZAKHONO implementation. It does **not** copy OpenSanctions source code, branding, proprietary datasets, or UI. External data must be used only under its applicable licence/terms. Screening results are signals for due diligence, not a declaration that a person or organisation is guilty of wrongdoing.

## MVP in this repository

`demo/` contains a dependency-free browser prototype using clearly labelled synthetic records. It demonstrates the product interaction without making live sanctions claims.

## Production roadmap

1. Identity resolution and fuzzy matching
2. Licensed/approved data connectors
3. Evidence provenance for every signal
4. Entity/relationship graph storage
5. Case management and approvals
6. Scheduled re-screening
7. Tenant isolation, RBAC and audit logs
8. API keys, quotas and usage metering
9. South African/African data-source adapters
10. Security, privacy and legal/compliance review before commercial launch

## Positioning

IZAKHONO FORTRESS INTELLIGENCE™ is designed to help organisations understand who they are dealing with, what relationships matter, and which records require further human review.
