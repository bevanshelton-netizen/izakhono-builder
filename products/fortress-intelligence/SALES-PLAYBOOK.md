## Objection handling

**“We already have screening.”**

FORTRESS is not positioned as a forced replacement. It can be the relationship/evidence/workflow layer around existing providers and can route screening to approved providers.

**“We cannot send sensitive data to another vendor.”**

Offer the on-premise/private-runtime architecture where licensing permits it, minimise fields sent to external providers, and document data flows before production.

**“We need regulatory assurance.”**

FORTRESS is a technology platform, not a regulator or legal opinion. Customer compliance responsibility remains with the accountable institution. Production deployments require legal, privacy, security and compliance review.
