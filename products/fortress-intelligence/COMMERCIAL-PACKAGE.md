### Buyer pain
Information is fragmented across company records, sanctions lists, ownership records, media and internal files. Teams lose time searching separately and struggle to explain why a case was escalated.

### Product answer
One search → one entity profile → connected relationships → source evidence → review workflow → auditable decision.
