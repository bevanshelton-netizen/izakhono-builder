# FORTRESS INTELLIGENCE™ — 30-DAY PILOT SOW

> Commercial template for review and adaptation. This is not legal advice and should be reviewed before signature.

## 1. Purpose

IZAKHONO will provide a controlled pilot of FORTRESS INTELLIGENCE™ to evaluate a customer's supplier, customer or counterparty due-diligence workflow.

## 2. Pilot scope

- Duration: 30 days
- Business unit: one agreed customer business unit
- Users: up to 5 named users unless otherwise agreed
- Screening volume: defined in the order form
- Synthetic demonstration environment followed by approved production data only when the applicable provider, privacy, security and customer approvals are complete
- Relationship intelligence and review workflow
- Case notes and audit history
- Pilot review meeting and executive findings report

## 3. Customer responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for its regulatory obligations, lawful processing basis, internal approval decisions, source-data permissions and final onboarding/rejection decisions.

The customer must provide authorised users, agreed test cases, required policies and timely feedback.

## 4. IZAKHONO responsibilities

IZAKHONO will provide onboarding, configuration, agreed product access, pilot support, issue tracking and a closing review.

IZAKHONO will not represent a provider signal as proof of criminality, misconduct or guilt. Production results will include provenance and review controls appropriate to the agreed implementation.

## 5. Data and confidentiality

The parties must agree the permitted data, processing purposes, retention period, security controls and any processor/operator obligations before personal or confidential production data is introduced.

No provider dataset may be redistributed outside the rights granted by its applicable licence or agreement.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

The pilot is successful when the parties have evaluated:

1. Search and entity resolution
2. Evidence/provenance visibility
3. Relationship discovery
4. Review queue usability
5. Case/audit workflow
6. Monitoring/re-screening concept where enabled
7. Operational fit and measurable time savings

## 7. Commercial conversion

At pilot close, the parties may agree an annual subscription, enterprise contract or API arrangement based on seats, screening volume, monitored entities, integrations and support requirements.

## 8. No automatic production commitment

A pilot does not create a production deployment obligation. Production access remains subject to licensing, privacy, security, legal/compliance review and a signed commercial agreement.
