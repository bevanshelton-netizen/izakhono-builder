# ExamReady language policy v1

- Store canonical syllabus concepts separately from translations.
- Keep official examination language(s) explicit per qualification pack.
- Let the learner select a preferred teaching language independently.
- Preserve official terms alongside localized explanations.
- Never silently machine-translate a high-stakes legal, medical, financial or safety term and present it as authoritative.
- Support human/expert review status per localized asset.
- Fall back visibly to the canonical verified language when a localized explanation is unavailable or unreviewed.
- Support Unicode and bidirectional/RTL rendering at the UI layer.
- Captions and transcripts are required for published tutor videos where technically available.
- Language support claims must reflect tested capability, not theoretical model coverage.
