# IZAKHONO ExamReady™ — Core v1

**PREPARE FOR THE BIG TIME.**

ExamReady is the reusable global examination-preparation engine for IZAKHONO. FAISReady remains a specialist product and is integrated as the first live qualification family rather than replaced.

## Permanent product loop

Diagnose → teach → practise → detect misconception → explain on demand → tutor video → ONE AI Q&A → mini-test → mastery check → readiness score → next mission.

## Global data contract

Every qualification pack is versioned by country/jurisdiction, profession, awarding/exam body, qualification/exam, syllabus version, official exam languages, learning languages, objectives, review date and legal/trademark notes.

Third-party qualifications are always described as **preparation / study support** unless IZAKHONO has separately verified authorization. Completion certificates certify only the IZAKHONO preparation programme; they never claim to award an external professional credential.

## Language engine

Learning language and examination language are independent. The learner may study a concept in a supported preferred language and practise official terminology in the actual examination language. Unicode, RTL, captions, transcripts, speech hooks and terminology glossaries are core requirements. High-stakes terminology must support expert review.

## Tutor-on-Demand

Every topic may expose:
- Explain this
- Simplify it
- Show a worked example
- Explain visually
- Teach me from scratch
- Ask ONE AI
- Watch tutor video
- Retest me

Tutor video metadata is separate from the syllabus so professionally recorded, localized, dubbed, captioned or AI-presenter explanations can be swapped without changing progress records.

## Readiness intelligence

Readiness is evidence-based, derived from diagnostic performance, topic mastery, recency, mock performance and unresolved weak areas. It is a preparation indicator, not a guarantee of passing an external examination.

## Initial vertical catalogue

- FAISReady — RE1, RE3, RE4, RE5 preparation
- FinanceReady — accounting, investment and finance preparation packs
- TechReady — cloud, networking and IT certification preparation packs
- CyberReady — cybersecurity preparation packs
- ProjectReady — project-management preparation packs
- Financial Planning — candidate study support, subject to current professional-body and trademark requirements

New packs are configuration/content additions to the shared engine, not new applications.

## Architecture

Owned-first and externally reversible:
ONE AI interface → IZAKHONO AI Gateway → ONE AI Engine → owned model workers.
External inference/hosting may be used as reversible resilience/overflow and must not control the owned engine.

ExamReady integrates with IZAKHONO CRM/App Fabric using strict entity/platform isolation. Payments use the approved platform payment gateway only after verified payment → receipt → entitlement acceptance.

No telemetry, behavioural profiling, advertising IDs or silent tracking.

4. Tutor-on-Demand lesson/video contract.
5. Preferred-learning-language plus official-exam-language model.
