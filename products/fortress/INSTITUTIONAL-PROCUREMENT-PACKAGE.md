# FORTRESS Institutional Procurement Package

## Executive proposition

FORTRESS is IZAKHONO's financial-crime defence and transaction-intelligence platform. It provides a controlled intelligence layer across identity, device, behaviour, transaction, scam, network, AML and investigation signals.

FORTRESS is designed to complement regulated banks, payment institutions, insurers, merchants, enterprises and public-sector systems — not replace their regulated functions.

## Why now

South Africa's payment-system modernisation explicitly includes intelligence and fraud management, interoperability and trusted digital credentials. SARB has also strengthened the regulatory and supervisory architecture of the national payment system in 2026. FORTRESS is positioned as infrastructure that helps institutions turn those priorities into measurable operational capability.

## Institutional pilot

### Standard 90-day engagement

**Phase 1 — Discover (Weeks 1–2)**
- Define fraud/scam/AML use cases
- Establish baseline metrics
- Map permitted data sources and privacy controls
- Agree governance and success criteria

**Phase 2 — Integrate (Weeks 3–6)**
- Connect approved event/data feeds
- Configure policies and reason codes
- Establish analyst workflow and case routing
- Validate security and access controls

**Phase 3 — Prove (Weeks 7–10)**
- Controlled replay and/or sandbox evaluation
- Measure detection lift
- Measure false-positive rate
- Measure decision and investigation latency
- Identify network/mule patterns where lawful and evidentially supported

**Phase 4 — Executive Review (Weeks 11–12)**
- Evidence report
- ROI/loss-avoidance model
- Production-readiness assessment
- Expansion recommendation

FORTRESS should be evaluated on:

- measurable fraud/scam detection improvement
- reduction in avoidable losses
- false-positive control
- decision latency
- investigation productivity
- network intelligence
- explainability
- auditability
- resilience
- security architecture
- privacy/data minimisation
- integration effort
- operational scalability

## Regulatory boundary

FORTRESS technology does not itself constitute banking, payment, clearing, settlement, custody or other regulated authorisation. FORTRESS must not independently transfer, custody, freeze, recover or settle customer funds. Where financial action is required, the authorised institution/provider remains responsible for execution under applicable law, policy and contract.

## Executive message

> **Cybercrime should not be allowed to remain a cost of doing business. FORTRESS turns fragmented security signals into an institutional decision and intelligence layer designed to make financial crime harder to execute and harder to monetise.**

## Institutional call to action

Select one high-value fraud/scam/financial-crime problem. Give FORTRESS a controlled evidence environment. Establish the baseline. Run the pilot. Measure the outcome. If the evidence demonstrates material improvement, expand institution-wide and then across the participating network.

The objective is not to replace the institution's systems. It is to make the institution's existing financial infrastructure materially harder to attack.

## Pilot safety standard

All initial pilots are controlled and sandbox-first. No live-money movement is enabled by this package. No production decisioning is represented as authorised until the institution's technical, legal, regulatory and operational gates have been satisfied.
