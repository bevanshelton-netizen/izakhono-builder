# FORTRESS™ R250M COMMERCIAL ENGINE

## Objective
Build FORTRESS into a recurring-revenue financial-crime defence platform capable of supporting a R250 million annual revenue ambition. This is a commercial target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## Revenue architecture

### 1. FORTRESS API
Risk decisioning, identity intelligence, transaction risk and network signals for payment and financial platforms.

Illustrative target: 20 institutions × R3.0M ARR = R60M ARR.

### 2. FORTRESS BANK
Enterprise deployment for banks and regulated financial institutions.

Illustrative target: 8 institutions × R10M ARR = R80M ARR.

### 3. FORTRESS MERCHANT
Fraud, scam and account-protection services for large merchant ecosystems and marketplaces.

Illustrative target: 100 enterprise merchants × R500k ARR = R50M ARR.

### 4. FORTRESS COMMAND
Fraud operations, investigations, case management, SLA, intelligence and analyst tooling.

Illustrative target: 10 enterprise deployments × R3M ARR = R30M ARR.

### 5. FORTRESS INTELLIGENCE
Threat intelligence, network analytics, assessments, managed intelligence and specialist services.

Illustrative target: R30M ARR across subscriptions and professional services.

**Illustrative total: R250M ARR.**

## Product moat
FORTRESS combines:
- identity risk;
- device intelligence;
- behavioural analytics;
- transaction risk;
- authorised-payment scam detection;
- network intelligence;
- threat graph analysis;
- AML monitoring support;
- investigation and case management;
- explainable decisions;
- operational SLA and escalation controls.

The strategic moat is the ability to correlate signals across the transaction lifecycle while maintaining privacy, lawful sharing, auditability and institutional control.

## Pricing principle
Use annual platform subscriptions plus usage-based API pricing, enterprise modules, implementation fees and managed intelligence services. Final pricing must be negotiated against customer scale, risk exposure, integration complexity, regulatory requirements and service levels.

## Non-negotiable controls
FORTRESS technology does not itself become a bank, payment institution, custodian or settlement operator merely by deploying these products. Regulated financial activity remains with appropriately authorized institutions and contracted providers until IZAKHONO obtains any required authorization.

FORTRESS must not autonomously move, custody, freeze, recover or settle customer funds. It may produce bounded risk decisions and communicate permitted actions to authorized systems under contract, law and policy.

## North-star metric
**Verified financial harm prevented or materially reduced per unit of FORTRESS revenue.**

Revenue growth must follow measurable customer protection, not the other way around.
