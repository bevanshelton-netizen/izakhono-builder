# FORTRESS Institutional Pilot Offer

## Purpose
A controlled 90-day pilot for banks, payment institutions, insurers, large enterprises and public-sector digital services.

## Pilot proposition
FORTRESS adds an intelligence and decision layer across identity, device, behaviour, transaction, scam, network and investigation signals without taking custody of customer funds.

## Pilot scope
- Identity and account-takeover risk
- Device and remote-control indicators
- Transaction and beneficiary risk
- Authorised-payment scam signals
- Network/relationship intelligence
- Case management and analyst workflow
- SLA and escalation monitoring
- Explainable decision and audit evidence

## 90-day structure
### Phase 1 — Discover (Weeks 1–2)
Define use cases, data boundaries, integration architecture, privacy requirements and baseline metrics.

### Phase 2 — Integrate (Weeks 3–6)
Connect approved synthetic, masked or contractually authorised data sources. Configure policies and decision contracts.

### Phase 3 — Prove (Weeks 7–10)
Run controlled scenarios and measured evaluation. No autonomous movement of funds.

### Phase 4 — Executive Review (Weeks 11–12)
Report detection lift, false-positive impact, decision latency, investigation productivity, network discoveries and recommended production architecture.

## Pilot success metrics
- Detection lift versus baseline
- False-positive rate
- Decision latency
- Investigation time reduction
- Analyst productivity
- Confirmed fraud/scam pattern detection
- Network/mule relationship discoveries
- Explainability and audit completeness

## Commercial model
Illustrative pilot fee: **R750,000 – R1,500,000** depending on scope, integration complexity and participating business units.

Production conversion is separately contracted and may include annual platform, API, intelligence, command-centre and usage components.

## Production boundary
FORTRESS is technology infrastructure. It does not independently custody, transfer, freeze, recover or settle customer funds. Any consequential financial action must remain with an authorised institution/provider under applicable law, policy and contract.
