# FORTRESS — EXECUTIVE INSTITUTIONAL OUTREACH PACKAGE

**Purpose:** Convert the FORTRESS institutional pilot proposition into executive conversations with banks, payment institutions and strategic ecosystem partners.

## 1. Executive proposition

**FORTRESS is a financial-crime defence and transaction-intelligence layer designed to make cybercrime harder to execute and harder to monetise.**

It brings together identity, device, behaviour, transaction, scam, network and case signals into an explainable decision layer that can recommend ALLOW, ALLOW_WITH_MONITORING, CHALLENGE, HOLD_FOR_REVIEW, BLOCK or ESCALATE.

FORTRESS does not independently custody, transfer, freeze, recover or settle customer funds. Consequential financial actions remain under the authority and controls of the regulated institution and its approved providers.

## 2. Why now

South Africa's reported digital banking crime losses reached R2.4 billion in 2025, with banking-app crime accounting for more than 70% of reported digital banking losses. The fraud problem is increasingly cross-channel: identity compromise, malware, social engineering, mule networks, beneficiary abuse and coordinated rings can intersect in the same incident.

SARB's Payments Ecosystem Modernisation programme is simultaneously moving the market toward more open, interoperable payments, stronger digital identity and a payments insights/fraud capability. FORTRESS is designed for this environment: institutions need a decision and intelligence layer that can operate across fragmented signals and institutional boundaries without becoming a closed-loop system.

## 3. First meeting objective

Do **not** attempt to sell a full enterprise deployment in the first meeting.

The objective is to secure agreement on one high-value fraud problem that can be measured in a controlled environment.

Recommended opening question:

> “If we could improve one fraud-control outcome in the next 90 days — before settlement, at beneficiary onboarding, during account takeover, or during scam intervention — which outcome would have the greatest value to your institution?”

FORTRESS should therefore approach Capitec as a potential complementary intelligence/pilot layer rather than pretending it is starting from zero.

## 7. The 30-minute executive meeting

**0–5 min — Context**
- FORTRESS proposition
- current fraud problem
- regulatory boundary

**5–12 min — Their problem**
- highest-loss fraud vectors
- current controls
- false positives
- investigation bottlenecks
- decision latency

**12–20 min — Demonstration**
Show one synthetic incident from:
identity → device → behaviour → transaction → network → risk score → reason codes → decision → case → SLA → audit.

**20–26 min — Pilot design**
Agree one use case, data boundary, success metrics and integration path.

**26–30 min — Next step**
Secure a technical discovery session and identify executive sponsor + technical owner + procurement path.

## 8. Discovery questions

1. Which fraud/scam category produces the highest avoidable loss today?
2. Where is the earliest point at which your institution can reliably intervene?
3. Which signals are currently siloed?
4. How quickly can your current controls make a decision?
5. Where are false positives creating customer or analyst friction?
6. How are beneficiary/mule/network relationships detected today?
7. What proportion of consequential alerts receive human investigation?
8. How long does a typical investigation take?
9. What evidence must an analyst retain for audit/regulatory purposes?
10. Which APIs/events/data feeds are available for a controlled pilot?
11. What approvals are required before a sandbox or production-like test can begin?
12. What metric would make the pilot an obvious success to the executive committee?

## 9. Pilot success metrics

Every pilot must establish a baseline before intervention.

Recommended metrics:
- fraud detection lift
- fraud-loss avoidance / prevented exposure
- scam intervention rate
- mule/network discovery rate
- account-takeover detection lift
- false-positive rate
- customer challenge rate
- decision latency
- investigation time
- analyst productivity
- case SLA adherence
- explainability/reason-code coverage
- audit completeness

## 12. Outreach rules

- Never claim a relationship that does not exist.
- Never send customer-sensitive or regulated data in first-contact outreach.
- Never represent FORTRESS as a bank, payment institution or regulator.
- Never promise fraud-loss reductions before measurement.
- Never bypass an institution's security, procurement or regulatory process.
- Always offer a controlled pilot rather than an uncontrolled production integration.
- Always preserve explainability, privacy, auditability and human oversight.
