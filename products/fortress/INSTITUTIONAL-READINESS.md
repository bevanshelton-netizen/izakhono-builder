# FORTRESS™ — Institutional Readiness Framework

## Purpose

FORTRESS is the IZAKHONO trust, cybersecurity and financial-crime defence layer. Its institutional objective is to make cybercrime materially harder to execute and monetize while giving authorised institutions stronger identity, transaction-risk, fraud-intelligence and response capabilities.

## Institutional posture

IZAKHONO does not ask banks, regulators, DFIs, enterprise partners or technology institutions to lower their standards. FORTRESS is designed to meet high standards through evidence, security controls, auditability, privacy safeguards, human oversight and regulatory discipline.

## Core capability pillars

1. FORTRESS ID — identity protection and account-risk signals.
2. FORTRESS DEVICE — device and session intelligence.
3. FORTRESS TRANSACTION — transaction-risk decisioning and behavioural anomaly detection.
4. FORTRESS SCAM SHIELD — authorised-payment and social-engineering risk controls.
5. FORTRESS FRAUD INTELLIGENCE — cross-entity pattern detection subject to lawful information-sharing arrangements.
6. FORTRESS AML — case management and financial-crime monitoring support.
7. FORTRESS MERCHANT — merchant fraud and account-abuse controls.
8. FORTRESS COMMAND — institutional investigation, alerting and response operations.
9. FORTRESS API — security infrastructure exposed through controlled APIs.
10. FORTRESS AI — decision support for anomaly, identity and fraud signals with human review and explainability controls.

## Decision model

Before a transaction: establish identity, device, behavioural, contextual, recipient and threat signals.

At decision time: allow, step-up, hold for review, decline or escalate according to configured risk policy and applicable law.

After the transaction: monitor, reconcile, investigate, generate alerts, support authorised recovery/recall processes and feed verified outcomes back into the intelligence layer.

## Trust principles

- Privacy by design.
- Data minimisation and purpose limitation.
- Lawful information sharing only.
- No secret or credential exposure.
- Strong authentication and least privilege.
- Immutable or tamper-evident audit evidence where appropriate.
- Human review for consequential decisions.
- Explainable risk outcomes where feasible.
- Fail-closed security controls.
- No claim of regulatory authorisation unless verified.
- Regulated money movement remains with authorised institutions and partners until IZAKHONO is appropriately authorised.

## Commercial model

FORTRESS can be commercialised through enterprise API licensing, institutional subscriptions, merchant protection, fraud-intelligence services, managed security operations, identity protection and transaction-risk services, subject to contractual, regulatory and data-protection requirements.

## Evidence standard

Every institutional claim should be traceable to architecture, implementation, test evidence, security review, operational evidence or independently verifiable external evidence. Marketing language must never outrun verified capability.

## Strategic objective

Build infrastructure that makes cybercrime dramatically harder to monetize and makes trusted digital commerce more attractive to institutions, merchants and consumers.
