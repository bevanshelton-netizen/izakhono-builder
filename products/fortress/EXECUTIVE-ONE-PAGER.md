# FORTRESS™ — EXECUTIVE ONE-PAGE

## The Trust & Financial-Crime Defence Network

**Cybercrime should not be allowed to remain a cost of doing business.**

FORTRESS is IZAKHONO's institutional financial-crime defence and transaction-intelligence platform. It brings fragmented signals across **identity, device, behaviour, transaction, scam, network and case intelligence** into an explainable decision layer for authorised institutions.

### The problem
Financial crime is increasingly fast, social and coordinated. South Africa's reported digital-banking crime losses reached **R2.4 billion in 2025**, with banking-app crime accounting for more than 70% of reported digital-banking losses. citeturn219133search0

The attack is no longer confined to a single transaction or a single compromised credential. Institutions must identify the relationship between identity compromise, device takeover, behavioural anomalies, new beneficiaries, mule activity, coordinated networks and authorised-payment scams.

**Identity + Device + Behaviour + Context + Transaction + Network Intelligence → Risk Score → Decision → Reason Codes → Action → Audit Evidence**

It can support institutional decisions including:

### The 90-day pilot
**Phase 1 — Discover | Weeks 1–2**
Select one high-value fraud problem, define baseline metrics, data boundaries and success criteria.

**Phase 2 — Integrate | Weeks 3–6**
Connect approved synthetic, sandbox or institution-authorised signals; configure rules and risk policies.

**Phase 3 — Prove | Weeks 7–10**
Run controlled scenarios and replay historical/synthetic patterns. Measure detection lift, false positives, latency, investigation productivity and network discovery.

**Phase 4 — Executive Review | Weeks 11–12**
Present measured outcomes, security/privacy evidence, operational findings and a production-readiness recommendation.

### What we measure
- Detection improvement
- Potential loss avoidance
- False-positive rate
- Decision latency
- Scam intervention opportunities
- Mule/network discovery
- Analyst productivity
- Investigation time
- Explainability and audit completeness

### Safety boundary
FORTRESS is technology infrastructure. It does **not** independently custody, transfer, freeze, recover or settle customer funds. Financial actions remain with authorised institutions/providers under applicable law, policy and integration permissions.

Initial pilots are sandbox-first and **live-money movement remains disabled** until all required institutional, technical and regulatory approvals and evidence are satisfied.
