# BUILD → MONEY CONTRACT

Every generated opportunity must have a monetisation path before it can enter the IZAKHONO App Factory.

## Required stages
1. Opportunity selected
2. Customer and problem defined
3. Revenue model selected
4. Pricing hypothesis created
5. MVP scope generated
6. Build handoff generated
7. Launch checklist generated
8. Acquisition plan generated
9. Retention/upsell path generated
10. Revenue metrics defined

## Default monetisation ladder
- Paid setup or activation
- Monthly subscription
- Transaction/service revenue where economically justified
- Premium automation / AI package
- Enterprise / institutional plan

## Guardrail
Revenue projections are hypotheses, not guarantees. The system must distinguish forecast assumptions from verified revenue.

## North-star metric
**Businesses successfully activated and generating verified customer revenue through IZAKHONO.**

## Handoff contract
The Idea Foundry passes a structured blueprint to the App Factory containing product name, target customer, problem, value proposition, revenue model, pricing hypothesis, required screens, data entities, workflows, integrations, launch checklist, acquisition hooks, retention hooks and success metrics.
