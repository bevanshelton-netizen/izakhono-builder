# IZAKHONO BOOKING

African-first booking and customer-management platform for service businesses.

## Business

Turn a service business into a bookable digital business: public booking page, availability, customer records, reminders, payments, and an owner dashboard.

## MVP

- Business onboarding
- Services and duration/pricing
- Staff/resources
- Availability and time-zone aware booking
- Customer profiles and booking history
- Booking confirmation/reschedule/cancel flows
- Reminder jobs
- Payment adapter boundary
- Owner dashboard
- Revenue and booking summary
- Mobile-first PWA shell

## Differentiation

- African-first currencies, languages and operating patterns
- Time-zone safe booking
- No-show reduction through reminders
- Provider-neutral payment architecture
- Owner-first data and infrastructure
- Designed to expand into WhatsApp, CRM, invoicing and marketplace channels

## Release gates

The MVP is a release candidate only until runtime deployment, HTTPS, database migrations, payment sandbox verification, reminder delivery and end-to-end acceptance evidence exist. No public-live claim is made by source generation alone.

## Product command

The Builder brief for this product is stored in `business/brief.json`. Architecture and API contracts are stored alongside it so the business can be regenerated deterministically.
