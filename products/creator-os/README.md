# IZAKHONO CREATOR OS

The Creator OS is the single operating contract joining IZAKHONO, ALLEGRO and KORA.

## One identity

A creator should not rebuild their identity for every platform.

Creator Passport carries:
- verified platform identity reference;
- legal/stage name;
- country/region/languages/genres;
- booking availability;
- career path;
- protected works;
- rights/contract status;
- public marketing fields;
- cross-platform link status.

## One lifecycle

**JOIN → PROTECT → PLAN → PUBLISH → DISCOVER → BROADCAST → SCREEN → TOUR → MONETISE → CLEARSET → GROW**

Career paths:
- Launch
- Grow
- Revive
- Relaunch

## Platform responsibilities

### IZAKHONO
Revenue, ARTIST PROTECT, Contract Shield, evidence, CLEARSET, payment orchestration and shared governance.

### ALLEGRO
Audio releases, artist marketing spaces, global discovery, bookings, radio, sponsor inventory and career engine.

### KORA
Music videos, live sessions, documentaries, movies, biopics, concert films, Tour2Screen, ticketing and screen monetisation.

## Non-negotiable gates

1. No creator monetisation without attributable ownership/rights state.
2. No disputed share paid while its ownership is unresolved.
3. Undisputed creator earnings are not automatically confiscated because another share is disputed.
4. No high-risk contract gets a “safe to sign” automated approval.
5. No KORA handoff auto-publishes.
6. No browser holds cross-platform integration secrets.
7. No live payment claim without actual provider/settlement proof.
8. No money labelled available until CLEARSET gates pass.
9. No “public on the internet” music is assumed royalty-free.
10. Every material action writes an auditable event/evidence reference.

## Commercial default

For creator revenue administered through ALLEGRO/Creator OS, the default platform share is 10%, creator side 90%, unless an approved written agreement explicitly overrides it.

Platform-owned advertising/subscription/service income is classified separately.
