# IZAKHONO RENT™

Africa-first, global-ready asset access marketplace.

## Product promise
Access anything. Rent everything.

## Core transaction paths
- Rent
- Buy
- Rent-to-own (eligible assets)

## Launch pillars
- Asset discovery and category browsing
- Owner asset monetisation
- Verified identities and listings
- Condition evidence at handover/return
- Deposits and controlled payment settlement
- Dispute workflow
- Business accounts and bulk inventory
- African-first payments, currencies and locations
- FORTRESS security integration
- IZAKHONO ID identity layer

## Initial categories
Homes & Stays, Vehicles, Tools & Equipment, Electronics, Events & Production, Cameras & Drones, Agriculture & Industrial, Furniture, Fashion & Wearables, Sports & Outdoor, Business & Office, Storage & Parking.

The current `index.html` is the launch experience. Transactional services should be connected through the IZAKHONO platform infrastructure before production commerce is represented as live.
