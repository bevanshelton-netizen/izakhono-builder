# YHVH GLOBAL BROADCAST ALLIANCE

## Mission

YHVH GOSPEL TV will build a worldwide Christian media alliance connecting television networks, Gospel radio stations, online radio, ministries, churches, artists, choirs, producers and distribution partners.

## Two-Way Distribution

**TELEVISION → RADIO**
- Broadcast-ready programme excerpts
- Interviews and testimonies
- Worship and choir features
- Event audio feeds
- Prayer and devotional blocks
- Gospel news/features

**RADIO → TELEVISION**
- Radio programmes adapted for video
- Studio interviews
- Live worship sessions
- Gospel music sessions
- Community stories
- Local-language Christian programming

## Partnership Principle

YHVH does not require partners to surrender ownership of their content. Rights remain with the creator/rights holder unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Payment, sponsorship or partnership does not guarantee airtime, endorsement or editorial approval.

## Technical Routes

1. IZAKHONO-owned distribution infrastructure where available and authorised.
2. Partner broadcast feeds and syndication.
3. Licensed satellite, OTT, FAST, web and radio distribution.
4. Authorised local distributors.
5. Approved external fallback routes.

Every route remains subject to rights, territory and regulatory controls.

## Global Acquisition Campaign

Build the partner registry country-by-country and region-by-region. Prioritise established networks first, then regional and local stations, then independent online broadcasters.

Discovery directories may be used to identify prospects, but no directory listing is treated as proof of partnership, ownership, contact validity, rights or regulatory clearance.

## Target Outcome

**YHVH GOSPEL TV + YHVH GOSPEL RADIO NETWORK = a connected African-origin Christian media distribution ecosystem serving the world.**
