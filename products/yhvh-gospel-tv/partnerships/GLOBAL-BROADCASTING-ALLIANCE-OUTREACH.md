# YHVH GLOBAL GOSPEL BROADCASTING ALLIANCE
**Authority:** IZAKHONO  
**Pillars:** Television + Gospel Radio + Digital Audio  
**Status:** Outreach framework established; individual contacts must be verified and logged.

## Purpose
Build a voluntary international alliance of Christian television networks, Gospel radio stations, online radio services, satellite and shortwave broadcasters, churches, ministries, producers and audio/video distributors.

YHVH is the African-origin coordination and distribution platform. Members retain their identity, ownership and editorial independence.

## Partnership invitation

**Subject: Invitation to Join the YHVH Global Gospel Broadcasting Alliance**

We would welcome a discussion around one or more of the following:
- Reciprocal television or radio programme exchange
- Gospel programme syndication and licensed carriage
- Live worship, conference and Gospel-event coverage

- Local-language production, translation and subtitling
- Digital streaming and audience discovery
- Joint production and separately agreed commercial opportunities

This is a collaborative proposal, not a request for your organisation to surrender its brand, editorial control, audience relationship or content ownership. Any use or redistribution of content would be governed by written permissions, territory-specific rights and a mutually agreed contract. Participation, sponsorship or payment does not guarantee airtime or editorial approval.

If this is of interest, please direct us to the person responsible for programming, syndication, partnerships or distribution. We would be pleased to arrange an introductory meeting and exchange technical and programming information.

## Tailoring rules
- TV networks: lead with programme exchange, carriage, live events, co-production and territory rights.
- Gospel radio: lead with audio syndication, interviews, worship sessions, event feeds and reciprocal programme exchange.
- Multiplatform networks: propose coordinated TV, radio, streaming and podcast collaboration.
- Regional/local stations: offer language-specific programming and locally relevant content partnerships.
- Do not claim an existing relationship, audience figure, carriage agreement, licence or technical capability unless verified.

## Contact and status discipline
Use the stages: RESEARCH → OFFICIAL CONTACT VERIFIED → INVITATION APPROVED → SENT → RESPONSE RECEIVED → DISCOVERY MEETING → DUE DILIGENCE → RIGHTS/TECHNICAL REVIEW → AGREEMENT → TEST → ACTIVE.

A public directory entry is only a research lead. Do not mark a contact SENT without an actual sent-message record. Do not mark a partner ACTIVE without a signed agreement, completed checks and successful technical test.

## Required pre-activation checks
1. Legal identity and authority of the broadcaster.
2. Content ownership and written distribution permissions.
3. Music composition, master recording, performer and collecting-society rights.
4. Territory, platform, language, term and exclusivity scope.
5. Local broadcasting, advertising and privacy rules.
6. Child safeguarding and contributor consent where applicable.
7. Technical stream, audio loudness, metadata and emergency contact tests.
8. Signed agreement and documented editorial independence.

## Alliance promise
**CONNECT. CREATE. EXCHANGE. DISTRIBUTE. REACH.**

The alliance expands access to Gospel media while respecting the law, creators, broadcasters, viewers and the independence of every participating organisation.
