# YHVH Global Christian Broadcast Alliance
## Television and Gospel Radio Partnership Framework

**Authority:** IZAKHONO  
**Network:** YHVH GOSPEL TV  
**Purpose:** Establish lawful, reciprocal relationships with Christian television networks, Gospel radio stations, Christian radio networks, digital audio broadcasters and regional distribution partners.

## 1. The invitation

YHVH GOSPEL TV is building an African-origin, globally connected Christian broadcasting alliance. We invite established broadcasters and emerging Gospel media platforms to explore practical cooperation that expands access to faith-centred programming, protects creator rights and connects audiences across languages and territories.

This is a partnership invitation, not a request for automatic carriage or an assumption of endorsement.

## 2. Partnership pathways

### Television broadcasters
- Programme exchange and licensed carriage
- Regional channel distribution
- Co-produced documentaries, worship events and Gospel features
- Event simulcast and outside-broadcast cooperation
- FAST, OTT, satellite and digital distribution where authorised

### Gospel radio stations and radio networks
- Radio programme syndication and scheduled programme exchange
- Gospel music, choir, artist and worship features
- Interviews, devotionals, testimony and Bible teaching
- Live event radio coverage and cross-promotion
- Podcast and on-demand audio distribution
- Local-language programme production and translation

### Hybrid and digital broadcasters
- Television/radio simulcast partnerships
- Shared production resources and technical delivery
- Approved audio/video feeds
- Joint creator discovery and audience development

## 3. What YHVH brings

- An African-origin Gospel television and creator ecosystem
- A structured content and creator intake pathway
- Rights, territory, safeguarding and technical review processes
- Opportunities to discover African Gospel artists, choirs, churches and producers
- Potential co-production, event and multilingual distribution collaboration
- A central partnership desk and documented agreement pathway

Availability of any programme, feed, service, audience figure or distribution route must be confirmed before it is offered.

## 4. What we ask prospective partners to share

1. Legal broadcaster or organisation name and country of registration
2. Official website and authorised partnership contact
3. Broadcast footprint and territories served
4. TV, FM, AM, DAB, satellite, OTT, streaming or podcast distribution methods
5. Languages and audience communities served
6. Programming interests and technical delivery requirements
7. Rights/licensing requirements and commercial model
8. Safeguarding, editorial and regulatory policies

## 5. Commercial and editorial safeguards

- Payment, sponsorship or advertising does not buy editorial approval or guaranteed airtime.
- No party may rebroadcast, stream, clip, translate or sublicense content without the relevant written rights.
- Territory, platform, term, language, exclusivity and monetisation rights must be stated in writing.
- Music publishing, master recording, performer, composition and collecting-society obligations must be checked separately.
- Each broadcaster retains its own editorial and regulatory responsibilities.
- Sponsorship and commercial terms remain separate from editorial decisions.
- Personal data and partner contact information must be handled securely.

## 6. Partnership pipeline

DISCOVERED → CONTACT VERIFIED → PRIORITY APPROVED → INVITATION PREPARED → INVITATION SENT → RESPONSE → DUE DILIGENCE → TERMS → AGREEMENT → RIGHTS/TERRITORY CLEARANCE → TECHNICAL QC → ACTIVE.

A prospect is not a partner until an authorised agreement is signed and all required operational gates are passed.

## 7. Initial rollout

**Wave 1 — African anchor broadcasters:** South Africa, Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe and regional Gospel radio networks.

**Wave 2 — Global Christian media networks:** North America, United Kingdom, Europe, Australia and New Zealand.

**Wave 3 — Language and diaspora corridors:** Portuguese-speaking Africa and Brazil; French-speaking Africa and Europe; Spanish-speaking Latin America; Arabic-speaking territories; South Asian and Southeast Asian language communities.

**Wave 4 — Local and specialist broadcasters:** independent Gospel radio, community stations, church radio, digital-only stations, campus radio and event broadcasters.

## 8. Measurement

Track verified prospects, invitations sent with evidence, responses, meetings, due-diligence completions, signed agreements, cleared programme hours, active distribution territories, radio syndication slots, languages served and creator payments. Do not report a prospect as contacted or partnered without evidence.
