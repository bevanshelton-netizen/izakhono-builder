# YHVH GLOBAL CHRISTIAN RADIO NETWORK

**Faith. Worship. Word. Africa to the World.**

Radio is a first-class YHVH distribution channel. The objective is to build lawful relationships with Christian radio networks, Gospel stations, church broadcasters, digital radio services, podcast networks and audio distributors worldwide.

## Operating model

**CREATE → PROTECT → DISTRIBUTE → EARN**

TV and radio operate as complementary channels:

- TV → radio audio adaptation
- Radio → TV programming discovery
- Live Gospel events → simultaneous TV/radio distribution
- Artists and choirs → cross-platform discovery
- Teaching and testimony → TV, radio and podcast formats
- Local-language Gospel → regional and international routing

## Partner pipeline

DISCOVERED → VERIFIED → CONTACTED → INTERESTED → DUE DILIGENCE → AGREEMENT SENT → NEGOTIATION → SIGNED → TECHNICAL QC → ACTIVE

## Non-negotiable controls

- A directory listing is a discovery lead, not a partnership.
- Free audio is not automatically broadcast-cleared.
- A station partnership does not transfer third-party rights.
- Territory and regulatory requirements must be checked where applicable.
- Payment does not guarantee airtime or editorial approval.
- YHVH does not bypass local law, licensing, censorship or access controls.
- Stream credentials and private keys never enter Git.

## First research signal

Public Christian-radio directories show substantial global scale, including large numbers of stations in North America, Africa, Europe, the Caribbean, Oceania and other regions. These directories are used only for discovery; every station is independently verified before outreach or activation.

## End state

YHVH GOSPEL TV becomes a **network of Christian media networks**: television + radio + digital audio + podcasts + events + creators + ministries + distribution partners, coordinated through IZAKHONO-owned control infrastructure.
