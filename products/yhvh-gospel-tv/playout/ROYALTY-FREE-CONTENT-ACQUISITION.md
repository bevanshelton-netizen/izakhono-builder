# YHVH GOSPEL TV — Royalty-Free Content Acquisition

## Objective
Build a lawful starter library for YHVH GOSPEL TV using content that is explicitly public-domain/CC0 or whose published license permits the intended commercial broadcast/streaming use.

## Important rule
"Free" does not automatically mean broadcast-cleared. Every asset must retain its source URL, exact license evidence, acquisition date, creator/rights-holder information where available, and any attribution requirement. Rights to music, performers, trademarks, privacy/publicity and underlying compositions are checked separately.

## Preferred tiers

### Tier A — CC0 / public domain
Preferred first. CC0 permits copying, modification, distribution and commercial use, subject to other rights that may apply. Public-domain status must be verified for the specific asset and jurisdiction.

### Tier B — Commercial Creative Commons
Accept CC BY where attribution can be carried in YHVH credits and CC BY-SA where the downstream licensing obligations are compatible with the intended use. Do not use NC or ND material for monetized broadcast/video without additional written permission.

### Tier C — Explicit royalty-free commercial libraries
Use only where the library's current terms expressly permit commercial broadcast/streaming and monetization. Capture the exact track/asset license, not merely the library homepage.

## Initial acquisition sources

1. **Pexels** — photos/video are stated by Pexels to be free for commercial use without attribution. Third-party privacy, publicity, trademark and endorsement issues still apply.
2. **Wikimedia Commons** — use only files whose individual file page shows public-domain/CC0 or another license compatible with commercial broadcast. Preserve attribution where required.
3. **Free Music Archive** — filter to CC0 or commercial-use licenses such as CC BY. Never use NC material for a monetized YHVH channel. Check each track individually.
4. **Worship Start Royalty-Free Music** — source states its donated library is royalty-free for TV shows, live streams and commercial/monetized productions; preserve the individual track terms and credit where applicable.
5. **SnipSound Gospel** — source states its gospel library is royalty-free and usable commercially; verify the exact license displayed for every selected track before ingestion.

## Acquisition gate

Every candidate remains `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until:

1. Source page captured.
2. Exact license captured.
3. Commercial/broadcast/streaming permission confirmed.
4. Attribution requirements recorded.
5. Creator/performer/third-party rights checked where applicable.
6. File downloaded into the YHVH content vault.
7. Technical QC passes.
8. Territory rights are cleared.
9. Owner approves the asset for broadcast.

## Never ingest automatically

- YouTube/Instagram/TikTok videos merely because they are publicly viewable.
- Music marked Content ID registered unless the actual rights position is verified.
- CC BY-NC or NC-SA material for the monetized station.
- CC BY-ND material if editing/syncing is required.
- Worship songs whose recording is free but whose composition/performer/public-performance rights remain unresolved.
- AI-generated content with unclear ownership or training/source provenance.
- Content containing identifiable people where the intended use creates endorsement/privacy concerns.

## Broadcast target

The acquisition engine should eventually build a large rotating library, but only assets with `rightsStatus=CLEAR`, `qcStatus=PASS`, correct territory and physical vault presence may enter the production playlist.
