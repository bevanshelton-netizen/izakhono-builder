# YHVH GOSPEL TV — GLOBAL CHRISTIAN TV + GOSPEL RADIO ALLIANCE

**Authority:** IZAKHONO  
**Brand:** YHVH GOSPEL TV  
**Tagline:** FAITH. WORSHIP. WORD. AFRICA TO THE WORLD.

## Mission

Build a lawful global alliance connecting Christian television networks, Gospel radio stations, streaming radio, churches, ministries, artists, choirs, producers, events and licensed distributors.

YHVH is the coordination and technology layer. It does not claim ownership of partner content unless a separate written agreement provides it.

## The ecosystem

**TV → RADIO → STREAMING → PODCAST → MUSIC → EVENTS → CREATORS → DISTRIBUTION**

A radio partner can contribute audio programming to YHVH, receive approved YHVH programming, cross-promote events, refer creators, carry authorised simulcasts and participate in commercial opportunities.

A TV partner can exchange programmes, events, documentaries, worship services and regional productions under written rights agreements.

PROSPECT → CONTACTED → INTERESTED → DUE DILIGENCE → AGREEMENT SENT → NEGOTIATION → SIGNED → TECHNICAL QC → RIGHTS CLEARED → ACTIVE

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Partnership does not equal endorsement.
- Payment does not guarantee airtime.
- Rights must be verified before broadcast.
- Territory rights must be verified before distribution.
- Music, performer and third-party rights must be addressed.
- Child safeguarding applies where minors participate.
- Medical, financial, fundraising and other sensitive claims require appropriate review.
- Local broadcasting and telecommunications law must be respected.
- No VPN, proxy or other mechanism may be used to evade licensing, censorship or access controls.
- Failover changes infrastructure, not legal rights.

## Radio integration architecture

**RADIO PARTNER STREAM → RIGHTS GATE → TECHNICAL QC → YHVH RADIO REGISTRY → LANGUAGE/TERRITORY ROUTING → AUTHORISED PLAYER/DISTRIBUTION**

**YHVH TV → APPROVED AUDIO EXTRACTION/PROGRAMME FEED → RADIO PARTNER** only where the written licence permits it.

## Discovery sources

Public directories can be used to identify prospects, but every partner must be verified through its official identity and contact channel before being treated as an active partner. Current discovery sources include World's Radio, HisAir.Net and RadioTune's Africa Christian FM directory.

## Success measure

The goal is not to collect logos. The goal is to establish **real, verified, signed, technically integrated partnerships** that expand Gospel reach while protecting creators, broadcasters and audiences.
