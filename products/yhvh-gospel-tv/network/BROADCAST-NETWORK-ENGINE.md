# YHVH Global Broadcast Network Engine

## Purpose

Operate YHVH as a **network of Christian television and Gospel radio partners**, rather than as a single channel.

## Engine layers

1. **Partner Engine** — discovers, registers, verifies and manages TV networks, TV stations, radio networks, radio stations, ministries and distributors.
2. **Rights Engine** — verifies content, territory, distribution method and agreement before activation.
3. **TV Playout Engine** — schedules Gospel television programming and produces authorised HLS/RTMP/SRT outputs.
4. **Radio Playout Engine** — schedules worship, Gospel music, Word, prayer and other audio programming and produces authorised audio outputs.
5. **Syndication Engine** — distributes approved feeds to authorised partners.
6. **Language Engine** — maps programming to language and territory, including translation/subtitle workflows.
7. **Commercial Engine** — manages sponsorship, advertising, licensing and revenue reporting without selling editorial control.
8. **Creator Engine** — protects creator ownership, rights, attribution, contracts and revenue participation.
9. **Analytics Engine** — measures technical health, audience, distribution and commercial performance.
10. **Security/Audit Engine** — identity, access, secrets, audit events, takedowns and revocation.

## Operating rule

`CREATE → PROTECT → DISTRIBUTE → EARN`

No partner, payment or distribution request bypasses rights, territory, safeguarding or technical gates.

## Partner onboarding

`PROSPECT → CONTACTED → INTERESTED → DUE_DILIGENCE → AGREEMENT_SENT → NEGOTIATION → SIGNED → TECHNICAL_QC → ACTIVE`

## Network proposition

A partner may contribute:

- TV programmes
- Gospel radio programmes
- live events
- worship/music programming
- Bible teaching
- African Gospel content
- local-language programming
- distribution capacity
- production capability
- audience reach

In return, YHVH can provide lawful content exchange, distribution, production, technology, multilingual routing, creator services, analytics and commercial opportunities.

## Fail-closed requirements

Before a feed is activated, the system must have:

- verified partner identity
- signed/valid agreement where required
- content rights clearance
- territorial clearance
- distribution-method clearance
- technical QC
- safeguarding/editorial review where applicable
- approved route

**Free content is not automatically broadcast-cleared. Payment does not guarantee airtime. A partner does not constitute editorial endorsement.**
