# YHVH GLOBAL MEDIA NETWORK PLAYBOOK

## Objective

Build YHVH GOSPEL TV into a global Christian media network connecting television, Gospel radio, online radio, podcasts, churches, ministries, artists, choirs, events, producers and distributors.

## Architecture

**AFRICAN ORIGIN → IZAKHONO CONTROL → GLOBAL TV + RADIO + DIGITAL DISTRIBUTION**

TV and radio remain distinct editorial products while sharing identity, creator, rights, territory, distribution, payments and analytics infrastructure.

## Radio partnership model

YHVH approaches stations as partners, not acquisitions. A station keeps its identity, audience, editorial responsibility and ownership unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Possible relationships:

1. YHVH audio feed carriage
2. YHVH programme syndication
3. Reciprocal content exchange
4. Simulcast of selected programmes/events
5. Local-language Gospel programming
6. Artist/choir discovery and promotion
7. Radio-to-TV programme development
8. TV-to-radio audio extraction where rights permit
9. Shared event coverage
10. Advertising/sponsorship partnerships
11. Creator Fund participation
12. Technical distribution partnership

## Global acquisition strategy

Work territory-by-territory and maintain a verified registry. Use public directories and official station/network websites only as discovery sources; the directory listing itself is not evidence of partnership, rights, licensing, ownership or current technical availability.

Priority order:

- Africa
- Caribbean
- Latin America
- North America
- Europe
- Middle East
- Asia
- Oceania

Within every territory, identify:

- Christian TV networks
- Gospel TV channels
- Christian FM/AM stations
- Gospel music stations
- Online Christian radio
- Church radio
- Community faith broadcasters
- Podcast/audio networks
- Satellite/digital radio distributors

## Partnership pipeline

DISCOVERED → VERIFIED → CONTACTED → INTERESTED → DUE DILIGENCE → AGREEMENT SENT → NEGOTIATION → SIGNED → TECHNICAL QC → ACTIVE

No station becomes ACTIVE solely because it responded positively or exchanged emails.

## Rights and editorial rules

- Payment never buys editorial approval.
- Partnership never equals endorsement.
- Free content is not automatically cleared content.
- A station's public stream is not automatically reusable by YHVH.
- No retransmission without the required authorization.
- Music, performance, recording, publishing and neighboring rights must be addressed where applicable.
- Territory restrictions must be enforced.
- Local broadcasting and advertising requirements must be respected.
- Child and vulnerable-person safeguarding applies to relevant content.
- Fail closed when rights or regulatory status is unresolved.

## Commercial model

Revenue can be generated through network sponsorship, programme sponsorship, faith-aligned advertising, licensed programming, events, production services, distribution services, creator marketplace activity and agreed revenue shares.

Commercial participation must remain contractually separate from editorial decisions.

## End-state

YHVH should become a **network of networks**:

**WATCH** — YHVH GOSPEL TV

**LISTEN** — YHVH GLOBAL GOSPEL RADIO

**CREATE** — YHVH CREATOR SPACE

**PARTNER** — YHVH GLOBAL NETWORK

**SUPPORT** — YHVH CREATOR FUND

**DISTRIBUTE** — YHVH WORLDWIDE

## Definition of done

The initiative is not complete merely because a registry exists. Completion requires verified partner records, executed agreements, rights-cleared content, technical connectivity, active distribution routes, reporting and independently verified audience delivery.
