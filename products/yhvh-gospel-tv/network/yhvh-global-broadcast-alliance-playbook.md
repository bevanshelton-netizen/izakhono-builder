# YHVH GLOBAL BROADCAST ALLIANCE

## Mission
Build a lawful worldwide alliance of Christian television networks, Gospel radio stations, web-radio platforms, churches, ministries, artists, choirs, producers, events and distributors around YHVH GOSPEL TV.

## Two-way network model
- TV → Radio: interviews, worship blocks, event audio, simulcast audio and licensed programme extracts.
- Radio → TV: radio-originated interviews, concerts, worship sessions and creator programming where rights are cleared.
- TV ↔ TV: programme exchange, regional distribution and co-production.
- Radio ↔ Radio: syndication and shared programming where rights permit.
- Network → YHVH: content, talent, events and distribution partnerships.
- YHVH → Network: African Gospel programming, technology, production, distribution and audience reach.

## Outreach sequence
1. Identify station/network and official contact.
2. Verify organization, territory, format and programming fit.
3. Send partnership invitation.
4. Record response in the registry.
5. Complete identity/entity, rights and safeguarding review where relevant.
6. Agree territory, language, format, content and commercial terms.
7. Technical QC.
8. Activate only after signed authorization and rights clearance.

## Partnership proposition
YHVH GOSPEL TV is building an African-origin global Gospel media ecosystem. Partners can exchange lawful programming, distribute Gospel content, collaborate on events, reach new audiences, develop creators and participate in transparent commercial opportunities while retaining editorial independence and creator ownership unless expressly agreed otherwise.

## Non-negotiables
- Prospect does not mean partner.
- Contact does not mean agreement.
- Payment does not guarantee airtime.
- Free content does not automatically mean broadcast-cleared.
- Rights must be documented.
- Territory rights must be verified.
- Local regulation must be respected.
- Sponsorship cannot purchase editorial approval.
- No VPN or technical mechanism may be used to bypass licensing, geo-rights, censorship or access controls.

## Commercial separation
Commercial proposals may include sponsorship, advertising, production, events, distribution and content licensing. Editorial decisions remain independent.

## Global objective
Create a connected Christian broadcast alliance that can lawfully move Gospel programming between Africa and the world through TV, radio, web, mobile, OTT, FAST, satellite and authorized local distribution.
