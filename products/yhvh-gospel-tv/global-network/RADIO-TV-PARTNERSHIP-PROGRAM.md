# YHVH GLOBAL CHRISTIAN BROADCAST NETWORK

## Mission
Build a worldwide Christian broadcasting alliance connecting YHVH GOSPEL TV with Christian television networks, Gospel radio stations, Christian radio networks, digital audio networks, churches, ministries and authorised distributors.

## The proposition
YHVH is not asking broadcasters to surrender their identity. The partnership model is reciprocal:

**CONTENT ↔ DISTRIBUTION ↔ LANGUAGE ↔ AUDIENCE ↔ EVENTS ↔ PRODUCTION ↔ TECHNOLOGY**

Partners may exchange programmes, interviews, music, sermons, worship, documentaries, event feeds, radio segments, podcasts and locally produced Gospel content subject to rights and territory approval.

## Media lanes
1. YHVH GOSPEL TV — television, OTT, FAST, web and event distribution.
2. YHVH GOSPEL RADIO — 24/7 Gospel audio, simulcast and syndicated radio programming.
3. YHVH DIGITAL AUDIO — podcasts, devotional audio, interviews and creator releases.
4. YHVH GLOBAL NETWORK — partner exchange, multilingual routing and authorised distribution.

## Partnership classes
- Christian TV Network
- Gospel Radio Station
- Christian Radio Network
- Satellite Broadcaster
- OTT / FAST Operator
- Digital Audio Network
- Podcast Network
- Church Broadcast Network
- Content Syndicator
- Local Distributor

## Operating rules
- Rights must be verified before distribution.
- Territory rights must be verified before distribution.
- Regulatory review is required where applicable.
- Payment, sponsorship or partnership never guarantees airtime or editorial approval.
- Creator ownership remains with the creator by default unless a written agreement states otherwise.
- No VPN or technical mechanism may be used to bypass licensing, territorial restrictions, censorship controls or local law.
- Failover can only move between independently authorised routes.
- All active routes must be auditable.

## Outreach sequence
DISCOVER → VERIFY → INTRODUCE → INTEREST → DUE DILIGENCE → RIGHTS REVIEW → AGREEMENT → TECHNICAL QC → TEST DISTRIBUTION → ACTIVE PARTNER

## Priority strategy
Start with high-value Christian TV networks and major Gospel radio networks, then expand country-by-country through regional stations and independent online broadcasters. Public directories are discovery sources only; each station must be independently verified before being treated as a partner.

## Commercial model
Potential revenue streams include:
- lawful content licensing
- syndication fees
- advertising inventory
- sponsorship
- production services
- event broadcasting
- distribution services
- creator marketplace revenue
- grants and mission-aligned funding

Commercial arrangements remain separate from editorial decisions.

## North Star
**ONE CHRISTIAN BROADCASTING ECOSYSTEM — MANY VOICES — ONE GLOBAL GOSPEL MISSION.**
