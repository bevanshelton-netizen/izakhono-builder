# CashFlow Guardian — Founder Onboarding

## Objective
Convert a qualified small business into an activated customer and then verify the first real paid subscription.

## 1. Qualification
Capture:
- Business name
- Contact person
- WhatsApp/email
- Number of invoices issued monthly
- Approximate receivables outstanding
- Current invoicing method
- Biggest collection/cash-flow problem

## 2. Activation
- Create business account
- Configure business profile
- Add first customers/debtors
- Add first outstanding invoices
- Set reminder preferences
- Review dashboard with customer

## 3. Conversion
Present Founder plan:
- R299/month
- Optional R499 once-off activation where appropriate

Do not record revenue from a promise, quotation or button click.

## 4. Payment verification
A payment becomes revenue only when the approved payment provider or verified financial record returns a successful transaction containing:
- customer_id
- product_id
- plan_id
- amount
- currency
- provider
- provider_transaction_id
- paid_at
- status=paid/succeeded

## 5. Retention
At 7 days:
- Check dashboard usage
- Confirm reminders sent
- Identify money recovered or actions completed

At 30 days:
- Review usage and customer feedback
- Identify upgrade opportunity
- Record retention status

## Founder success gate
**First activated business + first successful subscription payment + verified revenue event.**

Until all three are true, CashFlow Guardian remains in launch-validation mode.
