# CashFlow Guardian — Sales Playbook

## Objective
Get the first paying customer, then repeat the motion until the acquisition and activation process is predictable.

## Founder offer
- Activation: R499 once-off (pricing hypothesis)
- Founder subscription: R299/month (pricing hypothesis)
- Promise: simple visibility over money owed, money due and the actions that can improve collections.

## Ideal first customers
Prioritise owner-managed businesses that already issue invoices and experience delayed collections:
- clothing/uniform suppliers
- small manufacturers
- contractors and service businesses
- training providers
- wholesalers
- agencies

## Qualification questions
1. How do you currently track money customers owe you?
2. How many invoices are normally outstanding?
3. What happens when a customer pays late?
4. How much time is spent chasing payments each month?
5. Would a daily action list showing who to chase and what is due save time?

## Offer script
> We are piloting CashFlow Guardian with a small group of businesses. It gives you one place to see what customers owe, what is due, and which payment actions need attention. Founder pricing is R299/month, with a R499 once-off activation. We will help you get your first cash-flow workspace running.

## Conversion path
1. Qualified conversation
2. 15-minute workflow demonstration
3. Confirm pain and use case
4. Customer accepts founder pricing
5. Approved payment-provider checkout
6. Account activation
7. Import first receivables/invoices
8. First useful action completed
9. Verify payment event
10. Record customer outcome and retention signal

## Acquisition scoreboard
Track:
- prospects contacted
- qualified prospects
- demos completed
- offers made
- activations
- paid subscriptions
- verified revenue
- 7-day active customers
- 30-day retained customers
- expansion/upsell revenue

## No fake traction
Demo numbers are product-interface examples only. They must never be reported as customer revenue, customers, collections or traction. A customer counts only after activation evidence exists; revenue counts only after a real payment is verified.
