# COMMERCIAL ADVERTISING PRODUCTION SKILL

## Mission
Turn a product, service, brand or campaign brief into a coherent commercial production package for digital, social, retail and broadcast use.

## Intake
Identify the product/service, target customer, market, offer, proof points, brand kit, mandatory claims, prohibited claims, CTA, channels, language, duration, budget/quality tier and rights-cleared product assets. Ask only for information that materially affects correctness.

## Required outputs
- Campaign objective and audience insight
- Single-minded proposition and creative concept
- Message hierarchy, hook, proof and CTA
- Script and storyboard
- Shot-by-shot production plan
- Product-preservation requirements
- Brand and legal/claims checklist
- Variants by duration, aspect ratio, channel and language
- Landing page, captions and distribution metadata where requested
- Review and approval checklist

## Product integrity
- Treat supplied product images, packaging, logos, labels, colours, patterns and proportions as protected reference facts.
- Do not invent certifications, endorsements, performance claims, pricing, warranties or customer testimonials.
- Do not alter logos or product text without explicit direction.
- Flag model limitations where generated imagery may distort product details.
- Prefer compositing, masking or product-preserving workflows when exact product fidelity matters.
- Require human review of all legal, regulated or factual claims before publication.

## Creative standards
- Make the first seconds communicate a reason to keep watching.
- Show the product/service in a credible context.
- Keep visual direction specific to the brand and target market.
- Localise language and cultural details respectfully; never use generic stereotypes.
- Generate cut-downs from a coherent master concept, not unrelated fragments.
- Never publish or spend on a campaign without the required approval.

## Output
Return a production-ready campaign manifest with stable scene/shot IDs, script, visual prompts, protected brand facts, channel derivatives and approval gates.
