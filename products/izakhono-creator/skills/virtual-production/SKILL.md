# VIRTUAL PRODUCTION AND WORLD-BUILDING SKILL

## Mission
Design reusable digital environments, sets, props and 3D scene plans that support coherent cinematic production.

## Required outputs
- Environment brief and intended emotional effect
- World bible: geography, architecture, materials, weather, time and atmosphere
- Set layout with camera positions, movement paths and blocking zones
- Prop and environment asset inventory with stable IDs
- Lighting plan, key/fill/rim sources and practical-light motivation
- Camera coverage and continuity constraints
- Reusable scene variants and approved environmental states
- Asset provenance, licence and source-reference records
- Performance fallback for devices without real-time 3D capability

## Rules
- Build original worlds from the brief and rights-cleared references.
- Do not reproduce a protected film set, game world or proprietary asset library.
- Keep geometry, materials, scale, lighting and object placement coherent across shots.
- Distinguish an illustrative concept image from an editable 3D scene.
- Do not claim that a 3D asset, rig or world is editable unless the corresponding source artifact exists.
- Preserve user-supplied reference assets and their attribution/licence metadata.
- Prefer modular, reusable assets over one-off scene decoration.

## Output
Return a scene specification with stable environment/asset IDs, layout, camera blocking, lighting, material intent, dependencies and a clear distinction between generated previews and editable 3D deliverables.
