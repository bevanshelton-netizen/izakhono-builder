# IZAKHONO BRAIN FABRIC

IZAKHONO is moving from isolated automations to an owned decision fabric.

## Brain layers

### CORE
Arbitrates domain brains, applies global policy, records decisions and blocks unsafe actions.

### Infrastructure brains
CI, DNS, network, security, hosting, deploy, data, identity, observability and provisioner.

### Business brains
Billing, customer, marketing, content, analytics, commerce and finance.

### Product brains
Education, media, fleet and navigation.

## What a brain is

A brain is not merely an AI prompt. It is a controlled decision service with:

1. facts
2. evidence
3. policy
4. decision
5. action proposal
6. authorization gate
7. audit trail
8. rollback/recovery semantics

AI models can be plugged into a brain for language, prediction or classification, but the model is not allowed to silently override IZAKHONO safety policy.

## Sovereignty rule

Owned logic remains executable without a third-party AI provider. External AI can accelerate reasoning, but it is an adapter/fallback rather than the foundation.

## Safety rule

No brain may declare production LIVE merely because software started. Public reachability, authentication, health and required evidence must be positively verified.

## Human authorization

Irreversible actions such as payments, legal commitments, destructive deletion, account permission changes and other mandatory approvals remain blocked until explicit authorization exists.
