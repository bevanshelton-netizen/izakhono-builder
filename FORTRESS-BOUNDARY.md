# FORTRESS SOVEREIGN BOUNDARY

## Locked group decision

IZAKHONO remains an owner-controlled sovereign technology stack protected by **IZAKHONO FORTRESS**.

External technology companies are optional suppliers and tools, not control points.

OpenAI may be used when useful for work outside the sovereign core, including research, advisory work, public content, design assistance, sanitized debugging, or other deliberately approved tasks.

The following remain inside the IZAKHONO trust boundary:

- identity and authentication
- customer and learner records
- entity-scoped access
- subscription entitlements
- payment orchestration and ledgers
- secrets and credentials
- private operational data
- owner AI runtime required for continuity
- source/deployment control as migration completes
- radio/media playout state and rights records
- business systems of record

## Four rules

1. **Default deny outbound.**
2. **Explicit entity scope.**
3. **Sanitize before external use.**
4. **No external provider may become a required authority or single point of failure.**

This does not prohibit the owner from meeting, consulting, researching, building or collaborating with OpenAI or other external providers. It prevents those relationships from compromising IZAKHONO ownership, continuity, confidential information or entity separation.
