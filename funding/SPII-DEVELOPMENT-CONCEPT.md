# IZAKHONO — SPII Development Concept

## Proposed development project
Development and validation of an African-owned, provider-independent technology infrastructure platform combining AI intelligence, mobility safety, cybersecurity and payment orchestration.

## Applicant
IZAKHONO Africa (Pty) Ltd, subject to final corporate, ownership, tax and IP documentation checks.

## Development objective
Produce a defined pre-production technology demonstrator with documented architecture, software modules, testing evidence, security controls, integration interfaces and a route to commercial deployment.

## Proposed qualifying development areas
- Software and technology development
- Direct development personnel
- Testing and trials
- Quality assurance and certification activities where eligible
- Specialist technical subcontracting where eligible
- Documentation and IP-related development work where eligible
- Required development tooling and software where eligible

## Technology components
1. IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE™
2. IZAKHONO NAV™
3. FORTRESS™
4. IZAKHONO PAY™
5. Shared Control Plane and resilience infrastructure

## Commercial outcome
The resulting technology is intended for enterprise, fleet, OEM, platform and strategic technology customers through licensing, APIs, subscriptions, integration fees, white-label arrangements and royalties.

## SPII alignment notes
The project must be structured around a specific eligible development project rather than the entire IZAKHONO portfolio. The final submission must demonstrate significant technological advance, South African development and production, South African IP ownership, eligible expenditure and the required level of applicant contribution. No expenditure incurred before a duly completed application should be presented as eligible.

## Funding pathway
Evaluate both the SPII Product Process Development scheme and Matching Scheme against the final project scope and ownership profile. The published maximums are R2m for the PPD scheme and R5m for the Matching Scheme, subject to applicable percentages, qualifying costs and programme rules.

## Required next evidence
- CIPC/company documents
- IP ownership evidence
- ownership/B-BBEE evidence where applicable
- technical development plan
- development-stage evidence
- detailed qualifying-cost budget
- applicant contribution evidence
- commercialisation pathway
- project milestones and completion percentage
- supplier/subcontractor quotations where required

## Critical boundary
This concept is a funding-preparation document, not a representation that IZAKHONO currently satisfies every SPII eligibility criterion. Final eligibility must be confirmed against the prescribed application and supporting-document requirements.
