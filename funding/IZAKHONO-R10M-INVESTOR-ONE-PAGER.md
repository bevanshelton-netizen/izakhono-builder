# IZAKHONO — R10M Strategic Capital Opportunity

## The opportunity
IZAKHONO Africa is building an African-owned technology infrastructure platform designed to provide reusable engines across intelligent mobility, cybersecurity, payments and AI.

**Capital objective: R10,000,000**

## Why IZAKHONO
Africa's digital economy needs technology that is locally relevant, commercially deployable and resilient to dependency on a single external provider. IZAKHONO is designed around reusable infrastructure rather than isolated applications.

## Core investable assets
### IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE™
Reusable AI decision and autonomy infrastructure for multiple products and industries.

### IZAKHONO NAV™
Intelligent mobility and driver-safety platform targeting consumer, fleet, OEM and secure-mobility markets.

### FORTRESS™
Security and resilience infrastructure for mobility, enterprise and transaction environments.

### IZAKHONO PAY™
Provider-independent payment orchestration infrastructure with routing, idempotency, ledger, reconciliation, webhook and failover architecture. Regulated settlement/custody functions remain with appropriately authorised partners unless and until IZAKHONO obtains the required authorisations.

## Business model
- Enterprise SaaS
- API usage
- Technology licensing
- OEM licensing
- White-label platforms
- Integration and deployment fees
- Device/usage royalties
- Strategic partnerships

## Funding architecture
The R10m objective is deliberately diversified across technology-development support, commercial/industrial funding, strategic investment and paid enterprise pilots. This reduces dependence on one funder and allows non-dilutive funding to de-risk later private capital.

## Strategic fit
Potential strategic capital profiles include African ICT infrastructure, software/SaaS, fintech, mobility/telematics, security and technology investors. Convergence Partners explicitly invests across African ICT infrastructure, software/SaaS, enabled platforms and fintech. HAVAÍC targets high-growth African technology businesses with global potential. Mamor Capital currently targets post-revenue South African technology businesses, including payments and technology-enabled financial inclusion, with a stated R6m–R27m pre-Series-A ticket; IZAKHONO should approach that route only once revenue evidence supports the fit.

## The ask
IZAKHONO is seeking a strategic funding partner or consortium to support the next stage of validated technology development, enterprise pilots and commercial scale.

Preferred structures can include equity, strategic investment, co-funding, paid pilot contracts, licensing advances or combinations thereof, subject to due diligence and legal documentation.

## Evidence discipline
Current technology materials include reference implementations and architecture work. Production certification, regulatory approval, independent security certification, customer contracts and commercial revenue will only be represented when documentary evidence exists.

## Strategic outcome
Build a South African technology platform capable of generating recurring software/API/licensing revenue while retaining core intellectual property and creating pathways to African and international scale.
