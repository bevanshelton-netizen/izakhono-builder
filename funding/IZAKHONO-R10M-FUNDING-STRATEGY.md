# IZAKHONO R10M Funding Strategy

## Objective
Secure R10,000,000 in combined technology-development funding, strategic capital and commercial funding for IZAKHONO Africa's technology platform.

## Core investment thesis
IZAKHONO is building African-owned technology infrastructure that can be deployed, licensed and commercialised across intelligent mobility, cybersecurity, payment infrastructure and AI, with additional technology assets forming a broader IP pipeline.

## Priority capital routes
1. TIA Technology Development Fund — prototype, demonstration and pilot development. TIA states the fund supports TRL 4–7 technology development and lists funding from R500,000 to R50,000,000.
2. SPII — South African technology/product development. The Product Process Development scheme has a maximum R2m grant; the Matching Scheme has a maximum R5m grant, subject to qualifying costs, ownership and development criteria.
3. IDC — commercial/industrial funding. IDC states it funds start-ups and existing businesses from R1m to R1bn, subject to its assessment and due diligence.
4. Strategic corporate investment and paid pilots — OEM, fleet, security, payment and enterprise partners.

## Proposed R10m use of funds
- R3.0m: core technology engineering and product development
- R2.0m: pilots, testing, security validation and technical demonstrations
- R3.0m: infrastructure, engineering capacity and commercial deployment capability
- R2.0m: market activation, strategic partnerships and enterprise/OEM engagement

## Flagship assets
- IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE™ — reusable AI decision/autonomy layer
- IZAKHONO NAV™ — intelligent mobility and driver-safety platform
- FORTRESS™ — cybersecurity and resilience fabric
- IZAKHONO PAY™ — provider-independent payment orchestration and infrastructure

## Commercial model
Subscription, API usage, enterprise licensing, OEM licensing, white-label licensing, technology licensing and usage/device royalties.

## Evidence discipline
No funding application should state that a system is production-certified, independently penetration-tested, live with a customer, regulator-approved or commercially deployed unless documentary evidence exists. Prototype/reference implementations must be described as such.

## Immediate workstream
- Prepare TIA technology-development application package.
- Prepare SPII project-specific development package.
- Prepare IDC executive funding case and financial model.
- Build strategic-investor and enterprise-pilot outreach list.
- Assemble a due-diligence data room covering corporate records, IP, architecture, prototypes, budgets, governance, security and commercial pipeline.

## Target outcome
R10m committed capital across multiple sources, rather than dependence on a single funder.
