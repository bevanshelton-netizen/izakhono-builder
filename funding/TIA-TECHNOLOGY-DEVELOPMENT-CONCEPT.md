# IZAKHONO — TIA Technology Development Concept

## Applicant
IZAKHONO Africa (Pty) Ltd

## Proposed technology programme
African-owned technology infrastructure for intelligent mobility, security and payment orchestration.

## Problem
African technology operators frequently depend on fragmented external platforms for navigation, security, payments, communications and AI. This creates integration complexity, vendor dependency and weak control over the technology layer.

## Proposed solution
Develop a reusable technology infrastructure stack consisting of:
- IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE™ — AI decision and autonomy layer
- IZAKHONO NAV™ — mobility and driver-safety platform
- FORTRESS™ — security, resilience and transaction-protection layer
- IZAKHONO PAY™ — provider-independent payment orchestration layer
- IZAKHONO Control Plane — shared provisioning, routing, audit, resilience and commercial metering infrastructure

## Development scope
The programme will advance controlled reference implementations toward validated prototypes and operating demonstrations. Activities will include software engineering, integration, testing, security validation, performance testing, pilot deployment preparation, documentation and commercial validation.

## Commercialisation
The technology will be commercialised through enterprise SaaS, API usage, OEM integration, technology licensing, white-label licensing and device/usage royalties.

## South African value
The programme is intended to build local technical capability, retain core IP in South Africa, create skilled technology roles and establish exportable African-origin technology products.

## Funding request
A detailed amount and milestone budget will be submitted against the applicable TIA instrument and confirmed eligible development activities. The broader IZAKHONO capital objective is R10m across complementary funding and commercial sources.

## Readiness statement
The programme includes reference implementations and architecture work. Product-specific production readiness, regulatory approvals, independent security certification and customer deployment will be pursued through defined validation stages and will not be represented as completed without evidence.
