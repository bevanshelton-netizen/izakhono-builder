# IZAKHONO R10M — APPLICATION OPERATIONS PLAYBOOK

Status: Operational funding execution baseline

## Objective
Convert the R10m funding architecture into controlled application execution without overstating readiness, eligibility, traction or approvals.

## 1. TIA — PRIMARY APPLICATION TRACK

### Target project
Project A — IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE + CONTROL PLANE.

### Proposed development scope
- AI orchestration and model/provider abstraction
- Context, reasoning, action and safety interfaces
- Human override and policy controls
- Identity/provisioning and engine registry
- Primary/backup/failover routing
- Survival/degraded mode
- Monitoring, audit and recovery
- API/SDK and reproducible-build documentation

### Budget baseline
R3,000,000 indicative project scope.

### TIA gates
1. Confirm legal entity and CIPC status.
2. Confirm CSD registration.
3. Confirm tax compliance.
4. Confirm governance/shareholding evidence.
5. Assemble proof-of-concept and TRL evidence.
6. Establish baseline TRL and remaining development work.
7. Map each cost to eligible development activity.
8. Prepare commercialisation and socio-economic impact case.
9. Identify private/industry co-funding where appropriate.
10. Register/use the TIA funding portal and submit only after internal QC.

TIA states that its Technology Development Fund supports prototype-to-demonstration development at TRL 4–7 and that SMMEs/start-ups can qualify subject to requirements. TIA's application process requires a complete application before the stated investment-decision turnaround clock begins.

## 2. SPII — QUALIFYING PROJECT SELECTION

SPII must NOT receive the entire IZAKHONO portfolio as one undifferentiated application.

### Selection rule
Select one clearly bounded development project after the eligibility matrix is completed.

### Candidate projects
- Project A: Intelligence + Control Plane
- Project B: NAV + FORTRESS
- Project C: PAY

### Mandatory checks
- Significant technology advance
- South African development
- South African production pathway
- IP resides in South African registered company
- South African registered enterprise
- No simultaneous application from the company
- No overlapping government-funded costs
- Qualifying development costs only
- Project not already substantially complete

### Budget control
Do not count marketing, general administration, production/commercialisation expenditure or pre-application costs as qualifying SPII development costs where excluded by the scheme.

## 5. APPLICATION CONTROL BOARD

Every application must carry one status:

- RED — blocked by missing evidence or eligibility uncertainty
- AMBER — evidence exists but validation/action remains
- GREEN — internally complete and ready for external submission
- SUBMITTED — externally submitted with timestamp/evidence
- DILIGENCE — funder reviewing
- APPROVED — formal written approval received
- CONTRACTED — agreement executed
- FUNDED — funds actually received
- COMMERCIAL — associated commercial activity evidenced

## 6. DAILY EXECUTION CHECK

Before submission:
- Corporate evidence current
- IP chain-of-title documented
- Budget reconciled
- Qualifying/non-qualifying costs separated
- Funding overlap checked
- TRL evidence attached
- Technical claims verified
- Market claims evidenced
- Regulatory boundaries documented
- Application form internally reviewed
- Supporting documents indexed
- Final PDF/package archived

## 7. CAPITAL ACCOUNTING RULE

R10m target is a programme target, not secured capital.

Only count money as secured when there is documentary evidence of the relevant stage:
- grant approval/contract;
- executed investment agreement; or
- signed commercial agreement with defined financial consideration.

Funds received are counted only after bank/accounting evidence confirms receipt.

## 9. NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARDRAIL

Operational does not mean approved, funded, certified or production-proven.

The system is operational when every claim has an evidence state, every funding request has a defined route, every cost has an eligibility classification, every application has an owner/status, and every external submission can be audited end-to-end.
