# IZAKHONO R10M — FUNDER PACKAGE ROUTING

**Status:** Execution package v1
**Purpose:** Convert the R10m programme into funder-specific application packages without mixing development, commercialisation, investor and pilot evidence.

## 1. PACKAGE A — TIA TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT

**Primary project:** IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE + CONTROL PLANE

**Positioning:** South African technology development project advancing reusable AI orchestration and resilient control infrastructure from executable prototype toward controlled operating-environment demonstration.

**Target instrument:** TIA Technology Development Fund. TIA describes this instrument as supporting prototype through technology demonstration at TRL 4–7, including prototype development, demonstration/piloting and techno-economic work. Private co-funding may be used where appropriate.

**Indicative project budget:** R3,000,000.

**Application evidence pack:**
- Company registration and governance documents
- Tax/CSD/B-BBEE evidence as applicable
- IP ownership statement and IP register
- Architecture and technical specifications
- Source-control history and prototype evidence
- Current TRL baseline and evidence
- Work packages, milestones and acceptance criteria
- Detailed personnel/software/testing/prototype budget
- Failure/failover/recovery test evidence
- Commercialisation pathway and target-market evidence
- Private co-funding evidence where applicable
- Risk register and dependency-replacement plan

**Claim discipline:** describe existing work as architecture/prototype/reference implementation unless independently verified as deployed production technology.

## 2. PACKAGE B — SPII MATCHING / DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

**Primary project:** Select ONE qualifying development project after eligibility review. Recommended candidate: a tightly frozen IZAKHONO PAY orchestration development scope or another project where significant technological advance, South African development/production and IP ownership can be evidenced.

**Target instrument:** SPII Matching Scheme, subject to formal eligibility. Current dtic guidance states a maximum grant of R5m and specifies qualifying development costs, ownership/production/IP requirements and non-overlap rules.

**Critical rule:** Do not submit the whole IZAKHONO portfolio. Do not submit overlapping government-funded costs. Do not include production, commercialisation, marketing or general administration expenditure where excluded.

**Pre-submission gates:**
1. Freeze one project scope.
2. Establish baseline and remaining development work.
3. Demonstrate significant technological advance.
4. Confirm South African development and subsequent production pathway.
5. Confirm IP resides in the South African registered company.
6. Map each cost to an eligible category.
7. Remove prior costs and excluded expenditure.
8. Confirm matching contribution and ownership percentage.
9. Confirm no simultaneous application creates a conflict.
10. Obtain internal legal/finance sign-off before submission.

## 3. PACKAGE C — STRATEGIC INVESTOR

**Primary thesis:** IZAKHONO as an African-owned technology infrastructure company with reusable engines spanning AI, intelligent mobility, cybersecurity/resilience and payment infrastructure.

**Ask:** R3m–R5m strategic capital, structured according to investor fit and diligence.

**Investor evidence pack:**
- Executive one-pager
- 12-slide investor deck
- Founder/company profile
- Technology architecture
- Product portfolio map
- Current prototype and QC evidence
- R10m use-of-funds model
- 12-month milestones
- Commercial model
- Target-customer map
- IP/data-room index
- Risk and dependency strategy
- Cap table/shareholding information when formally prepared
- Investor-specific investment thesis and proposed instrument

**No unsupported claims:** no valuation, customer traction, investor interest, revenue, patent status or production status unless evidenced.

## 4. PACKAGE D — ENTERPRISE PILOT / LICENSING

**Primary products:** NAV + FORTRESS; PAY where regulated partners are appropriate.

**Commercial ask:** paid pilot, integration agreement, technology licence, API agreement or OEM/white-label pathway.

**Evidence pack:**
- Product capability statement
- Pilot scope
- Acceptance criteria
- Security architecture
- Data/privacy boundary
- Integration requirements
- Pricing model
- Pilot economics
- Licence/OEM structure
- Exit/renewal terms
- Technical support model

## 5. MASTER R10M CAPITAL STACK

| Route | Target | Primary use |
|---|---:|---|
| TIA development | R3.0m–R5.0m | Intelligence + Control Plane |
| SPII | Up to R5.0m subject to eligibility | One defined development project |
| Strategic capital | R3.0m–R5.0m | Platform scale + commercialisation |
| Paid pilots/licensing | R0.5m–R1.5m | Validation + revenue |

These are target pathways, not secured funding. The R10m objective is achieved through a stack of validated funding and commercial routes rather than assuming one programme will finance the entire portfolio.

## 6. EXECUTION ORDER

**Gate 1 — Evidence:** complete corporate, IP, technical, TRL, financial and market evidence register.

**Gate 2 — Eligibility:** run project-by-project eligibility checks before submission.

**Gate 3 — Application:** produce funder-specific forms, budgets and narratives.

**Gate 4 — Commercial:** package NAV/FORTRESS for paid pilots and PAY for regulated-partner pilots.

**Gate 5 — Investor:** send qualified investor packages only after the data room is internally consistent.

**Gate 6 — Validation:** update every claim from RED/AMBER to GREEN only when documentary evidence exists.

## 7. NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTROL

The R10m programme is not considered fundable merely because the technology is impressive. It becomes fundable when **technology + evidence + eligibility + economics + market pathway + governance** are all documented and internally consistent.
