## Slide 1 — The Proposition
**IZAKHONO**

African-owned technology infrastructure for AI, intelligent mobility, security and financial infrastructure.

Build reusable technology engines that can be deployed, licensed, integrated and monetised across multiple markets.

**Core thesis:** Own the engine. Own the resilience. License the capability. Scale through partners.

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem
Modern businesses increasingly depend on fragmented external technology stacks.

- AI depends on external models and providers.
- Mobility depends on mapping, telematics, connectivity and vehicle data.
- Security depends on disconnected tools and weak recovery paths.
- Payments depend on multiple processors, rails and reconciliation systems.
- African operating environments require resilience under connectivity, infrastructure and affordability constraints.

**IZAKHONO's opportunity:** build the orchestration and resilience layer that makes critical technology more independent, adaptable and commercially reusable.
