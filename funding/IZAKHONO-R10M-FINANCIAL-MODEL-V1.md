## 4. Funding architecture

### Track A — TIA

Primary candidate: Technology Development Fund for qualifying technology development at the appropriate TRL. TIA states that the TDF supports development from prototype toward demonstration in an operating environment, including TRL 4–7, and that co-funding may be sourced privately.

**Candidate project:** IZAKHONO INTELLIGENCE + CONTROL PLANE + resilience infrastructure.

**Required evidence before submission:** technology baseline, TRL assessment, technical work packages, development milestones, qualifying budget, commercialisation case, corporate compliance, IP ownership and any required co-funding/partner evidence.

### Track B — SPII

SPII Matching Scheme has a maximum grant of R5m and supports qualifying costs in a specified development project. The project must satisfy the applicable South African development/IP/innovation criteria.

**Candidate project:** one tightly scoped technology development project, not the entire 27-box portfolio.

**Guardrail:** do not combine the same costs with another government-funded project. SPII also excludes production/commercialisation and other non-qualifying costs.

### Track C — Strategic capital

Priority investor thesis:
- African-owned technology infrastructure
- reusable AI/intelligence
- intelligent mobility
- cybersecurity/resilience
- payment infrastructure
- provider independence
- licensing/API/OEM/royalty potential

Priority targets should be qualified by actual stage fit before outreach.

### Track D — Paid pilots

Target customers:
- fleets
- logistics/security companies
- mobility/telematics businesses
- payment/fintech businesses
- enterprise technology users
- public-sector/municipal technology partners where procurement allows

Pilot revenue must be based on signed agreements, purchase orders or invoices; pipeline is not revenue.

### Track E — Licensing/OEM

Potential commercial structures:
- annual technology licence
- API usage
- per-device royalty
- OEM integration fee
- white-label licence
- enterprise deployment fee

## 12. Evidence classification

**GREEN — verified:** documentary evidence exists.

**AMBER — claimed/planned:** requires verification.

**RED — prohibited claim:** no evidence and must not appear in external funding/investor materials.

Examples:
- Architecture exists → GREEN if repository evidence is verified.
- Production deployment → RED unless independently verified.
- Investor interest → RED unless documented.
- Paid customer → RED unless agreement/invoice exists.
- Patent granted → RED unless official registration evidence exists.
- Prototype test → GREEN when reproducible test evidence exists.
