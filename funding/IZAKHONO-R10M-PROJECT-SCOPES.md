# SPII ELIGIBILITY GATE

SPII's current official guidance says the Matching Scheme has a maximum grant of R5m and supports a specified development project. Qualifying costs include personnel, direct materials, capital/tooling, eligible software, documentation, testing/trials, licensing, QA/certification, patent costs and subcontracting/consulting. It excludes production/commercialisation, marketing/administration, single-client development, basic/applied research and projects already too far advanced at application. It also prohibits simultaneous applications from the same company and projects receiving other government funding.

Therefore, before any SPII submission:
1. Select exactly one project.
2. Freeze its scope and cost base.
3. Identify the development starting point and remaining work.
4. Remove non-qualifying expenditure.
5. Confirm South African development/production and IP position.
6. Check overlap with every other government funding request.
7. Confirm the required matching contribution.
8. Preserve evidence of costs incurred only after the eligible application point where required.
