# IZAKHONO PORTFOLIO MARKETING CONTROL DIRECTIVE

Effective: 22 September 2026

## Mandatory rule

**All advertising and marketing for every IZAKHONO portfolio product must originate in IZAKHONO CREATE.**

This applies to current and future products, trading names, platforms, campaigns and commercial ventures operated through the IZAKHONO portfolio.

No portfolio application should maintain an independent creative-production workflow that bypasses IZAKHONO CREATE.

## Control-plane model

1. **IZAKHONO CREATE** — compulsory creative source and brand-control layer.
   - campaign brief
   - offer/message
   - visual design
   - copy
   - brand kit
   - format adaptation
   - approvals
   - reusable campaign package

2. **IZAKHONO ADS** — downstream distribution and measurement layer.
   - audience/channel selection
   - campaign scheduling
   - connector management
   - spend controls
   - lead attribution
   - analytics
   - optimisation
   - publishing only after the required approvals and connector gates

3. **Portfolio products** — destinations and conversion surfaces.
   - receive traffic
   - expose approved landing pages and offers
   - preserve product-specific legal, merchant, privacy and regulatory boundaries
   - report conversion/lead/revenue signals back to the marketing stack where permitted

## Required campaign flow

`Product -> IZAKHONO CREATE -> approved marketing package -> IZAKHONO ADS -> approved channel connector -> audience -> product landing/conversion surface`

Every campaign package must identify:
- product / brand
- campaign name
- source = IZAKHONO CREATE
- creative/project payload
- intended channels/formats
- offer and CTA
- approval/spend status
- legal/regulatory notes where applicable
- attribution identifiers

IZAKHONO ADS must not treat an independently created creative as approved portfolio marketing.

## Safety and commercial gates

- CREATE approval does not itself authorise advertising spend.
- ADS remains paused-by-default until an external channel account is connected, credentials are held server-side, tracking is configured, and owner approval is present.
- Product-specific legal/regulatory rules remain authoritative.
- Financial, insurance, education, employment, health, broadcasting and other regulated claims require the appropriate compliance review before publication.
- Customer and campaign data remain tagged/isolateable by legal entity and product.
- External advertising networks are distribution channels, not the system of record for IZAKHONO creative assets.

## Infrastructure rule

Use IZAKHONO-owned infrastructure first. Keep external hosting/channel providers reversible and replaceable. A provider outage or quota must not make campaign source assets inaccessible.

## Future products

New products are covered automatically by this directive. Product onboarding should register a marketing identity and landing destination with IZAKHONO CREATE before the product is considered marketing-ready.
