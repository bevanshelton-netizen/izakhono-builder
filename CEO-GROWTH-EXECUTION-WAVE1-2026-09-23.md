# CEO Growth Execution — Wave 1

Effective: 23 September 2026

## Objective

Move the portfolio from build-heavy activity into measurable revenue execution using the existing control chain:

`Product -> IZAKHONO CREATE -> approved campaign package -> IZAKHONO ADS -> audience -> activation -> retention/referral -> revenue`

Wave 1 concentrates on products with a clear buyer, clear offer and short path to revenue.

## Wave 1 products

### 1. FAISReady

Primary audience: South Africans preparing for applicable FAIS regulatory examinations.

Primary CTA: **Start preparing**

Revenue event: completed course or bundle purchase.

Acquisition mix:
- high-intent search and SEO;
- LinkedIn career/professional content;
- Facebook/Instagram short-form creative;
- WhatsApp sharing;
- FSP, brokerage, bank and insurer cohort partnerships.

Activation: buyer begins the learning pathway and completes the first study milestone.

Retention: progress, practice, mock assessments and readiness milestones.

Compliance: position as exam preparation. Do not imply that FAISReady is the official regulatory examination body or guarantee a pass.

### 2. Edu-Build Institute

Primary audience: ECD practitioners, prospective students, centres, employers and cohort funders.

Primary CTA: **Enrol / Request campus information**

Revenue event: confirmed enrolment, institutional cohort or funded seat.

Acquisition mix:
- campus and community outreach;
- WhatsApp;
- Facebook;
- ECD centre and school partnerships;
- employer/funder outreach;
- search content around ECD practitioner development.

Activation: qualified lead becomes an enrolled learner or confirmed cohort.

Retention: classes, progress, assessments, learner support and qualification progression.

Compliance: qualification/accreditation claims must match the currently approved scope and supporting evidence.

### 3. Izakhono Africa Clothing Manufacturing

Primary audience: schools, sports clubs, teams, businesses and organisations buying uniforms, apparel and PPE.

Primary CTA: **Request a quote**

Revenue event: accepted quote/deposit/order.

Acquisition mix:
- direct institutional sales;
- WhatsApp catalogue/share links;
- local search/SEO;
- school, team and club referrals;
- corporate procurement outreach;
- short-form showcase content.

Activation: qualified quote request includes item, quantity, sizes/specification and deadline.

Retention: repeat institutional ordering, reorder reminders and account management.

## CEO execution rules

1. Every landing page must answer within five seconds: what is this, who is it for, what should I do next?
2. Each Wave 1 page gets one dominant CTA.
3. Share/WhatsApp actions must be present where appropriate.
4. iKhokha remains the preferred live payment route where technically and commercially suitable.
5. Organic proof, direct sales and partnerships precede paid scaling.
6. No behavioural surveillance, hidden tracking, spam or dark-pattern growth.
7. No campaign is called published until the external channel connector and public destination are independently verified.
8. External infrastructure may be used as a reversible resilience route; owned IZAKHONO infrastructure remains primary.

## Operating cadence

**Daily**
- respond to qualified leads;
- remove checkout/CTA friction;
- produce and distribute approved CREATE assets;
- record aggregate leads, activations and revenue.

**Monday**
- revenue and pipeline review.

**Wednesday**
- partnership and distribution review.

**Friday**
- conversion bottleneck and product-growth review.

## Wave 1 KPI board

Track:
- qualified leads;
- CTA conversion rate;
- completed purchases/enrolments/orders;
- revenue;
- partner-sourced leads;
- referrals/shares;
- repeat customers/retained learners;
- support/compliance blockers;
- infrastructure cost per active account.
