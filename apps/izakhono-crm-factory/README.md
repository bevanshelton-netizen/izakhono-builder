# IZAKHONO CRM FACTORY

Owned-first CRM builder for IZAKHONO internal businesses.

## Purpose
Build one reusable CRM engine and provision multiple business-specific workspaces without paying for a separate CRM platform for each company.

## Factory templates
- General Business
- Sales
- Education
- Manufacturing
- Services

## Roadmap
1. Workspace provisioning
2. Contacts / companies / leads
3. Pipeline and deal stages
4. Tasks and follow-ups
5. Quotes, orders and payments
6. AI sales assistant
7. WhatsApp and email connectors
8. Role-based access
9. Supabase persistence and authentication
10. Multi-tenant CRM provisioning
11. CRM template editor
12. IZAKHONO Provisioner integration

## Operating principle
Build once. Reuse everywhere. Keep business logic owned by IZAKHONO and make external infrastructure replaceable.
