## Regulatory / public-sector engagement

Potential stakeholders include the FSCA, SARB/Prudential Authority, public financial-literacy programmes and industry bodies. The initial approach must be capability/education focused and must **not** imply regulatory endorsement, accreditation or supervisory approval.

The FSCA regulates and supervises financial institutions and maintains regulated-entity information; SARB/PA supervises banks, insurers and financial market infrastructure.
