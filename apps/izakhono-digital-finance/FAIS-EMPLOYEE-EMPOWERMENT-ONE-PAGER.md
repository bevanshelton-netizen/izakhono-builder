# FAIS Digital Finance Employee Empowerment Package

## IZAKHONO Digital Finance Academy

**R1,000 per employee**

### The problem
Financial-services teams are operating in a rapidly changing environment shaped by digital payments, blockchain, tokenisation, digital assets, AI, cyber risk and evolving financial-crime controls. Institutions need workforce capability that connects these topics to real roles and responsibilities.

### The solution
IZAKHONO Digital Finance Academy provides a role-based digital-finance capability programme for financial-services employees.

Employees can be routed into relevant learning across:
- digital-money and blockchain foundations;
- blockchain architecture;
- smart contracts and programmable finance;
- digital assets, tokenisation and custody;
- payments, stablecoins and CBDCs;
- regulation, compliance and financial crime;
- cybersecurity, fraud and digital-asset risk;
- FinTech, AI and future-of-finance topics;
- specialist compliance, tokenisation and fraud/cyber labs;
- enterprise leadership.

### Built for the workforce
The programme can be configured for advisers/representatives, Key Individuals and leadership, compliance/AML, fraud/cyber, operations/contact centres, and payments/innovation teams.

### Automation
The operating target is 90–95% automation of routine administration: learner routing, progress, assessments, remediation and standard completion/reporting workflows after the protected enterprise adapters are connected and verified.

Human governance remains for regulatory-content approval, security incidents, appeals/exceptions and other decisions requiring accountable sign-off.

### Regulatory boundary
This package is **employee empowerment and professional development**. It is not an FSCA-endorsed product, university degree, national qualification, professional licence, financial/investment/legal/tax advice service, or replacement for applicable RE examinations, Class of Business or product-specific requirements.

### Commercial model
**R1,000 × number of employees** for the defined package scope, with institutional terms, data requirements, delivery mode and localisation agreed in the proposal.

### Recommended pilot
Start with one business unit or defined workforce cohort. Establish baseline capability, run the programme, review completion and learning outcomes, resolve governed exceptions, then expand institution-wide.

### Institution-ready delivery
Self-paced • cohort • live virtual • blended • executive briefing • train-the-trainer • private institutional academy
