# IZAKHONO Digital Finance Academy — FAIS Employee Empowerment Package

## Positioning

**Employee empowerment for financial-services organisations operating in a rapidly changing digital-finance environment.**

This package is designed for authorised FSPs, banks, insurers, brokerages, financial advisers, compliance teams, key individuals, representatives, operational teams and crypto-asset businesses.

It complements — but does not replace — statutory FAIS competence requirements.

## Why it matters in the FAIS environment

South Africa's FAIS framework requires FSPs, key individuals and representatives to maintain competence under the Fit and Proper requirements. Crypto assets are treated as financial products for FAIS purposes, meaning businesses that provide advice or intermediary services in relation to crypto assets fall within the FAIS regulatory perimeter. Crypto-asset FSPs, key individuals and representatives also have specific crypto-related competence and CPD conditions.

Even where an employee does not advise on crypto assets, banks, insurers and FSPs increasingly face customer questions and operational risks involving digital payments, scams, tokenisation, digital assets, cyber fraud and AI-enabled financial services.

The commercial proposition is therefore not "another compliance course". It is a **workforce capability layer** that helps employees understand the environment in which regulated financial services are increasingly delivered.

## Package structure

### 1. Leadership & Key Individual Briefing
Audience: Executives, KIs, compliance leadership, risk leadership and heads of distribution.

Covers:
- blockchain and digital assets without hype;
- emerging payment models;
- tokenisation and custody;
- regulatory and conduct-risk implications;
- fraud and cyber threats;
- governance questions;
- institutional readiness.

### 2. Representative & Adviser Empowerment
Audience: Representatives, advisers, sales and client-facing employees.

Covers:
- how digital money and crypto assets work;
- common customer questions;
- what employees should and should not say;
- scams, social engineering and fraud warning signs;
- custody and wallet basics;
- conduct-risk scenarios;
- referral and escalation rules.

### 3. Compliance, Risk & Financial-Crime Pathway
Audience: Compliance officers, AML/FIC teams, fraud teams, legal and risk.

Covers:
- crypto-asset regulatory perimeter;
- AML/CFT concepts;
- transaction and wallet risk;
- Travel Rule concepts;
- sanctions and screening;
- fraud typologies;
- governance and audit evidence.

### 4. Operations, Service & Contact-Centre Pathway
Audience: Customer service, operations, claims, back office and contact-centre teams.

Covers:
- digital-payment terminology;
- fraud and account-takeover scenarios;
- customer scam reports;
- safe escalation;
- privacy and identity protection;
- explaining digital-finance concepts in plain language.

### 5. Private Institutional Academy
For larger employers:
- role-based pathways;
- multilingual interface and learning packs;
- company policy overlays;
- cohort and completion reporting;
- private tenant option;
- executive dashboard;
- optional integration with FAISReady.

## Relationship with FAISReady

FAISReady remains the exam-readiness and regulatory-preparation product.

IZAKHONO Digital Finance Academy becomes the broader workforce-empowerment layer.

Together, an employer can support:

**Regulatory readiness + practical digital-finance capability + fraud/cyber awareness + leadership education.**

The two systems remain technically independent and may be bought separately.

## Compliance boundary

The package must be sold accurately:

- not a replacement for RE1, RE3, RE4 or RE5;
- not a recognised qualification unless separately approved;
- not Class of Business or product-specific training unless formally mapped and accepted as such;
- not marketed as approved CPD unless specific CPD approval/recognition is obtained;
- not financial or investment advice;
- no FSCA endorsement implied.
