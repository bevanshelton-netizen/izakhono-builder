**A multilingual digital-finance capability platform for institutions.**

## Language strategy

The platform separates four localisation layers:

1. **Interface language** — navigation, institutional positioning and platform controls.
2. **Learning language** — full lesson, assessment and tutor content.
3. **Jurisdiction overlay** — local laws, regulators, terminology and compliance notes.
4. **Institution overlay** — the customer's policies, products, processes and approved terminology.

A language must not be marketed as a fully translated course merely because its interface pack exists. Full learning packs require translation QA and subject-matter review.

Initial interface packs:
English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Kiswahili, isiZulu, Chinese, Hindi and German.

Future packs should be added according to signed institutional demand rather than by unsupported "all languages" claims.
