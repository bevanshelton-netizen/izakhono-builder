# IZAKHONO Digital Finance Academy — Institutional Sales Playbook

## Commercial objective
Convert institutional interest into a controlled pilot, then expand the pilot into an institution-wide capability programme.

**Core proposition:** Build digital-finance capability across leadership, product, technology, risk, compliance, operations and customer-facing teams — in the language and regulatory context the institution actually uses.

## Priority entry offer
### FAIS Digital Finance Employee Empowerment Package
**Price:** R1,000 per employee.

Designed for financial-services workforces that need practical digital-finance capability across:
- advisers and representatives;
- Key Individuals and leadership;
- compliance, AML and financial-crime teams;
- fraud, cyber and operational-risk teams;
- operations and contact-centre teams;
- payments, innovation and digital-product teams.

The package is **professional development and employee empowerment**. It does not claim FSCA endorsement, replace RE examinations, replace Class of Business or product-specific requirements, or constitute financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

## Pilot structure
1. **Scope** — institution selects employee population, roles, languages and jurisdiction.
2. **Baseline** — role-aware learning paths and baseline assessment.
3. **Learning** — self-paced or cohort delivery with practical digital-finance modules.
4. **Assessment** — knowledge checks, remediation and completion logic.
5. **Management reporting** — completion/progress reporting subject to the approved institutional data model.
6. **Review** — human governance reviews exceptions, regulatory-content changes and security matters.
7. **Expansion** — add departments, specialist labs, executive briefings, private academy or localisation.

## Operating model
The target is **90–95% automation of routine administration** once the protected identity, roster, entitlement, notification, certificate and reporting adapters are connected and verified.

Human governance remains mandatory for regulatory/legal content approval, security incidents, appeals and exceptions, institutional governance, and decisions requiring accountable sign-off.

## Buyer conversation
### CFO / CHRO / L&D
"Turn digital-finance capability into a measurable workforce programme without creating a new training operation from scratch."

### Compliance / Risk
"Put blockchain, digital assets, payments, fraud, cyber risk and financial-crime concepts into a controlled learning environment with jurisdiction-aware content governance."

### COO / Operations
"Create role-based learning paths and automate routine learner administration while keeping exceptions under accountable human control."

### CIO / Digital / Innovation
"Give technical and business teams a common digital-finance language spanning blockchain, tokenisation, programmable finance, payments and AI."

## Procurement pack
A first institutional proposal should contain:
- programme scope and employee count;
- roles/departments included;
- delivery mode;
- language and jurisdiction requirements;
- price and pilot term;
- data/privacy boundary;
- reporting requirements;
- security and deployment requirements;
- regulatory-content governance;
- acceptance criteria and expansion option.

## Conversion path
**Executive briefing → 30–90 day pilot → procurement approval → workforce rollout → specialist labs → private institutional academy.**
