# ALLEGRO VIBEZ

**THE MUSICIAN'S BUSINESS MANUAL + ARTIST PLATFORM**

A sovereign-first music business platform for African and global creators.

## Product pillars
- Artist registration and creator profiles
- Music upload and discovery
- Release moderation and rights metadata
- Royalty ledger, wallet and payout records
- Fan subscriptions and creator monetisation
- Career/business education based on *ALLEGRO-VIBEZ — THE MUSICIAN'S BUSINESS MANUAL*
- Messaging and creator/fan engagement
- Public discovery catalogue

## Sovereign architecture
The application is designed to run on IZAKHONO-owned infrastructure. External hosting/services are reversible fallbacks only.

## Revenue model
- Creator subscriptions
- Fan subscriptions
- Music/creator transactions
- Royalty administration
- Education/manual sales
- Platform services

## Build acceptance
A release is not called LIVE until public reachability, registration, upload/discovery, payment, royalty/wallet recording, onboarding and transaction evidence have all passed.
